## Meaning

Is difficult to do

He's difficult to understand, isn't he?

ja: 出来るごとが難しいです。

Meaning credits: tigert

## Formation

- Verb ます-stem + にくい

### Comment 6 — Groove

The different is that 難い is added to a verb stem to change it to an adjective, whereas 難しい is an existing adjective meaning 'difficult', and thus can't be conjugated to a verb stem.<br/><br/>For example:<br/><br/>That word is difficult to remember (lit. That word's remembering is difficult.)<br/>その言葉が思い出し難いな。<br/><br/>as compared to:<br/><br/>Remembering that word is difficult (lit. That word's remembering is difficult).<br/>その言葉は思い出すことが難しいだ。<br/><br/>EDIT 10/15/07: You're right; thanks tiger!
