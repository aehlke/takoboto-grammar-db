# くらい

## Meaning

about, as

(about) as normal as any other guy on the street.

Meaning credits: dc

## Formation

- Noun + くらい/ぐらい

### Comment 1 — dc

kurai <br/><br/>literally means how long or much, but often used in conversation for adding an "about" feeling to soften a phrase.<br/><br/>sometimes pronounced gurai depending on the phrase before; for example if the sentence before ends with a vowel.<br/><br/>3万ドルぐらい = about 30,000 dollars

### Comment 5 — Miki

Reply to dc's 質問. I don't think there is any rule for Gurai and Kurai. Even I sometimes think which of them is more often used for a prior word. We often use both like 10分くらい、10分ぐらい、あいさつくらい、あいさつぐらい、どれくらい、どれぐらい。You can see them by Google search.

### Comment 6 — your name

KURAI here implies 程度. the rough standard, the degree, or the range of something. So, ex#609 implies somebody is expected at least a manner something like a hello. ex#3117, somebody is expected to do at least something like what a child can do.

### Comment 13 — mel

I have been taught to use it this way:<br/>"ｘはｙくらいです", meaning "Y is about the only X". This can only be used when Y is about the only case where X holds true. X and Y are both either nouns or noun phrases.<br/>E.g. 東京が静かになるのは，お盆のころくらいです。
