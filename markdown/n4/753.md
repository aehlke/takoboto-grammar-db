## Meaning

'topic marker' (also shown as 'wa')

X wa Y desu : X is Y

Meaning credits: Amatuka

### Comment 1 — Denzil

Introducing は "wa" and です。"desu."<br/>the particle は marks the topic.<br/>(Remember particles attach to the word which they follow, and that when は is a particle it is pronounced wa.)<br/>です。functions like "is" or "topic is."

### Comment 2 — Rinji\_HalfElf

<b>は</b><br/><br/>Is pronounced as "wah".<br/>は a subject or topic marker.<br/>は can appear as ha, or wa in romaji depending on the system used.<br/><br/>The kana used for は is usually pronounced as "hah", but pronounced as "wah" when used as a particle.<br/>The same phenomenon happens in the word "dewa/deha" では in the negative conjugation of the verb [desu]. <br/><br/><b>Other notes:</b><br/>In yakuza speech,<br/>は、becomes や　(yah).<br/>Typically, the y sound is softened such that it sounds like "ah"<br/>Example:<br/>Ore ha --turns to-&gt; Ore ya --but sounds like-&gt; Ore a

### Comment 4 — Amatuka

When to use は vs が?<br/>Both sentences like 雨は降っている。[ame ha <br/>futteiru] and 雨が降っている。[ame ga futteiru] are <br/>valid, so working out when to use which form is a <br/>tricky point.<br/><br/>First: In answering a question such as <br/>Q. 机の上に何がありますか。[tsukue no ue ni nani ga arimasu ka]<br/>A. 鉛筆があります。[enpitsu ga arimasu].<br/>Here が is used.

### Comment 5 — Amatuka

On the other hand if attention has been placed on something in advance and the question is asked with that object as the topic...<br/>Q. 本はどこにありますか。[hon ha doko ni arimasu ka.]<br/>A. 本はイスの下にあります。[hon ha isu no shita ni arimasu]<br/>then は is used.

### Comment 6 — Amatuka

が is also used to indicate a specific choice.<br/>私が行きます。[watashi ga ikimasu]<br/>_I'll_ go. (not any of the other possibilities).

### Comment 7 — Amatuka

は is used for contrastative sentences (see ha-2).<br/>For example normally you'd say <br/>犬が好きです。[inu ga suki desu] I like dogs.<br/>However if you want to contrast how you feel about dogs with how you feel about other animals then<br/>犬は好きだが、猫はどうも・・・[inu ha suki da ga, neko ha doumo ...]<br/>I like _dogs_ but I really don't (get on with) cats.

### Comment 8 — Amatuka

は [ha / wa] follows a noun or a noun phrase.<br/>(e.g. 犬は [inu ha] 'as for dogs'<br/>知るのは [shiru no ha] 'as for what (I) know'<br/>The latter case の nominalizes (turns into a noun) 知る)<br/><br/>N = noun
