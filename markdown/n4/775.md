## Meaning

? (basic question particle)

Is this your book?

Meaning credits: Amatuka

## Formation

- Sentence + か

## Examples

### Example 621

これはあなたの本です**か。**

これはあなたのほんですか。

en: Is this your book **?**

Credits: Amatuka

### Example 4829

家に帰りましょう**か。**

いえにかえりましょうか。

en: Why don't we go home **?**

Credits: Miki

### Example 4830

ひとつ質問をしてもいいです**か。**

ひとつしつもんをしてもいいですか。

en: May I ask you a question **?**

Credits: Miki

### Example 8628

今日は鶏肉を買いました**か**。

きょうはとりにくをかいましたか。

en: Did you buy chicken today **?**

Credits: Sakuratree

### Example 8811

関西弁が分かります**か**

かんさいべんがわかりますか

en: Do you understand Kansai dialect **?**

Credits: 貴博

## Discussion

### Comment 1 — Sakuratree

か has many uses but it can be used as a question mark, just like what we have in English:<br/><br/>Did he eat dinner?<br/>彼は夕方ご飯を食べましたか。<br/><br/>Did he go to Japan last year?<br/>来年彼は日本に行きましたか。

### Comment 2 — Amatuka

N.B. Commonly [name-san] would be used in place of [anata]

### Comment 3 — Amatuka

Placed on the end of a sentence. <br/><br/>S = sentence

I see question mark "?" after particle ka in several japanese writing on internet.
