# これ(此・是・之・惟)、それ(其), あれ(彼)

Source: [Takoboto #580](https://takoboto.jp/bunpo/580/)  
Label: kore, sore, are  
JLPT: N5  
Credits: Amatuka

Other forms: これ, それ, あれ

## Meaning

this, that, the other

this is mine, that is yours and the other is anybody's guess.

Meaning credits: Amatuka

## Discussion

### Comment 1 — blutorange

The kanji are just given for reference. Kana spelling is normal today. <br/>These words are part of what is called kosoado. The ko, so and a indicate the relation to the speaker, the re turns them into the pronouns for a certain object.<br/>The most important thing to remember is that you cannot these words to say, for example, "I want this apple" = "kore ringo ga hoshii." That is wrong. You can only use them for "I want this."<br/>("this apple" would be "kono ringo")

### Comment 2 — Amatuka

kore : near to speaker<br/>sore : near to spoken to<br/>are : far from both speaker and spoken to
