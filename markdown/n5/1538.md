## Meaning

netai: I want to sleep

Meaning credits: Parul

## Discussion

### Comment 1 — eriustobin

It is considered impolite to ask someone else if the "want" to do something. Instead, use the negative form of the verb in a question.

### Comment 2 — kuro\_hannah

Very important: "tai" can only be used for the I person, like "i want". <br/>If you want to say that someone else wants, you use "tagaru". <br/>Ex:かれは牛乳を飲みたがっています。<br/>This is because Japanese never imply they know what someone else wishes, and "garu" means: "i think that", "i heard that", etc.
