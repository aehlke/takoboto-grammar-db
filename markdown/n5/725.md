## Meaning

but, however, still

I know him but we've never met.

Meaning credits: Amatuka, Raza

## Formation

- Sentence 1 + が + Sentence 2

## See also

- [けれども](../n4/724.md)
- [でも](../n4/726.md)

## Examples

### Example 865

突然です**が**ボードゲーム告知をてっきり忘れてました。

とつぜんですがボードゲームこくちをてっきりわすれてました。

en: This is out of nowhere \[a sudden change of subject\] **but** I completely forgot about the BoardGame notice.

Credits: Amatuka

### Example 866

悪いです**が**ちょっと静かにしてください。

わるいですがちょっとしずかにしてください。

en: Sorry \[Bad of me\] **but** could you keep it a little quieter?

Credits: Amatuka

as far as i know, ga is more like a "yet" type of but. demo means something closer to "however". And keredemo is closer to "nevertheless".

### Comment 6 — nellyaudrey

oh, i should add that ga typically contrasts between two differing sentences. Like that one above, <br/>毎日一生懸命に英語を勉強したが、あまり身につかなかった。 <br/>I studied English very hard every day, but I did not learn a lot. <br/><br/>a demo sentence would be<br/>この本はすてきでも買えないだからすぎる高いですよ．<br/>a keredemo sentece implies that you've done something despite something else. remember, this GA is not the logic particle GA. THIS ONE comes after a VERB, the logic particle GA comes ONLY after a NAME.
