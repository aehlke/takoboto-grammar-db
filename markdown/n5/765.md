## Meaning

'object marker' (also shown as 'o') 目的

That \[←marked by object marker\] please.

Meaning credits: Amatuka

## Formation

- Noun + を + Verb

## Discussion

### Comment 1 — sarruby

を follows a noun or place. It indicates that that noun is the direct object of the verb, that the place is the place of departure, or that the place was passed through.<br/><br/>を is romanized as "wo" or "o".<br/><br/>Amatuka said this below.

### Comment 2 — Amatuka

The three uses of を [wo] are <br/>1. objective / target (目的) <br/>2. area passed through (通過点) <br/>3. departure point (出発点)

### Comment 3 — Amatuka

Note : The object marker を is said 'o' but may be written as either 'wo' or 'o' depending on romaji system used.

### Comment 4 — Amatuka

を is used to mark a noun or noun phrase.<br/><br/>N = noun.
