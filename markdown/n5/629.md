## Discussion

### Comment 1 — izumichan31

Use nan before b, p, d, t, n, r, and z. Use nani before all other letters.

### Comment 2 — Rinji\_HalfElf

<b>Nan and Nani</b><br/><br/>Generally nan, or nani is used for which ever flows better.<br/>However, Nani is used when wanting to be more polite and formal.<br/>Other situations may require getting a feel for using nan versus nani, or memorization.<br/><br/>In general, if the following word begins with a syllable from the t, n, and d groups. Nan is used.<br/><br/>Common usages:<br/>Nan is used before particles [no]/の, [de]/で and verb da/desu.<br/><br/>Nani is used Before [ka]/か and [ni]/に,<br/><br/>If nan was used before ka/か, it would sound like the word nanka, which means "things like" or "something like that" which is also considered rude.<br/>Nanika is instead used. Both nanka and nanika mean "something", but nanika is polite.<br/><br/>If nan was used in the case of ni/に, it would produce nanni なんに. This　sounds similar to nannimo なんにも, which means "nothing at all". <br/>Generally mo/も、to indicate nothing, or absence. This could confuse a listener, waiting for a [mo] to be uttered.

### Comment 3 — Amatuka

Note that 何 will be either なに or なん depending on where it is used.
