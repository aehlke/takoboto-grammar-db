## Meaning

how much

How much is a postcard?

Meaning credits: Amatuka

Just a note to say that いくらか is a completely diferent word meaning "a relatively small amount." <br/><br/>いくらか得したの = Did you make some gain?<br/>いくら得したの = HOw much gain did you make?
