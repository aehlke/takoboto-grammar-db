## Meaning

1) just, only, simply, merely; 2) as much as X wants/like/can (whatever, anything)

1) don't just love her; 2) eat as much as you want (eat whatever/anything you'd like)

Meaning credits: dc

## Discussion

### Comment 1 — ziaz

There are several different uses of dake and different meanings as well. <br/>1) only;just;simply;merely<br/>2) as ... as possible; whatever; anything<br/>出来るだけ早く＝as quickly as possible

### Comment 2 — rubyhatchet

As for the second use of だけ (〜 as much/long/many as you/I/etc (verb)), note that you only use verbs for this grammar, just as you do in English:<br/><br/>Ｖplain form + だけ<br/>Ｖpotential form + だけ<br/><br/>As for other miscellaneous forms that do not use verbs, the most commonly used ones include:<br/>〜(し)たいだけ<br/>ほしいだけ<br/>好きなだけ
