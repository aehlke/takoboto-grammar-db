# とは限らない

Source: [Takoboto #516](https://takoboto.jp/bunpo/516/)  
Label: tohakagiranai  
JLPT: N3  
Credits: dc  
Retrieved: 2026-10-03T05:35:50+00:00  
License: [CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/)

Other forms: とはかぎらない

## Meaning

not always, not necessarily

expensive things are not always better

Meaning credits: dc

## See also

- [必ずしも・・・でない](../n2/618.md)
- [に限らず](../n2/380.md)

## Examples

### Example 813

高い物が**必ずしも**安いものより良い**とは限らない。**

たかいものがかならずしもやすいものよりよいとはかぎらない。

en: Expensive things **are not always** better than cheap things.

Credits: Amatuka, dc

### Example 3834

いつでも自分の思い通りにできる**とは限らない**よ。

いつでもじぶんのおもいどおりにできるとはかぎらないよ。

en: You cannot always have your own way.

Credits: dbx

### Example 3835

うまい冗談を言うことはいつもたやすい**とは限らない**。

en: It is not always easy to make a good joke.

Credits: dbx

en: Delicious looking food **doesn't necessarily** taste good.

Credits: dbx

### Example 3838

en: The rich are not always happy.

en: Even if you have your own way, you will not always be successful.

### Comment 1 — dc

seems to be used in a couple of ways:<br/><br/>with [kanarazushimo]<br/>A ga kanarazushimo B yori (adjective) tohakagiranai<br/>高い物が[必ずしも]安いものより良いとは限らない。<br/>Expensive things are not always better than cheap things.<br/><br/>お金持ちが必ずしも幸せとは限らない。<br/><br/>also with a verb<br/><br/>お金が幸福を<b>もたらす</b>とは限らない。<br/>Money does not always bring happiness. <br/><br/> いつでも自分の思い通りに<b>できる</b>とは限らないよ。 <br/>You cannot always have your own way.
