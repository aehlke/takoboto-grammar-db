# と言えば

Source: [Takoboto #1712](https://takoboto.jp/bunpo/1712/)  
Label: toieba  
JLPT: N3  
Credits: Dom  
Retrieved: 2026-10-03T05:33:21+00:00  
License: [CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/)

Other forms: といえば

## Meaning

speaking of ~

Speaking of rain, what is the weather like tomorrow?

Meaning credits: Dom

## Formation

- Noun + と言えば

## Examples

### Example 7291

春**と言えば**、卒業の季節。

はるといえば、そつぎょうのきせつ。

en: **Speaking of spring,** it is the season for graduation.
