## Meaning

just (just recently) / nothing but

I just bought it / you do nothing but play around

Meaning credits: dc

## Formation

- Verb casual past た-form + ばかり
- Verb て-form + ばかり
- Verb dictionary form + ばかり
- Noun + ばかり

### Comment 3 — drdave

I second KWhazit's comment: there are most definitely two separate entries here. <br/>Also need to beware: depending on meaning ("just happened"/"nothing but"), placement is different:<br/><br/>食べたばかり -&gt; I just ate (moments ago).<br/>ばかり食べた -&gt; All I/we/etc did was eating (and nothing else).<br/><br/>Furthermore, I believe pronunciation/spelling: ばっかり is also valid (very often heard in speech).
