## Meaning

without

i went without eating

Meaning credits: Meghana

## Formation

- Verb ~ない form without ない + ずに

## See also

- [事無く](../n2/247.md)
- [ないで](../n4/1619.md)

## Examples

### Example 5921

朝ごはんを食べ**ずに**学校へ来ました。

あさごはんをたべずにがっこうへきました。

en: I came to school without eating breakfast.

Credits: Meghana

### Example 6705

勉強**せずに**テストを受けた。

べんきょうせずにテストをうけた。

en: I took the test **without** having studied.

Credits: Sion

### Comment 2 — maki

formation: take the negative root (ない形）and replace the final ~ない　with ~ず<br/><br/>例）<br/><br/>　　書く　-&gt; 書か「ない」 -&gt; 書かず（に）<br/>　　見る　-&gt;　 見「ない」 -&gt; 見ず<br/>　　来る　-&gt; 来ず　（読み方：こず）<br/>　　する　-&gt; せず　　<br/><br/>Compared to the "naide" form, this one is used with a more negative connection to the thing you didn't do (as you would regret that you didn't do something). <br/><br/>例）<br/><br/>昨日学校へ行かず、ゲームセンターで遊んでしまった。

### Comment 13 — RBY

To nellyaudrey<br/>You can' t do a negative of a negative form in Japanese. You'll only be mishunderstood. The Japanese are more simple minded. The negative of a negative is positive, so say it positive! 朝ご飯食べて学校に来ました。I came to school having eaten breakfast.<br/>Anyway, if you want to say your negative-negative sentence at any cost the closer sentence you can use is a negation of the all positive sentence. 朝ご飯食べずに学校に来ましたと違います.It's not true that I came to school without eating breakfast.
