## Meaning

even; only; just

I can't even see it

Meaning credits: dc

## Formation

- Verb ます-stem + さえ + (する/しない)
- Verb て-form + さえ
- Verb dictionary form + こと + さえ
- Noun + さえ
- Noun + で + さえ
