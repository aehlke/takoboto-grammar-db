# て形+みる

Source: [Takoboto #1559](https://takoboto.jp/bunpo/1559/)

JLPT: N3  
Credits: jayupuro

Other forms: てみる

## Meaning

to try (out)

try to read

Meaning credits: jayupuro

## Examples

### Example 5904

着て**みて**もいい？

きてみてもいい？

en: Is it ok if i try it on **to see?**

Credits: karekora

### Example 5907

あなたは自分で行っ**て見る**べきです。

あなたはじぶんでおこなってみるべきです。

en: You should go and see for yourself.

Credits: dc

### Example 5908

老いた故郷の両親のことを考え**て見る**べきだ。

おいたふるさとのりょうしんのことをかんがえてみるべきだ。

en: You must think of your old parents at home.

Credits: dc

### Example 5909

やって見ようか？

やってみようか？

en: Shall we have a go?

Credits: dc

### Comment 1 — dc

見る (miru) as a verb means to see, but used with another verb it means "try to do something".<br/><br/>theres a similar phrase in english: "try it and see"

### Comment 2 — dc

used with the -te form of the previous verb<br/>書いてみる　- try and write it<br/>食べてみる - try and eat it (taste a bit of it to see what its like)

### Comment 3 — dc

miru itself can be converted to a -te form to keep the sentence flowing... like <br/><br/>食べてみたけど、、、<br/>I tried to eat it, but...<br/>(-ta is past of -te)

### Comment 6 — yookoso

Success and Failure: <br/>According to the book Basic Connections by Kakuko Shoji, 〜てみた indicates that the subject actually tried and achieved the result. When the speaker tried or intended to do something but failed, 〜うとした (volitional verb form + toshita) is used.<br/><br/>example: 一所懸命、納豆を食べようとしたのですが、やっぱりだめでした。<br/>(Isshoukenmei, nattou o tabeyou to shita no desu ga, yappari dame deshita)
