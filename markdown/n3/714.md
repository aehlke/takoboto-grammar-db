## Meaning

? (for casual questions)

Who's it?

Meaning credits: Amatuka

## Formation

- Adj Na/Noun + だい
- Verb/ adj i + ん + だい
- Adj Na/Noun + な + ん + だい
- Adj Na/Noun + だった + ん + だい

### Comment 1 — american57

The equivalent of a question mark. Used mostly in informal male speech. Ungrammatical for questions that can be answered "yes/no" (see also "kai"). Unsuitable for asking a superior a question.<br/><br/>There is a second "dai" at the end of sentences. It is equivalent to a weak exclamation point and is heard in the speech of little boys. "Sore boku no dai." That's mine!

### Comment 2 — Amatuka

Typical 'standard politeness' question ends in <br/>ですか or Vmますか。(e.g. いますか。Are (you) in?)<br/>Casual questions can omit the ? particle and use <br/>tone. (e.g. 誰だ？ Who is it?)<br/>Alternatively だい and かい can be used.<br/>The plain form with か is quite abrupt and rough.
