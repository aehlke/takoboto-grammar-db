# 気がする

Source: [Takoboto #1117](https://takoboto.jp/bunpo/1117/)  
Label: kigasuru  
JLPT: N3  
Credits: dc  
Retrieved: 2026-10-03T05:35:46+00:00  
License: [CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/)

Other forms: きがする

## Meaning

to feel, realize

I have a feeling he's a bad person

Meaning credits: dc

## See also

- [気が付いた](../n1/382.md)

## Examples

### Example 1327

電車の中での勉強はとても無理なような**気がします**。

でんしゃのなかでのべんきょうはとてもむりなようなきがします。

en: **I feel like it's** almost impossible to study on the train .

Credits: dc, bamboo4
