## Meaning

I wonder.. (Based on か question particle.)

I wonder if he'll be here soon?

Meaning credits: Amatuka

## See also

- [〜っけ](../n2/1276.md)

## Examples

### Example 5276

この靴は高い**かな**

このくつはたかいかな

en: I wonder if these shoes are expensive

Credits: alarma

### Example 5284

締め切りに間に合わなかったらどうなる**かな。**

しめきりにまにあわなかったらどうなるかな。

en: **What if** I miss the deadline?

Credits: Miki

### Example 5285

「彼の病気は重いの**かな。」**「そうでないといいね。」

「かれのびょうきはおもいのかな。」「そうでないといいね。」

en: "Is he seriously ill **?"** "I hope not."

Credits: Miki

### Example 5286

「肘が痛むんだよ。病院に行こう**かな。」**

「ひじがいたむんだよ。びょういんにいこうかな。」

en: "My elbow really hurts. **I guess** I should go to a hospital."

Credits: Miki

### Example 5287

７月の上旬に、海に行くのは早い**かな。**

しちがつのじょうじゅんに、うみにいくのははやいかな。

en: **I wonder** if the first week (or so) of July is too early to go to the beach.

Credits: Miki

## Discussion

### Comment 1 — kkat

かな is used by males, it sounds weird if females use it. Thus females use かしら。

### Comment 2 — Amatuka

Three main uses of かな.<br/>1. A question with little doubt.<br/>e.g. ジムならうまくいくかな。As it's Jim I wonder whether it will go well.<br/>2. Talking to yourself.<br/>ゲーセンに行こうかな。Shall I go to the game center?<br/>3. Expressing wish / hope.<br/>誰かこないかな。Is anybody going to come? (I hope somebody comes.)

### Comment 3 — Amatuka

Placed on the end of a sentence.<br/><br/>S = Sentence.

### Comment 4 — bamboo4

かな is made up of か, a particle for interrogatory and な a particle to express one's emotion. It is used to ask oneself a question or a question to another as to the degree of uncertainty involved. When you use it in the form of ないかな, you are expressing a desire for something to come true.<br/><br/>間違えたかな = Did I screw it up?<br/>天気にならないかな = Hope the weather clears up!
