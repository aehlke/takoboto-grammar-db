## Meaning

(often it'd be better to do \_\_) Such-and-such an option is something

You'd better study then.

Meaning credits: Amatuka

## Examples

### Example 908

３月１日の**方が**都合がよいのですが。

さんがつ１にちのほうがつごうがよいのですが。

en: We feel that March 1 **would be more** convenient.

Credits: Amatuka

### Example 910

９時以降に電話した**方が**安いですか。

９じいこうにでんわしたほうがやすいですか。

en: Is it cheaper to call after 9:00?

Credits: Amatuka

### Example 911

物事を生半可に知っているくらいなら全然知らない**方が**ましだ。

ものごとをなまはんかにしっているくらいならぜんぜんしらないほうがましだ。

en: I would rather not know a thing at all than know it imperfectly.

Credits: Amatuka

### Example 912

遅くても着実な**方が**競争に勝つ。

おそくてもちゃくじつなほうがきょうそうにかつ。

en: Slow but sure wins the race.

Credits: Amatuka

### Example 913

私は肉**より**魚の**方が**いい。

わたしはにくよりさかなのほうがいい。

en: I prefer fish to meat.

Credits: Amatuka

### Example 6994

無理をしない**方が**いいですよ。

en: You'd better not push yourself too much.

### Example 8055

## Discussion

### Comment 1 — Roxsan

Format: ta-form of verb or nonpast, negative form of verb + ほうがいい（です）

### Comment 2 — Amatuka

Two forms, <br/>"[noun] no hou ga ___" (e.g. ３月１日の 方が 都合がよい)<br/>"[verb] no hou ga ___" (e.g. 電話した方が安い)<br/>If the verb is positive it is usually past tense, even if you are talking about actions to be taken in the future.<br/>If the verb is negative it does not need to be put into past tense.

### Comment 3 — Amatuka

Several forms, <br/>"[noun] no hou ga ___" (e.g. ３月１日の 方が 都合がよい)<br/>"[na-adjective] na hou ga ___" (e.g. 着実な 方が 競争に勝つ)<br/>"[verb] no hou ga ___" (e.g. 電話した方が安い)<br/>If the verb is positive it is usually past tense, even if you are talking about actions to be taken in the future.<br/>If the verb is negative it does not need to be put into past tense.
