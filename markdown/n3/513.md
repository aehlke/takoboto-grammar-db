# と共に

Other forms: とともに

## Meaning

as well as; with; along with; at the same time; when; as; while

As I'm recovering from my illness, my appetite is coming back.

Meaning credits: dc

## Discussion

### Comment 1 — Viola

There are several usages:<br/><br/>A<br/>meaning: with<br/>form: noun + totomoni<br/>eg. Oshyougatsu ha kazoku totomoni sugoshitai.<br/> I want to spend New Year (party) with my family.<br/><br/>B<br/>meaning: at the same time<br/>form: verb (dict. form) + totomoni<br/> i adj. + totomoni<br/> noun + de aru + totomoni<br/> na adj. + de aru + totomoni<br/>eg.Kobayashisan ha gakusei wo shidou suru totomoni, kenkyuu nimo chikara wo ireteiru.<br/> Mr. Kobayashi coaches the students and puts his strenght in making some researches at the same time.<br/><br/> Kono seihin no kaihatsu ha, konnan de aru totomoni, hiyou ga kakaru.<br/> Regarding the development of this product, it is difficult and needs money at the same time.<br/><br/>C<br/>meaning: is used when a change leads to another one.<br/>form: verb (dict. form) + totomoni<br/> noun + totomoni <br/>eg. Toshi wo toru totomoni, tairyoku ga otoroeru.<br/> As you become older, your physical strength becomes weaker.

### Comment 2 — rubyhatchet

＊The meaning of とともに changes depending on the sentence pattern and context.<br/><br/>1) 「ＡはＢとともにＣ」: when A and B share the common property C, とともに means "as well as." (See example #6291.)<br/><br/>2) 「ＡはＢとともにＣ」: when A and B are the actors of the action C, とともに means "with; along with." (See example #6292.)<br/><br/>3) When とともに is preceded by a noun representing an action or event, とともに means either "at the same time as; when" or "as; with" depending on the event or action represented by the noun. (See example #6293.)<br/><br/>4) When とともに is preceded by a verb, とともに means "as; while." (See example #6294.)<br/><br/>5) When とともに is preceded by Ｎ・なＡ(stem) + である, とともに means "as well as." (See example #6295.)<br/><br/>FORMATION:<br/>Ｖ(dict. form) + とともに<br/>Ｎ・なＡ(stem) + である + とともに<br/>いＡ + とともに<br/>Ｎ + とともに

### Comment 3 — dc

when a verb comes before it, とともに means "as" - As I am recovering...

### Comment 4 — dc

when a noun comes before, とともに means "as well as" -<br/>As well as Tokyo...

### Comment 5 — Miki

A noun case is OK.<br/>と共に of 病気が回復すると共に can be replaced by ni turete.<br/>病気が回復するにつれて、食欲も出てきた。<br/>風が吹く とともに/につれて、雲が出てきた。<br/>As it blows, clouds begin to appear.
