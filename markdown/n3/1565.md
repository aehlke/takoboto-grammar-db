## Meaning

Try to do something, but the nuance is that it was unsuccessful.

I tried to kiss her, but she said no.

ja: 試してみるだけで結果がともなわないニュアンスがある。

Meaning credits: ninja\_k, Jacob

## Formation

- よう形 + とする

## Examples
