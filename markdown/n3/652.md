## Meaning

Adds emphasis - sentence ending particle; Emphasizes personal desires

Won't it be great if we can go fishing tomorrow?

ja: 自分のしたいことや欲しいものなど

Meaning credits: Amatuka

- [〜な](../n3/705.md)
