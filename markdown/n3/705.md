## Meaning

Vru, (negative command - sentence ending particle)

Don't do that\!

Meaning credits: Amatuka

## Formation

- Verb dictionary form + な

## Discussion

### Comment 1 — yutenji

Group 1: Change the last sound of the ます-form into the え sound. E.g. やすみ「ます」-&gt;　やすめ<br/>Group 2: Append ろ to the ます-form. E.g. ねろ / 寝ろ<br/>Group 3: します-&gt;しろ きます-&gt;こい<br/><br/>Quite 'rough' speech, normally only used by men or those in senior positions.

### Comment 2 — FekketCantenel

This construct expresses a rough negative command ("Don't you dare do X!"). It takes the [dictionary form] of the verb and appends 〜な to it.

### Comment 3 — Amatuka

Formed from dictionary form verb + na<br/><br/>Vru = 'Dictionary' form verb (e.g. 歩く、する、いる)

### Comment 4 — hassan

ふざけるな！　(they often say it in anime etc. means<br/>something like... how to say it... 'stop that!',<br/>'don't make me a fool')

### Comment 5 — anon

ふせげるな？

### Comment 6 — Miki

We don't say ふせげるな! but we say ふせぐな！ 防ぐ+な<br/>ふざける is 'Dictionary' form verb and not potential form. ふざけるな can be also said as ふざけるんじゃない

### Comment 7 — 誠

ふざけるな　or ふざけないで meaning : " don't/ stop messing around and behave yourself"<br/>ふざける = basic meaning is " to play around", "to mess about" but can also mean " to talk nonsense".
