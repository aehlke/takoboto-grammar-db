## Discussion

### Comment 1 — Stefanie

No da ist used for formal and written expressions. <br/>It is used to give a sentence a tone of explanation. Or of asking for an explanation, if it is followed by "ka". <br/>The "no" makes the whole preceding part of the sentence into a single noun-like entity.<br/><br/>"no" is often abbreviated to "n" in the common speech.
