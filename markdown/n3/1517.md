# 〜中に、〜上に、〜下に、など

Source: [Takoboto #1517](https://takoboto.jp/bunpo/1517/)  
Label: naka, ue, shita, etc.  
JLPT: N3  
Credits: Xephyer  
Retrieved: 2026-10-03T05:34:00+00:00

Other forms: 〜なかに、〜うえに、〜したに、など

## Meaning

Talking about relative location of an object

My hat is in your car

ja: ある物について立地を説明するために

Meaning credits: Xephyer

## Discussion

### Comment 1 — tigert

Location is specified with the combination of a location noun and/or relative place word (上、下、中、外、後ろ、前、横、そば）and the location marker に､and one of the two existance verbs ある or いる<br/><br/>ある　is used when talking about inanimate objects or states of being. while いる　is used for animate objects.<br/><br/>My dog is in your car<br/>わたしの犬はあなたの車にいます｡<br/>My hat is in your car<br/>わたしのぼうしはあなたの車にあります｡<br/><br/>If you want to specify a relative location IE in something, next to something, on something. you combine the　location noun with the relative location word using the posessive particle　の<br/><br/>On the desk<br/>つくえの上<br/>In the bag<br/>かばんの中<br/>by the tv<br/>テレビの横<br/>behind the car<br/>車の後ろ<br/>infront of the computer<br/>パャRンの前<br/><br/>becareful to include the location particle に at the end of the relative location statement.<br/>で can also be used for location, but not in combination with the いる and ある verbs.

### Comment 2 — bsloh

の中に is to indicate object location, for example スパートの中に本屋があります。(Bookshop is in the supermarket.)<br/><br/>の中で is exlpain as "among the category", for example 果物の中で、どちらが一番すきですか。(Among the fruits,what is your favourite?)

According to my Japanese friend they never use "の中に" when you are picking items out of a list (like picking your favorite).
