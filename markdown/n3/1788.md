## Meaning

by doing this　(meant to highlight a method or means)

By listening to Japanese music, you will become better.

Meaning credits: miamimo8

## Formation

- Verb dictionary form + ことで
- い adjective + ことで
- な adjective な + ことで
- Noun + の + ことで
