(ate it to the end) . (She ate all knowing what she was doing.)<br/>ダイエット中のくせに彼女はケーキを食べてしまった。<br/>(I don't know she ate cake all or not, anyway) although she is on diet, she ate a cake (as she could not stop herself.)<br/><br/>夏休みが前に、宿題をやりきろう。<br/>Let's finish all the homework before summer vacation starts.<br/>夏休みが前に、宿題をできるだけやってしまおう。<br/>Let's finish homework as much as we can before summer vacation.
