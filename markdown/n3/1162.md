# ところに、ところへ、ところを

## Meaning

(Just) when

Just when I had decided to call her, she phoned me.

Meaning credits: newt

## Formation

- Verb（ている形） + ところ
- Verb（た形） + ところ
- Noun + の + ところ
- い-adjective + ところ

### Comment 2 — claytonian

Generally speaking, the final particle in this grammar is interchangable with others, giving different nuance.<br/>[ところへ] emphasizes the process of getting to the given situation described by ところ such as XXXのところへ行った、<br/>[ところに] emphasizes the fact that you were at that given situation such as XXXのところに出くわした.<br/>[ところを] takes the situation described by ところ and provide the bridge to whatever was done to that siatuation, such as XXXのところを見られた
