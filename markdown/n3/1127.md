# て形+くる (て来る)

Source: [Takoboto #1127](https://takoboto.jp/bunpo/1127/)  
Label: tekuru  
JLPT: N3  
Credits: bi-ru  
Retrieved: 2026-10-03T05:34:57+00:00  
License: [CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/)

Other forms: てくる

## Meaning

Do something, and return

I'll go buy the newspaper (and come back)

Meaning credits: bi-ru

## Formation

- Verb て-form + 来る

## Examples

### Example 3134

新聞を買って**くる**。

しんぶんをかってくる。

en: I'll go buy the newspaper. **(implying you'll be back)**

Credits: bi-ru

### Example 3136

医者に行って薬をもらって**きました**。

いしゃにいってくすりをもらってきました。

en: I went to the doctor and got some medicine **(and came back with it)** .

Credits: bi-ru

### Example 4963

日本語が少しずつ分かって**きた**

にほんごがすこしずつわかってきた

en: Little by little, I **came to** understand Japanese.

Credits: bi-ru

### Example 4964

物価が下がって**くる**。

ぶっかがさがってくる。

en: Prices **continue to** fall.

Credits: bi-ru

## Discussion

### Comment 1 — bi-ru

As I found on (Takasugi Shinji's site) - contrast the difference of -tekuru and -teiku in the following:<br/><br/>物価が下がっていく。<br/>物価が下がってくる。<br/><br/>Both mean that prices continue to fall. Both tekuru and teiku indicate continuation in this example. However teiku implies motion away from, whereas tekuru implies motion towards.<br/><br/>You think prices are going away from you in sentence 1, such as when you don't like the trend. You think prices are approaching you in sentence 2, such as when you like it.
