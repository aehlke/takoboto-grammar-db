# 儚い

Source: [Takoboto #886](https://takoboto.jp/bunpo/886/)  
Label: hakanai  
JLPT: N3  
Credits: bamboo4  
Retrieved: 2026-10-03T05:36:11+00:00  
License: [CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/)

Other forms: はかない

## Meaning

hopeless, fleeting, transient, fickle

Unrequited (hopeless) love

Meaning credits: bamboo4
