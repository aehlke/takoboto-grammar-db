# 訳ではない

Source: [Takoboto #544](https://takoboto.jp/bunpo/544/)  
Label: wakedehanai  
JLPT: N3  
Credits: dc  
Retrieved: 2026-10-03T05:33:06+00:00

Other forms: わけではない

## Meaning

It does not (necessarily) mean that 〜; I don't mean that 〜; It is not (true) that 〜; It is not the case that 〜

It doesn't necessarily mean he understood.

Meaning credits: dc

## Formation

- Verb (casual form) + わけではない
- な-adjective + な + わけではない
- い-adjective + わけではない
