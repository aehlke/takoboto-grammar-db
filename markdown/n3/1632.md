# 積もりだった

Source: [Takoboto #1632](https://takoboto.jp/bunpo/1632/)  
Label: tsumoridatta  
JLPT: N3  
Credits: chadxtiman  
Retrieved: 2026-10-03T05:36:13+00:00  
License: [CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/)

Other forms: つもりだった

## Meaning

I had the intention to do (but I didn't)

僕は彼女と結婚するつもりだった。/ I had the intention to marry her (but I didn't).

Meaning credits: chadxtiman
