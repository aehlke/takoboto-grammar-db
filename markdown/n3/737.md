## Meaning

? (sentence ending particle - question) \[F\]

What is it you don't get?

Meaning credits: Amatuka

## Formation

- Phrase + の

## See also

- [〜か](../n4/775.md)

## Examples

### Example 882

「納得いかないんですよ」「何が？何がです**の**？」

「なっとくいかないんですよ」「なにが？なにがですの？」

en: "I just can't accept that\!" "What? What is it that you can't accept **?** "

Credits: Amatuka

### Example 3260

en: This shop is already closed, **is it?**  It's still 4.

Credits: Miki, bamboo4
