## Meaning

Can\`t possibly

There is so much (food) that I can\`t possibly eat it all

ja: 無理と言う意味

Meaning credits: tigert

## Formation

- とても + verb negative form

## Examples

### Example 6603

たくさんあって､とても食べきれません

たくさんあってとてもたべきれません

en: There is so much (food) that I can\`t possible eat it all.

Credits: tigert

### Example 6604

東京ではとても家なんか買えません。

とうきょうではとてもいえなんかかえません。

en: I can\`t possibly buy a house in Tokyo.

Credits: tigert

## Discussion

### Comment 1 — tigert

This phrase expresses a very strong sense of impossiblity. The verb is usually in the potential form.

### Comment 2 — nicemon

「たくさんあって､とても食べきれません」 fits with the translation if you read it carefully...

### Comment 3 — gahoangdai

Ex#6603...the potential form of 食べる Is 食べられる。。。So..the example must be...たくさんあって、とても食べられません。

### Comment 4 — vizoso

The example is correct. 食べきれません is in potential form.<br/>食べ : masu form of "to eat"<br/>切る : Used as an auxiliary verb to mean "to finish an action completely"<br/>切る became 切れる and then 切れません
