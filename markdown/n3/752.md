## Meaning

\! (exclamation mark, add emphasis, denote new information being imparted)

The bus is coming\!

Meaning credits: Amatuka

## Discussion

### Comment 1 — Amatuka

よ [yo] is used at the end of the sentence (e.g. a sentence ending particle).<br/><br/>S = Sentence.

### Comment 2 — Amatuka

Generally not to be used to 'out group' / 'superiors'.
