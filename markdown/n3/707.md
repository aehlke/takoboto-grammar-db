## Meaning

Vm, despite, while, although

while knowing that smoking is bad, I just keep doing it

Meaning credits: dc, perezalvarezhi

## Formation

- Verb masu stem + ながら
- Noun + ながら
- な-Adjective (without な) + ながら
- い-Adjective + ながら
