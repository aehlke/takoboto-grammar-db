## Meaning

but, although (contrast, surprising),even though,despite

It's summer but it's cold.

ja: 夏なのに、寒い。

Meaning credits: dc

## Formation

- い-Adjective + のに
- な-Adjective (without な) + なのに
- Noun + なのに

## Examples

### Example 5124

このコンピューター１年前に買ったばかり**なのに**、もうすっかり時代遅れだわ。

このコンピューター１ねんまえにかったばかりなのに、もうすっかりじだいおくれだわ。

en: **Even though**  I bought this computer one year ago, it's already old fashioned.

Credits: dc

en: This information is not as up-to-date as it should be.

en: You must have a strong leg to have walked all the way.
