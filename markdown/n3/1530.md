## Meaning

-like

It is a child-like expression.

Meaning credits: bi-ru

## Formation

- Verb stem + っぽい
- Noun + っぽい

### Comment 1 — ninja\_k

The following is a subject of debate among native speakers: っぽい (っぽ) can only be used for comparisons to what the speaker considers commonly accepted knowledge. Young people hold no such restrictions, but the older generation does (&gt;_

### Comment 2 — rubyhatchet

＊How to use this grammar as an adjective or adverb (works just like an い adjective): 「〜っぽい + Ｎ」 or 「〜っぽく + Ｖ」.<br/>Example: 子供っぽい人 [a childish person] <br/>子供っぽく見える [look childish]<br/><br/>FORMATION:<br/>Ｖ(minus ます) + っぽい<br/>いＡ(minus い) + っぽい<br/>Ｎ + っぽい
