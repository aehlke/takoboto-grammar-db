## Meaning

Used much like the な of na-adjectives - somewhat archaic.

With my sacred sword I shall smite thee\!

Meaning credits: Amatuka

- [〜な](../n3/638.md)

## Examples

### Example 717

悪魔め！俺様は聖**なる**剣で貴様を倒す！

あくまめ！おれさまはせいなるけんできさまをたおす！

en: Foul demon\! \_I\_ will smite you with this sacred **+** sword\!

Credits: Amatuka

### Example 5326

スター・ウォーズのエピソード４の副題は、「新た**なる**希望」である。

のエピソード４のふくだいは、「あらたなるきぼう」である。

en: The subtitle of Episod IV of Star Wars is "a new **+** hope".

Credits: Miki

This shows up constantly in the (imitation?) archaic style that seems to be preferred for writing spell incantations in the fantasy genre.<br/><br/>Taking a few examples from spell incantations in the Slayers anime...<br/>ドラグ・スレイブ（竜破斬） (Dragon Slave):<br/>...偉大[なる]汝の名において　我ここに　闇に誓わん...<br/>...I hereby swear unto darkness by thy * great name...<br/>リカバリイ（治療） (Recovery):<br/>聖[なる]癒しの御手よ　母[なる]大地の息吹よ...<br/>O holy * healing hand, O breath of Mother * Earth...<br/><br/>Looking at 母なる大地, it seems that なる can also be used with nouns (like の in modern Japanese).
