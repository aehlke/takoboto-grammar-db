# くらい、ぐらい (位)

## Meaning

to the extent that; so ~ that ~ almost ~; at least; the only ~; rather than ~

It's so warm this evening that we (almost) don't need a heater.

Meaning credits: rubyhatchet

## Examples

### Example 6081

今晩は暖かいので、ストーブが要らない**くらい**だ。

こんばんはあたたかいので、ストーブがいらないくらいだ。

en: It's so warm this evening that we **almost**  don't need a heater.

Credits: rubyhatchet

### Example 6082

私たちは一歩も歩けない**くらい**疲れていた。

わたしたちはいちほもあるけないくらいつかれていた。

en: We were so tired that we couldn't take a single step.

Credits: rubyhatchet

### Example 6083

山田さん**くらい**物を忘れる人はいない。

やまださんくらいものをわすれるひとはいない。

en: There's no one as forgetful as Yamada (who forgets things as much as Yamada).

Credits: rubyhatchet

### Example 6084

私は料理は下手ですが、ご飯**くらい**(は)炊けます。

わたしはりょうりはへたですが、ごはんくらいはたけます。

en: I'm a poor cook, but I can at least cook rice.

Credits: rubyhatchet

### Example 6085

今この仕事が出来るのは彼**くらい**のものだ。

いまこのしごとができるのはかれくらいのものだ。

en: He's the only person who can do this job now. (The person who's capable enough to do this job is he.)

Credits: rubyhatchet

### Example 6086

そんなことをする**くらい**なら、死んだほうがましだ。

そんなことをするくらいなら、しんだほうがましだ。

en: I would rather die than do such a thing.

Credits: rubyhatchet

### Example 6137

合格の知らせを受け取ったときは、うれしくて、踊りあがった**くらい**だ。

ごうかくのしらせをうけとったときは、うれしくて、おどりあがったくらいだ。

en: When I received the notice that I had passed, I was so happy that I **almost** leaped for joy.

Credits: rubyhatchet

### Comment 2 — rubyhatchet

[kurai / くらい] with a negative predicate indicates a superlative. (Mr. Yamada is the most forgetful.)<br/><br/>It also expresses the idea of "at least." ?くらい in this use implies that the degree of a state is such that one cannot expect much more than X from that state.<br/><br/>Ｓのは?くらいのものだ expresses the idea that the speaker cannot think of anyone or anything else but ? that meets the description in Ｓ. In other words, ? meets the description in Ｓ to the highest degree among those the speaker can think of.<br/><br/>Ｖ1くらいならＶ2ほうがましだ expresses the idea the one would rather Ｖ2 than Ｖ1.
