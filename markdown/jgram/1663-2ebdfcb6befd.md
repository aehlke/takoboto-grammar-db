## Meaning

AT〜; TO〜

話しかける＝speak to/address （someone）;　笑いかける＝smile at/to

## Notes

### 1 — deeana

VBryk （ます）＋かける<br/>
- a communicative action is directed toward someone <br/>
<br/>
a).　彼は彼女に話しかけた。<br/>
He addressed/ started talking to her.<br/>
<br/>
b).彼女は彼に笑いかけた。<br/>
She smiled at him <br/>
<br/>
