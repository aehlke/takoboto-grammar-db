<li><a>[that]と言うわけで、[this]   [ex #902]</a></li>
And for [that] <strong>reason </strong>[this]   <br/>
<li><a>僕は決して農業が嫌いな訳じゃない。     [ex #903]</a></li>
By no means do I dislike farming. (Lit: The <strong>reason </strong>is not that I dislike farming)   <br/>
<li><a>私は訳もなく悲しくなった。   [ex #904]</a></li>
I felt sad for no <strong>reason </strong>.   <br/>
<li><a>わけありの二人   [ex #1268]</a></li>
A couple with <strong>some amorous circumstances </strong> <br/>
<li><a>それを纏めるのはわけはない   [ex #1269]</a></li>
Not <strong>difficult </strong>to resolve that issue.   <br/>
<li><a>それとこれとはわけが違う   [ex #1270]</a></li>
You are trying to compare <strong>apples and oranges. </strong> <br/>
<li><a>そういうわけには行かない.   [ex #4960]</a></li>
That is not <strong>the way </strong>the ball bounces.   <br/>
<li><a>訳の判らぬことを言うな。   [ex #4961]</a></li>
Don't tell me such <strong>nonsense! </strong> <br/>
<li><a>たとえアルバイトしても、勉強を残るわけにはいかない。.   [ex #5735]</a></li>
Even if you do have a part-time job, that's no reason to drop your study. <strong>. </strong> <br/>
<li><a>彼女の部屋に行った事がある訳ないよ！   [ex #6991]</a></li>
There's no way I've been to her room! <strong> </strong> <br/>
<li><a>そんなにたくさん食べられる訳ないよ！
   [ex #6990]</a></li>
There's no way I can eat that much! <strong> </strong> <br/>
<li><a>毎日三時間も日本語を勉強しているんですか。　よく出来るわけですね。wake   [ex #8049]</a></li>
(Are you studying Japanese(as long as)three hours every day? No wonder your Japanese is good.) <strong>wake </strong> <br/>

<li>わけ originally comes from the act of dividing things into what is right and what is wrong.Therefore, わけ came to mean "reason" or the rationale of how things are done. That later got expanded to include the amorous relationship in the sense of "there is a reason for them to be together or in love," as suggested by <b><a href="http://www.jgram.org:80/rss/jlpt3.xml#1268">#1268</a></b>.<br/>
 (contributor: <i>bamboo4</i>)<br/>
<li>〜わけだ:<br/>
１．〜ということが納得できる (no wonder.../ that's why....)<br/>
<br/>
2.　〜という結論になる; Note:「〜というわけだ」の形も多い。<br/>
e.g. 「200キロ1000円なら、2000円で400キロ買えるわけだ。」<br/>
 (contributor: <i>誠</i>)<br/>
<li>「〜というわけだ。」: (Therefore) 〜as a conclusion / 〜as a result / "By this reason....., therefore can be concluded that 〜"/ "It can be said that 〜　as a conclusion/result"  <br/>
<br/>
<br/>
「〜というわけで．．．」: "By 〜 as the conclusion,... "/ "Concluding with 〜 as a result,..."<br/>
<br/>
「〜というわけではない」: "It can't be concluded that 〜" /  "It doesn't mean that 〜" / "It doesn't follow that 〜 as a conclusion/result"<br/>
<br/>
〜次第だ：〜というわけだ　（事情／経過を示す）<br/>
e.g.「　．．．。以上のような次第で、....」＝<br/>
　　「．．．というわけで、....」<br/>
 (contributor: <i>誠</i>)<br/>

 (contributor: <i>Miki</i>)<br/>
