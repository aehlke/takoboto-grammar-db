## Meaning

so, therefore (explaining something unavoidable)

The dog ate my homework (so) I can't turn it in today.

## Notes

### 1 — Televangelist

Can't believe this got uploaded without anyone explaining its usage.  You have to ask yourself - if "so..." is all monodesukara means, why not simply use "desukara"?<br/>
<br/>
Monodesukara is used in situations where you're giving a reason why something couldn't be helped, usually something negative - a <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> kind of situation.<br/>
<br/>
For instance, if the dog ate your homework and you were explaining that to your teacher, you'd use monodesukara to finish up giving your reason.<br/>
<br/>
It's used in cases where you're obviously conciliatory due to the unfortunate outcome, but humbly emphasizing that it wasn't your fault.
