## Meaning

of all the ... / especially / only / just

our child of all children / especially our child

## Notes

### 1 — dc

nikagitte<br/>
can mean limited to<br/>
明日に限って、割引します<br/>
just for tomorrow, we are giving a discount

### 2 — rubyhatchet

＊This is a compound particle to show that only X is different from others. It's meant to express an exclusive focus on topic item X. The predicate is either explicitly negative as in example <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=nikagitte#">#</a></b>, or implicitly negative as in example <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=nikagitte#">#</a></b>. An explicitly affirmative predicate cannot occur with に限って.<br/>
<br/>
THIS IS WRONG: その日に限っていい天気だった。 (Only on that day, the weather was good.)

## Discussion

### 1 — Amatuka

The implication is that you wouldn't put it past _other_ children.

### 2 — dc

ex1 might be translated as "would be the last to"

### 3 — halx

What about "would be the last to do" (sorry I'm not a native speaker of neither English nor Japanese)?<br/>
<br/>
Other forms: ~に限る with two meanings<br/>
1) resticted to, exclusively, only (女性に限る)<br/>
2) the best (夏はビールに限る)

### 4 — dc

i think this expresses a contrast, or slight exasperation. <br/>
"I usually dont sleep in, but just this one day (when i had an interview) I overslept".

### 5 — dc

there isnt a direct single word in english for it. "of all days" / "just this day" / "particularly today"
