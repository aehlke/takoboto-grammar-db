## Meaning

Vte,  (te-form verb + contrastative ha  wa)

Don't die\! (Lit: Your dying would be terrible)

## Discussion

### 1 — Amatuka

Note this form shortens in a similar way to てしまう。<br/>
ては→ちゃ<br/>
では→じゃ

### 2 — Amatuka

Formed from te-form verb + ha (wa)<br/>
<br/>
Vte = te-form verb

### 3 — anon

The first sentence is the same as 死んではいけない/ならい:<br/>
You must not die!

### 4 — anon

I'm sorry, but what is the exact usage of this phrasing?

### 5 — yookoso

I read somewhere that the te form is considered a noun. I find that a useful thing to keep in mind in cases like this ては...

### 6 — ljfurlong

I guess this just means "doing (this) would be..." <br/>
<br/>
The two examples given (だめ、まずい）are negative. Can this be used positively?<br/>
例：行っては素晴らしい。<br/>
行っちゃ素晴らしい。<br/>
