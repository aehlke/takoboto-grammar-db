## Meaning

in the middle of; not yet finished V-ing

I fell asleep while in the middle of reading the magazine.

## Notes

### 1 — deeana

Verb ryk (VB masu) kakeru<br/>
- used with Transitive/Intransitive verbs<br/>
- an action/ a state is about to take place- you start doing something but you don't finish that action, so it remains half done<br/>
= BEGIN TO~; BE ABOUT TO~; ALMOST (DO)~<br/>
<br/>
a). ご飯を食べかけた時に、地震が起きた。<br/>
　　When I was about to eat[/just started to eat] my meal, the earthquake occured. (so I didn't finish the action)<br/>
<br/>
b).父は若い時に、海でおぼれかけたそうだ。<br/>
I was told that my father almost drowned in the sea when he was young.<br/>
<br/>
c).雨が降りかけたが、また天気になった。<br/>
It started to rain, but it became fine again.<br/>
<br/>
d).オアは開きかけたが、また閉まってしまった。<br/>
The door begun to open, but it closed again<br/>

### 2 — brazenxvirtue

ます形　＋　かける<br/>
<br/>
1. 〜かけている/〜かける：実際に起こる直前<br/>
This form takes the meaning of "just as", "just before", or "on the verge of" "about to." It uses verbs that finish or change in an instant (一瞬で終わる/変わること), i.e. 消える、死ぬ、切れる etc.<br/>
<br/>
Ex:<br/>
携帯電話のバッテリーが切れかけている。<br/>
私は子供の頃病気で死にかけたことがあるらしい。<br/>
死にかけていた猫を拾ってきた。<br/>
<br/>
2. 〜かける/〜かけて: 直前<br/>
This form implies the idea that the action had barely begun and then stopped.<br/>
<br/>
Ex.<br/>
今日こそ彼に一言いってやろうと思ったが、言いかけてやめた。In other words, "he" never got the complaint. The speaker probably stopped at, "umm... nevermind."<br/>
<br/>
宿題の文作を書きかけたが、なかなか先にすすまないので結局途中でやめてしまった。This person probably got as far as writing their name before quitting.<br/>
<br/>
3. 〜かけの/〜かけだ: 途中<br/>
This form means in the middle of an incomplete action. It takes verbs that take time/occur over time (動作が続く動詞), i.e. 食べる、飲む、etc.<br/>
<br/>
Ex.<br/>
テーブルの上の飲みかけのコーヒー、誰の。<br/>

### 2 — angelitosh2004

〜かける indicated that one has begun an action and is currently involved with it. In almost all cases 〜かけるmay be used in place of 〜かかる, although it expresses a slightly more advanced state (see examples above).
