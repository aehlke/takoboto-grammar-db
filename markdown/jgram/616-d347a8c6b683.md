## Meaning

it must mean, it boils down to



## Notes

### 1 — dc

This has two meanings<br/>
1. something I heard:<br/>
新聞によると、また地下鉄の運賃が値上げされるとういうことだ。<br/>
I heard (read!) from the newspaper that subway fares will increase again.<br/>
<br/>
2. it means that, as mentioned here <br/>
<br/>
(thanks Brett)

### 2 — rubyhatchet

dc is correct.<br/>
<br/>
ということだ(1): "It seems that〜" (something heard or read).<br/>
FORMATION:<br/>
Ｖ(plain form) + ということだ<br/>
いＡ + ということだ<br/>
なＡ・Ｎ + (だ) + ということだ<br/>
<br/>
ということだ(2): "The fact that 〜 means 〜" (in other words; that is to say; in short)<br/>
FORMATION (same as above):<br/>
Ｖ(plain form) + ということだ<br/>
いＡ + ということだ<br/>
なＡ・Ｎ + (だ) + ということだ

### 3 — rubyhatchet

＊Be careful!<br/>
There is a difference between (1) 〜というのは、〜ということだ　and (2) 〜ということは、〜ということだ.<br/>
<br/>
(1) is meant to give a defintion, explanation, or interpretion of a word, phrase, or sentence.<br/>
(2) is meant to provide an interpretation of a fact (or something nearly factual).

From now on I`m going to save my money.<br/>
