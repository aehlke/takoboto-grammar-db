## Meaning

'object marker'  (also shown as 'o') 目的

That \[←marked by object marker\] please.

## Notes

### 1 — sarruby

を follows a noun or place. It indicates that that noun is the direct object of the verb, that the place is the place of departure, or that the place was passed through.<br/>
<br/>
を is romanized as "wo" or "o".<br/>
<br/>
Amatuka said this below.

## Discussion

### 1 — Amatuka

Note : The object marker を is <b>said</b> 'o' but may be <b>written</b> as either 'wo' or 'o' depending on romaji system used.

### 2 — Amatuka

The three uses of を <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> are <br/>
1. objective / target (目的) <br/>
2. area passed through (通過点) <br/>
3. departure point (出発点)

### 3 — Amatuka

を is used to mark a noun or noun phrase.<br/>
<br/>
N = noun.
