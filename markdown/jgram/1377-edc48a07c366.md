## Meaning

Notwithstanding, even while,  even though,  as she was

child as she was

<br/>
ex <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=nagaramo#">#</a></b> "Even though a woman she fought bravely" or "Even though she was only a woman she fought bravely"<br/>
<br/>
ex <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=nagaramo#">#</a></b> "Even though he was only a child he was brave" or "He was brave even though only a child."<br/>
