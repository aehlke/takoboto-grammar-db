## Meaning

by; according to; due to; owing to; due to

we lost our father due to the war

## Notes

### 1 — dc

Usage:<br/>
<br/>
Noun<br/>
辞典によって漢字を調べる<br/>
examine kanji by using a dictionary<br/>
<br/>
YesNo<br/>
お金が貰えるかどうかによって決めます<br/>
depending on whether or not I can receive money, I will decide<br/>
<br/>
SubjInf<br/>
読むことによって決めます<br/>
I can decide by reading

The example on the note 辞典によって漢字を調べる is not natural. Usually　辞典（字典）で漢字を調べる。　<br/>

### 6 — lilibr

### 7 — Monte Cristo

lilibr, ni yori is simply a literary version of ni yotte. It is common in written style.
