## Meaning

? (for casual questions)

Who's it?

## Notes

### 1 — american57

The equivalent of a question mark. Used mostly in informal male speech. Ungrammatical for questions that can be answered "yes/no" (see also "kai"). Unsuitable for asking a superior a question.<br/>
<br/>
There is a second "dai" at the end of sentences.  It is equivalent to a weak exclamation point and is heard in the speech of little boys. "Sore boku no dai."  That's mine!

## Discussion

### 1 — Amatuka

Typical 'standard politeness' question ends in <br/>
ですか or Vmますか。(e.g. いますか。Are (you) in?)<br/>
Casual questions can omit the ? particle and use <br/>
tone. (e.g. 誰だ？ Who is it?)<br/>
Alternatively だい and かい can be used.<br/>
The <b>plain</b> form with か is quite abrupt and rough.
