## Meaning

verb-totally

totally sold out

## Notes

### 1 — IMABI

-kiru is an auxiliary verb that shows you did something completely.

## Discussion

### 1 — Amatuka

Formed from Verb -masu + kiru<br/>
Note that 'kiru' conjugates like a normal verb <br/>
(e.g. past tense, negative etc are possible)<br/>
Vm = Verb -masu base

### 2 — infinite\_trial

is this somehow related to shimau which also means to complete?

### 3 — eidolon\_shell

I believe kiru is more informal and prose in nature than shimau.

### 4 — atsui\_nemurenai

The difference between shimau and kiru is not a matter of informal and formal, I don't use the words in that way as a native.Their nuance are different and they are used in a different way. <br/>
Kiru would be one intend to finish doing something "to the end." <br/>
Shiamu would be one finish something,  "sometimes" with the nuance it was against one's will.<br/>
ハリーはなんとかその川を泳ぎきった。 ex4654<br/>
Harry managed to swim across the river( with his intension.)<br/>
ハリーはなんとかその川を泳いで渡ってしまった。 <br/>
Harry somehow managed to swim across the river.<br/>
<br/>
ダイエット中のくせに彼女はケーキを食べきりました。 ex <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=kiru#">#</a></b> <br/>
Even though (despite the fact) she was on a diet, she ate all of the cake. (ate it to the end) . (She ate all knowing what she was doing.)<br/>
ダイエット中のくせに彼女はケーキを食べてしまった。<br/>
(I don't know she ate cake all or not, anyway) although she is on diet, she ate a cake (as she could not stop herself.)<br/>
<br/>
夏休みが前に、宿題をやりきろう。<br/>
Let's finish all the homework before summer vacation starts.<br/>
夏休みが前に、宿題をできるだけやってしまおう。<br/>
Let's finish homework as much as we can before summer vacation.
