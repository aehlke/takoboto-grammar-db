## Meaning

'topic marker' (also shown as 'wa')

X wa Y desu : X is Y

## Notes

### 1 — Denzil

Introducing は "wa" and です。"desu."<br/>
the particle は marks the topic.<br/>
(Remember particles attach to the word which they follow, and that when は is a particle it is pronounced wa.)<br/>
です。functions like "is" or "topic is."<br/>

### 2 — Rinji\_HalfElf

<b>は</b><br/>
<br/>
Is pronounced as "wah".<br/>
は a subject or topic marker.<br/>
は can appear as ha, or wa in romaji depending on the system used.<br/>
<br/>
The kana used for は is usually pronounced as "hah", but pronounced as "wah" when used as a particle.<br/>
The same phenomenon happens in the word "dewa/deha" では in the negative conjugation of the verb <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b>. <br/>
<br/>
<b>Other notes:</b><br/>
In yakuza speech,<br/>
は、becomes や　(yah).<br/>
Typically, the y sound is softened such that it sounds like "ah"<br/>
Example:<br/>
Ore ha --turns to-&gt; Ore ya --but sounds like-&gt; Ore a<br/>

## Discussion

### 1 — Amatuka

See <a href="http://homepage3.nifty.com/i-yasu/Lesson5.htm">here</a> and <a href="http://homepage3.nifty.com/i-yasu/lesson6.htm">here</a> for two very interesting Japanese pages on using ha vs. ga

### 2 — Amatuka

When to use は vs が?<br/>
Both sentences like 雨は降っている。[ame ha <br/>
futteiru] and 雨が降っている。[ame ga futteiru] are <br/>
valid, so working out when to use which form is a <br/>
tricky point.<br/>
<br/>
First: In answering a question such as <br/>
Q. 机の上に何がありますか。[tsukue no ue ni nani ga arimasu ka]<br/>
A. 鉛筆があります。[enpitsu ga arimasu].<br/>
Here が is used.

### 3 — Amatuka

On the other hand if attention has been placed on something in advance and the question is asked with that object as the topic...<br/>
Q. 本はどこにありますか。[hon ha doko ni arimasu ka.]<br/>
A. 本はイスの下にあります。[hon ha isu no shita ni arimasu]<br/>
then は is used.

### 4 — Amatuka

が is also used to indicate a specific choice.<br/>
私が行きます。[watashi ga ikimasu]<br/>
_I'll_ go. (not any of the other possibilities).

### 5 — Amatuka

は is used for contrastative sentences (see <a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=ha-2">ha-2</a>).<br/>
For example normally you'd say <br/>
犬が好きです。[inu ga suki desu] I like dogs.<br/>
However if you want to contrast how you feel about dogs with how you feel about other animals then<br/>
犬は好きだが、猫はどうも・・・[inu ha suki da ga, neko ha doumo ...]<br/>
I like _dogs_ but I really don't (get on with) cats.

### 6 — Amatuka

は [ha / wa] follows a noun or a noun phrase.<br/>
(e.g. 犬は [inu ha] 'as for dogs'<br/>
知るのは [shiru no ha] 'as for what (I) know'<br/>
The latter case の nominalizes (turns into a noun) 知る)<br/>
<br/>
N = noun
