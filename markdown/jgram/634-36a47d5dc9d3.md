## Discussion

### 1 — Amatuka

Note Japanese 'AdSpeak' transformed the meaning of 'manshion'.

### 2 — dc

和風　&gt; japanese style

### 3 — rahulsangole

why is it de ii desu? and not ga ii desu?<br/>
"nihonfuunomanshiongaiidesu"

### 4 — anon

Regarding "nihon-fuu no manshon GA iidesu", nihon-fuu no manshion would be your first priority in your selection list. Meanwhile "nihon-fuu no manshon DE iidesu" implys "I don't care much what style of apartment is, but Japanese-style is fine if you recommended or if anything, Japanese-style is preferable.

### 5 — Miki

<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=fuu#">#</a></b> Japanese is weird. I would say どんな卵料理が作れますか。<br/>
For exmaple 「何風のお好み焼きが作れますか。」「広島風です。」<br/>
or どんな風に卵を料理しますか。How do you cook an egg?
