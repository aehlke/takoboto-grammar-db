## Meaning

considering ..., for ...

she looks good for her age

## Notes

### 1 — demcjojo

わりに<br/>
rather <br/>
fairly<br/>
relatively<br/>
comparatively<br/>

### 2 — rubyhatchet

「ＡのわりにＢ」<br/>
＊This grammar expresses a judgement in which the situation expressed by Ｂ does not meet the expectations of Ａ or seem like what Ａ implies.<br/>
<br/>
FORMATION:<br/>
Ｖ(plain form) + わりに<br/>
Ｎ + の + わりに<br/>
いＡ + わりに<br/>
なＡ + な + わりに

## Discussion

### 1 — Amatuka

Kanji version 割に

### 2 — dc

in Ex 3&gt;<br/>
　　悪く<b>は</b>ない<br/>
　　上手くない<br/>
why does the negative case take a は？

### 3 — Miki

You can say 悪くない。<br/>
は here is used to emphasis "bad".<br/>
So you can say 上手く_は_ない。, too.

### 4 — bamboo4

Using 上手く probalby would confuse students. Why not use うまく like all Japanese would use?

### 5 — bamboo4

Corrected 上手く to うまく.<br/>

### 6 — Ludi

Same as (な)のに // にしては //  ...だが、...<br/>
「A(の)わりにB」＝「Bの状態は、Aらしくない・Aに合わない」という評価を表す。<br/>
