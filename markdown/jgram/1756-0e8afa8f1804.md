## Meaning

Emphasizing a strong emotional reaction either good or bad ~をとても強く感じる・話してがそのことに対して強い感情を持っているときに用いる。

I'm absolutely furious about the way he treats me.

## Notes

### 1 — LR

<b>On the difference between <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> and <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b></b><br/>
There are two たえないs of note in the Japanese language... hurray.　How to distinguish them?<br/>
<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> is for saying you just can't stand something and should be constructed from VERB辞書形 + にたえない<br/>
<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> is for saying that you have a strong emotional reaction to something - "氏の当会へのご援助はまことに感謝に堪えません｜We cannot be too grateful to him for the help he has given our society." and should be constructed NOUN + たえない.<br/>
<br/>
Both are on the old JLPT 1 syllabus, if anyone knows what's on the new N1, please advise
