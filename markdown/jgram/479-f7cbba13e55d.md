## Meaning

to be possible, to be likely

Do you think this is feasible?

## Discussion

### 1 — Rahulk

Can somebody brief me What is the difference between ありうる&amp;ありえる？

### 2 — bamboo4

ありえる is an incorrect reading of あり得る, which should be pronounced as "ariuru." As such it is not a proper Japanese word.

### 3 — bamboo4

Forgot to mention that 有り得ない is properly "arienai." Here the comstruction is あり＋得ない, thus there is no inconsistency with ありうる.<br/>
