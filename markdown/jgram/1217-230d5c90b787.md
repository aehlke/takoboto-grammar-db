## Discussion

### 1 — Amatuka

This is one of the weaker 'because' words, if you see what I mean.

### 2 — Amatuka

In other words, the causal relationship isn't always spelled out in obvious terms in translations of sentences with 'node', hence the 'and' option.

### 3 — dc

node is almost a connector between phrases, whereas <a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=karaniha">karaniha</a> is much stronger.

### 4 — Miki

But you cannot replace 'node' with 'karaniha'. Please see <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b>.

### 5 — GenkiGuy

ex <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=node#">#</a></b> - Can 'node' also mean 'Since' in this context? "He shoplifted goods in a supermarket yesterday since he was hard up for money."
