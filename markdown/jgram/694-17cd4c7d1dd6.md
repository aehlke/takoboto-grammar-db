## Meaning

why?, what for?

Why didn't you come?

## Notes

### 1 — natsumi

どうして　must end with &lt;b&gt;んです&lt;/b&gt;　instead of まです　form

### 7 — bamboo4

どうして来るのですか。招待していなのに。<br/>
Why do you come?<br/>
<br/>
The above is rather ambiguous. The English translation should be "Why do you want to come,even when not invited." <br/>
<br/>
Besides, the expression is pretty rude. Consider: どうしてお出でになりたいのですか、ご招待申しあげてもいませんのに.

### 8 — tigert

bamboo<br/>
<br/>
I have never said "why do you want to come, even when not invited" in my entire life. (native English speaker)<br/>
<br/>
A natural translation would be "Why are you coming? I didn`t invite you." <br/>
<br/>
English is a little more direct than Japanese. translating a phrase word for word never sounds natural.
