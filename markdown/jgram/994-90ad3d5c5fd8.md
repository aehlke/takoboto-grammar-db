## Meaning

apparently,  naturally

of course she got angry

## Discussion

### 1 — bamboo4

済みません means that one's mind remains unsettled and therefore not 澄む, that is not clear. From 澄む came the concept of 済む meaning "finishied."<br/>

### 2 — bamboo4

You can simply say どうも and you can dispense with all other phrases you can think of!<br/>
