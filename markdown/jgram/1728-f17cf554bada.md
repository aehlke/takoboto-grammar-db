## Meaning

begin/start...

Rain started to fall.

## Notes

### 1 — Maru1

Vマス＋だす

### 2 — american57

This suffix, meaning to start doing something, must be distinguished from a host of compound verbs ending in "dasu," such as 乗り出す(launch), 染出す (dye), 積み出す, (send off), など。I believe it is new Level ３．
