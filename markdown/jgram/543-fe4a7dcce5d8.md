## Meaning

as soon as, the instant

as soon as I opened the door

## Notes

### 1 — rubyhatchet

「Aたとたん、B」: After doing A, or after A happened, almost simultaneously, B occurred. B is an abnormal/unusual event that happens suddenly. The case where B is a strange incident occurs often.<br/>
<br/>
Vた形 + とたん<br/>
Vた形 + とたんに (used as an adverb) See example <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=totan(ni)#">#</a></b> and example <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=totan(ni)#">#</a></b>.

### 2 — brij\_shukla2000

This is a noun/adverb which expresses the idea that something occured the moment or took place..<br/>
<br/>
example ..<br/>
ドアがあけた東端にカレーの匂いがした。<br/>
The moment I opened the door, I smelled curry.

### 3 — deeana

VB た/ その/ VBたら, + 途端に<br/>
｛→と同時に｝<br/>
-&gt; the action in PS takes place very soon or simultaneously with the action in PP<br/>
①-&gt; PP - uncontrolable action, although when the SBJ of the VB is a 3rd person, a controlable VB in PP is acceptable<br/>
ex:＊私は先生の顔を見たとたん、立ち上がった。<br/>
　I stood up the moment I saw my techer. (wrong use)<br/>
ex:洋子さんは先生の顔を見たとたん、立ち上がった。<br/>
<br/>
②-&gt;　その can precede TOTAN, この　and　あの　cannnot<br/>
ex：助かったと思った。そのとたん（に）、力が抜けて気を失ってしまった。I thought I was saved; then (lit: in that moment), my strenght was gone and I lost conscience.<br/>
<br/>
-&gt; と同時に　expresses the simmilar idea；does not have the restricions described in ①<br/>
<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b>a)　先生の顔を{見たとたん・見ると同時に}宿題があったことを思い出した。<br/>
　b）＊私は 先生の顔を{見ると同時に・*みたとたん}立ち上がった。<br/>
<br/>
-&gt; と同時に expresses concurent states, while とたん is used only for actions<br/>
ex:群衆が遠くに｛見えると同時に・＊見えたとたんに｝太鼓の音も聞こえる。A crowd can be seen in the distance and, at the same time, drums can be heared.<br/>
-&gt; と同時に can be preceded by Nouns&amp; Adj,とたん no

### 12 — 赤毛

Tigertさん, I think that PP and PS refer to french grammar. Proposition Principale in french, also called main clause in english, is the part wich ends the sentence in japanese. Proposition Subordonnee, also called dependent clause in english, is the part wich comes first in japanese. Clause is 文節 in japanese. <br/>
Ex: お弁当を食べた学生が公園に行った。<br/>
お弁当を食べた-&gt;PS<br/>
学生が公園に行った。-&gt;PP<br/>
In other terms, PS is the part before とたん, and PP is the last part of the sentence.
