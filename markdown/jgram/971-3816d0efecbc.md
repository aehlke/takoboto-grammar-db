## Meaning

leaving A aside,  A is good, but B is another thing

price aside, its great.

## Notes

### 1 — dc

Aはともかく、( Bは良い）<br/>
　　= leaving aside A (B is good)<br/>
　　= A is another thing but B is great.<br/>
<br/>
usually it is used for two contrasting things. <br/>
　　A hatomokaku B ha ii<br/>
　　= A is bad but B is good.<br/>
<br/>
　　味はともかく、値段が安い<br/>
　　The taste is another thing, but the price is good<br/>
<br/>
But this can also be used just for asides<br/>
　　費用の問題はともかく<br/>
　　setting aside the question of expense

### 2 — gvrchandran

kuroi kaoiro wa tonikaku, kare wa shinsetsu na hito desu<br/>
gvramachandran
