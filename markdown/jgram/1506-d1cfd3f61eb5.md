## Meaning

(can't) possibly / very much とても

I can't possibly do that.

## Notes

### 1 — dc

到底(toutei) itself means "possibly", but grammatically it is usually used with a negative:<br/>
　到底そうとは思えない。<br/>
　I really doubt it.<br/>
<br/>
this is a little similar to <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b>'s way of use<br/>
　英語しか話せない
