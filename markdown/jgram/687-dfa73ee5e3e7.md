### 2 — your name

in the example above the と is a half of こと 「事 」<br/>
I think it is almost always になる <br/>
に as in "to" (a place). maybe sometimes its omitted.<br/>
　大変となります...<br/>
someone native will have to answer...<br/>

### 3 — Miki

I don't think there is a difference between them when noun + -となる or -になる.<br/>
eg. ok 雨になる。雨となる。社長になる。社長となる。<br/>
But I agree -になる is used mostly.
