## Meaning

because/since - explains the cause or gives a reason for  他でもない〜の(ひと・こと・もの）だから

Since it was early morning, there wasn't even a dog around.

## Notes

### 1 — crlane

Not normally used in speech. A more formal, written word for letters and whatnot.  Explains the reason for something with the sense that it is precisely because of (person,thing) that(  ). <br/>
昼間はにぎやかなこの道も、早朝のこととてあたりに犬一匹いなかった。<br/>
Because it was early in the morning, there wasn't even a dog around this street that is so lively during the day.<br/>
〜のことだから、〜のことなので　<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b>

### 2 — LR

<b>Ref <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=kototote#">#</a></b></b> Kanzen Master Level 1 - p20 - no.19

### 3 — LR

Often used to give reasons for excuses or apologies.
