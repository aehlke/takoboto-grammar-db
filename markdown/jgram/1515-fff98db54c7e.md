## Meaning

Keep ~ing / To leave ~

I kept standing for 5 hours.

## Discussion

### 1 — blabby

This is a rare case of 一級 grammar that I hear in every day conversation.  I learnt this when I was still a beginner.  I think it has a similar meaning to "〜たまま".

### 2 — bamboo4

It is a colloqual form of 放し, meaning doing something and leave it at that.It has a lot more emphasis than ...たままにする.

### 3 — Futaro

How can I know how must I read the kanji, if in on yomi or kunyomi.<br/>
For example: in nﾂｰ 5446 is:<br/>
koko ni "TE" ki ppanachi<br/>
or<br/>
koko ni "OKU" ki ppanachi?<br/>
It is very difficult for people who donﾂｴt know japanese very well.

### 4 — kryptolus

It's not that difficult.<br/>
The fact that there's a hiragana 'ki' after it tells you that it should be 'oki'. This has nothing to do with this grammar though...

### 5 — Cacawate

It's common practice that in compounds you'll be using onyomi.
