## Meaning

not always,  not necessarily

expensive things are not always better

## Notes

### 1 — dc

seems to be used in a couple of ways:<br/>
<br/>
with <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b><br/>
A ga kanarazushimo B yori (adjective) tohakagiranai<br/>
高い物が[必ずしも]安いものより良いとは限らない。<br/>
Expensive things are not always better than cheap things.<br/>
<br/>
お金持ちが必ずしも幸せとは限らない。<br/>
<br/>
also with a verb<br/>
<br/>
お金が幸福を<b>もたらす</b>とは限らない。<br/>
Money does not always bring happiness. <br/>
<br/>
 いつでも自分の思い通りに<b>できる</b>とは限らないよ。  <br/>
You cannot always have your own way.

## Discussion

### 1 — Amatuka

Lit : Not limited to being [...]

### 2 — Miki

高い物_が_ is more natural in context.

### 3 — yookoso

Is this like ...というわけではない <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b>？

### 4 — Miki

Yes. とは限らない can be reaplced by (という）わけではない.

### 5 — LittleFish

Frequently appears with 必ずしも.<br/>
<br/>
(Question: Could this be applied to nouns in this way: (これは鉛筆として使うとは限らない、マーカーとしても使えますよ。)? It isn't limited to the function of a pencil, it can also (perform the function of) a marker.<br/>
Something that isn't limited to one single use or function.)

### 6 — Miki

It's grammatically correct. 鉛筆 used also as マーカー…. If you mean a mechanical pencil, it should be シャーペン.<br/>
