## Meaning

depending on

depending on the weather, we'll go

## Notes

### 1 — shittakaburi

ex<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=shidaide#">#</a></b> 1624 The words, or predicate, "to go" should perhaps be in parenthesis as the decision could be of any nature (e.g. the decision to:  plant seed,  play outdoors etc. )
