## Meaning

due to

due to construction, the train is late.

## Notes

### 1 — khongbietboi

[名]＋につき

### 2 — rubyhatchet

＊This grammar is used in formal sentences, such as those appearing on bulletins, notices, etc.<br/>
<br/>
