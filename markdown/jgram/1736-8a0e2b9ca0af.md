## Meaning

in the middle of something 何か始めたけれど、途中で止めてしまいました。完成はまだです。

these files are yet to be completed.

## Notes

### 1 — ashishjoshi

[v(-masu)+kake] is a pattern used to show that something was stopped in the middle or before the actual completion.
