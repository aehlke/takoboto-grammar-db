## Meaning

Whether X or Y; A structure that means "no matter which one is the case"

No matter if one is famous or nameless, life is short.

## Examples

### 7960 — LR

<a href="http://www.csse.monash.edu.au/~jwb/cgi-bin/wwwjdic.cgi?9MHJ男性であれ、女性">
				男性<strong>であれ</strong>、女性<strong>であれ</strong>、人間としての権利は同じはずだ。</a> <br/>
<strong>Whether </strong>
		one is a man or a woman, one's rights as a human being should be the same.

### 7961 — LR

<a href="http://www.csse.monash.edu.au/~jwb/cgi-bin/wwwjdic.cgi?9MHJ有名であれ、無名">
				有名<strong>であれ</strong>、無名<strong>であれ、人生は短い。 </strong></a> <br/>
<strong>No matter </strong>
		if one is famous or nameless, life is short.

### 7962 — LR

<a href="http://www.csse.monash.edu.au/~jwb/cgi-bin/wwwjdic.cgi?9MHJ注射であれ飲み薬">
				注射<strong>であれ</strong>飲み薬<strong>であれ</strong>、良く効く薬はかえって副作用が心配だ</a> <br/>
<strong>Whether </strong>
		it's an injection or oral medecine, I worry that drugs that work well will have adverse side-effects.

### 7963 — LR

<a href="http://www.csse.monash.edu.au/~jwb/cgi-bin/wwwjdic.cgi?9MHJ父は菓子であれ、果物">
				父は菓子<strong>であれ</strong>、果物<strong>であれ</strong>、甘い者は一切口にしない。</a> <br/>
		My father won't eat any anything sweet, 
		<strong>whether </strong>
		it be sweets or fruit.

### 7964 — LR

<a href="http://www.csse.monash.edu.au/~jwb/cgi-bin/wwwjdic.cgi?9MHJ正社員であれ、パート">
				正社員<strong>であれ</strong>、パート<strong>であれ</strong>、仕事に対する責任は変わりません。</a> <br/>
<strong>Whether </strong>
		a full time employee or a part-timer, your duty to your job is the same.

### 7965 — LR

		<strong>whether it be </strong>
		philosophy, linguistics or anthropology
