## Meaning

to the extent of; to the extent that  ~; (not as) ~ as ~ ; about \[REL. bakari; kurai\]  私はレンズほど強くありません。

(Lit, I'm not strong as Lenz's extent. (=I'm not as strong as Lenz))

## Notes

### 1 — lenzras

1. When hodo is preceded by a noun, the predicate must be negative. The following sentence is ungrammatical.<br/>
<br/>
*私は太郎ほどつよい。<br/>
(I am as strong as Taro.)<br/>
<br/>
<br/>
If a sentence or a demonstrative modifies hodo, however, the predicate of the main clause can be either affirmative or negative.<br/>
<br/>
その仕事はそれほど易しいんですか。<br/>
Is that job that easy (lit. easy to that extent)?<br/>
<br/>
<br/>
When hodo is used with a quantifier, it means 'about'. Example:<br/>
<br/>
ビールを三本ほど飲みました。<br/>
(I drank about three bottles of beer.)<br/>
