## Meaning

'ha (wa)' used to indicate contrast

I don't eat /meat/ (but I do eat fish).

## Discussion

### 1 — Amatuka

The 'normal' negative sentence would be 私は肉を食べない。'I don't eat fish'.<br/>
With the object marker を overridden by は this implies that not eating meat is in contrast to eating something else (for example fish).<br/>
<b>Note:</b> When Japanese refer to meat (肉) they generally would not understand that as including fish (魚).

### 2 — Amatuka

This contrastative は can fit in places that you might not expect.  Take <b>生きている '(she's) alive.'</b> vs. <b>生きてはいる '(she's) alive (but not well).'</b>.
