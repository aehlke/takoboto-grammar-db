## Meaning

over; span(ning) の間(の); に広がって; の間続いて

His interests span many fields.

## Notes

### 1 — rubyhatchet

＊This grammar can be used to describe time, distance, or extent/scope.<br/>
<br/>
FORMATION:<br/>
Ｎ + にわって・にわたり<br/>
Ｎ1 + にわたる + Ｎ2

### 2 — sureshnihon

時間的，空間的にその範囲全体に広がっていることをあらわす。

### 17 — yuuhi2525

Shidareさん。<br/>
<br/>
The reason why it is にわたる is because it defines what the noun is.<br/>
2年にわたる新しい道路の建設工事がやっと終わった<br/>
This can be separated like this:<br/>
建設工事 = Construction work<br/>
道路の建設工事 = Construction work of the highway<br/>
新しい道路の建設工事 = Construction work of the new highway<br/>
2年にわたる新しい道路の建設工事 = Construction work of the new highway that spanned 2 years<br/>
<br/>
So the real noun here is 道路の建設工事, but 「2年にわたる新しい」defines some properties to the noun as well. This grammar is very common when you describe something about a noun. Other examples may be:<br/>
<br/>
「これはネズミを食べる猫です」<br/>
This is a cat that eats mice.<br/>
<br/>
You need to have the verb in it's dictionary form to define something about the noun that comes after ^^
