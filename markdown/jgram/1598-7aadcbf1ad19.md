## Meaning

Politely asking someone to convey a message.

Could you please tell Anthony that i will phone him later.

## Notes

### 1 — Overfiend

Formation:<br/>
(Person)に(message plain form)と伝えていただけませんか。<br/>
例えば：すみませんが、渡辺さんに明日のパーティーは６時からだと伝えていただけませんか。<br/>
英語：Excuse me but, could you tell Watanabe that the party tomorrow will start from 6.
