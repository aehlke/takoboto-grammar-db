## Meaning

there's a chance that, it could be that

There's a chance I might be transfered overseas in the future, so I'm going to learn English.

## Notes

### 1 — leviathon

Informal clause + とは限らない/とも限らない<br/>
<br/>
With 〜とは限らない、what comes before is not 100% guaranteed.<br/>
<br/>
With 〜ないとも限らない what comes before it is not unlikely to happen (i.e. there is a possibility).<br/>
<br/>
は or も? - も is for emphasis.
