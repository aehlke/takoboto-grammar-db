## Meaning

not only, but also

not only smokes, but also drinks

## Notes

### 1 — Nashira

<b>1.いろいろな場合がある。<br/>
<br/>
2.二つのことが同時に備わっている。</b>

### 9 — 赤毛

I intent to answer to Tigertさん. About the conditional form (条件形), we learned that this form apply to more words than verbs in japanese:<br/>
Vウ-&gt;Vエバ:     する-&gt;すれば<br/>
Aイ-&gt;Aケレバ：  高い-&gt;高ければ<br/>
Aナ-&gt;Aナラ:     嫌[な]-&gt;嫌なら<br/>
N-&gt;Nナラ:       雨-&gt;雨なら<br/>
Somewhere も〜ば〜も and も〜なら〜も don't seem different.
