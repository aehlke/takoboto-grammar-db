## Meaning

just like, alike to, similar to

Kanojo wa marude tori no you ni tonde imasu = she's flying just like a bird\!  (tonde imasu= flying, tobimasu- fly)

## Notes

### 1 — pandahart

~のような (〜の様な), usually written in kana, means "like, similar to"; compare to ~ようだ <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> meaning "seeming to be, appearing to be" and usually written at sentence-end, and 〜ように <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b>* meaning "in order to (e.g. meet goal), so that".<br/>
*〜ように <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> at the end of a sentence indicates hope, wish, request or mild command.
