## Discussion

### 1 — Amatuka

The 'to' indicated by made can be time like (40 days) space like (Kyushu) or more metaphorical ([lit: up to raining])

### 2 — Amatuka

Note there is some difference between までに and まで.  <br/>
まで : "I stayed up till 10pm" <br/>
１０時まで起きてた。<br/>
[You actually went to bed at 10pm]<br/>
までに : "I had to get back by 10pm" <br/>
１０時までに帰らなくっちゃ<br/>
[You could come back earlier, but not later]<br/>
(Hmm, hope that last example is OK)
