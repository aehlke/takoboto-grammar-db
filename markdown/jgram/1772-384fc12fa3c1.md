## Meaning

must \_\_\_\_; really should \_\_\_\_\_; it won't do if you don't \_\_\_\_.

You must use the large envelope.

## Notes

### 1 — american57

Use conditional negative verb "ba" form + "narimasen" (or "naranai" for informal/written).  The Japanese is a double negative: "It will not do if you do not _____." Tends to be translated as "must _____" but is maybe not always that strong.  A relative of "-te ha ikemasen."<br/>
<br/>
手袋を持ってこなければならない。<br/>
Tebukuro wo motte konakereba naranai.  You must bring gloves.<br/>
