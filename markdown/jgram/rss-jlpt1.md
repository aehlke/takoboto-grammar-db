## まだしも (madashimo)

Publication date as displayed: Wed, 20 Nov 2019 01:04:01 PST

[Original entry](http://www.jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=madashimo&date=2019-11-20)

<span>まだしも (madashimo)</span><br/><br/>
       <span><b>Meaning</b>: would be much better, would be acceptable </span><br/>
    <strong>Example</strong>: <i>If it was next month that would be better, but it isn't going to happen until next year.</i><br/>
<br/>

<li><a>一度ならまだしもここまで六回その言葉を間違えって書いた。   [ex #6242]</a></li>
Once would be acceptable, but you've misspelled that word six times now. <strong> </strong> <br/>
<li><a>この方がまだしもあれよりよい。
   [ex #6245]</a></li>
This would be better than that. <strong> </strong> <br/>
<li><a>こんなひどい目に合うよりまだしも死んだほうがよい。
   [ex #6246]</a></li>
I would rather die than have such a terrible experience. <strong> </strong> <br/>
<li><a>すうじつならまだしも１０日も滞納している。    [ex #6247]</a></li>
A few days would have been excusable but you are in arrears for 10 days. <strong> </strong> <br/>
