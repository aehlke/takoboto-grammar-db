## Meaning

to, from (indirect object marker)

I was given this by my mother.

### 7 — yari

Isn't the verb in <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=ni-2#">#</a></b> in passive form? If that's the case, then に denotes the agent (I think that's the correct English term) of the action. In other words, the one acting/subject.<br/>
<br/>
That's why the English translation is in passive form also. I think.
