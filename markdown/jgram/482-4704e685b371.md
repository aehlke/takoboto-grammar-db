## Meaning

(thing) you know? isn't it?

its a fun thing, exchanging mail on mobile phones, isn't it.

## Notes

### 1 — khongbietboi

A　show admiration ; grief ; a heart felt feeling<br/>
B　express something as common sense　Same as A<br/>
C　suggest something ; give a light command<br/>
D　remember a past happening or occurrence  <br/>

### 2 — 赤毛

C' Give a command. See [ex 6690]<br/>
E  Ideal to be reached (or not) [ex 6691]+[ex 6692]<br/>
F  Explain circumstancies, reason or signification [ex 6693] same as A?<br/>
F' When explaination is suspicious, だ-&gt;らしい [ex 6694]<br/>
F" When in children conversation, ものだ-&gt;だもの [ex 6695]<br/>

### 10 — 誠

〜ものだ：1. 本当に〜だなあ　　（感慨）an emotional exclamations, expression sort of " ..oh..is (really) so...".<br/>
<br/>
          2. 過去の回想　「〜たものだ」の形で使われる  something nostalgic<br/>
<br/>
          3. 〜が一般的な常識だ　（人に対して注意する時によく使われる）<br/>
         "It is generally known that......"<br/>
         "It is common sense that ....(should).."<br/>
         "It is known that ...."<br/>
<br/>
ex<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=monoda-2#">#</a></b> means the above category 3 of common sense. <br/>
 "People would generally greet 'Good Morning' when they meet others in the morning" <br/>
<br/>
While ex<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=monoda-2#">#</a></b> has the meaning of category 1 which is an exclamation of strong feelings, 楽しいものだな、楽しいものですね, " oh... is really fun!" <br/>
<br/>
For the rest of the examples,  I cannot figure out yet the usage of ものです.... <br/>
<br/>
So which category does this entry belongs to?
