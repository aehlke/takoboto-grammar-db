## Notes

### 1 — dc

<b><a href="http://jgram.org:80/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=karaniha">karaniha</a></b> because, since<br/>

I have to do it because I promised.<br/>
<br/>

### 2 — dc

verb ~ <b><a href="http://jgram.org:80/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=tara">tara</a></b><br/>
if, when...<br/>
駅に着いたら、、、<br/>
when you get to the station...<br/>
<br/>
verb ~ <b><a href="http://jgram.org:80/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=ba">ba</a></b> <br/>
if...<br/>
駅に来れば、、、<br/>
if you come to the station...<br/>
<br/>
<b><a href="http://jgram.org:80/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=node">node</a></b><br/>
most frequent basic "so" connective.<br/>
駅に来たので、、、<br/>
because I came to the station...<br/>
<br/>
<b><a href="http://jgram.org:80/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=ueha">ueha</a></b> &lt;b&gt;Because of A, B must be done&lt;/b&gt;<br/>
used for duty, obligation<br/>
約束した上は、行かなければなりません<br/>
Because I promised, I have to go<br/>
<br/>
<b><a href="http://jgram.org:80/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=karaniha">karaniha</a></b><br/>
"since" but used for heavy duties<br/>
 女として生まれた からには  綺麗でいたい<br/>
since I was born a woman, I want to be pretty<br/>
<br/>
verb ~ <b><a href="http://jgram.org:80/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=teirai">teirai</a></b><br/>
implies timeline connection<br/>
日本に来て以来<br/>
since coming to japan...
