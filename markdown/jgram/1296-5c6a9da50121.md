## Meaning

leads to, all the way to, as far as

this thinking will lead us to glory

## Notes

### 1 — dc

noun + に至る<br/>
　東京に至るまで as far as tokyo<br/>
<br/>
Vdic + に至る<br/>
<br/>
often used with まで：until
