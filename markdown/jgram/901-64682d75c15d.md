## Meaning

Since; Take the opportunity afforded by 〜 to 〜

Since I was moving, I also took the chance to buy new furniture.

## Notes

### 1 — dc

see <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> for comparisons

### 2 — dc

used when a major event created an opportunity for something else at the same time. <br/>
"on moving to paris, i decided to learn french".<br/>
<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> and other phrases are a more casual "at the same time as A, I did B".

### 3 — rubyhatchet

FORMATION:<br/>
Ｎ + をきっかけに・きっかけとして・きっかけにして<br/>
<br/>
(＊Note: you always need を after the noun.)<br/>

### 4 — ani3000

since he was out, she took the opportunity to hide it.
