## Meaning

as soon as; depending on

As soon as i get to India, I will call you.

## Notes

### 1 — rubyhatchet

次第: as soon as; if 〜, then immediately 〜.<br/>
This grammar implies a planned sequence of actions. It does NOT imply a surprising result. As a result, this grammar cannot be used with the past tense.<br/>
<br/>
Ｖ(ます form MINUS ます) + 次第<br/>
<br/>
次第で(は) or 次第だ: depends on; is decided by; changes depending on.<br/>
<br/>
Ｎ + 次第だ・次第で(は)

### 2 — brij\_shukla2000

This is a conjuction/noun which is used to express that an action indicated by a preceding verb or an action implemented by a preceding noun is a prerequisite for another action.<br/>
<br/>
インドに着き次第を電話差し上げます。<br/>
As soon as I get to india, I will call you.
