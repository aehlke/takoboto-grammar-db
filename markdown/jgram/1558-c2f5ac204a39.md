## Meaning

it is even more unlikely that 〜

even adults also being eluded of this matter,there is no way/it is even more unlikely that children knows such a thing

## Examples

### 5844 — 誠

<a href="http://www.csse.monash.edu.au/~jwb/cgi-bin/wwwjdic.cgi?9MHJ大人でさえ知らない事は、 まして子供は知っている">
				大人でさえ知らない事は、 <strong>まして</strong>子供は知っている<strong>はずがない</strong>。</a> <br/>
<strong>It is even more unlikely/it is impossible </strong>
		for children to know things that even adults don't know.

### 5979 — 誠

<a href="http://www.csse.monash.edu.au/~jwb/cgi-bin/wwwjdic.cgi?9MHJ大人でさえ知らないし、まして子供は知っている">
				大人でさえ知らないし、<strong>まして</strong>子供は知っている<strong>はずがない</strong>。</a> <br/>
		Even adutls also have no idea about it, 
		<strong>it is even more unlikely for/it is impossible for/let alone of </strong>
		children to know about it.

## Discussion

### 1 — bamboo4

In <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=mashite+%7E+hazuganai#">#</a></b>, 大人でさえ知らない事は should be changed to read: 大人でさえ知らないし

### 2 — nanashi

The English is wonky.  How about "It is impossible for children to know about this matter that even adults don't know."?  For bamboo4's suggestion: "Even adults don't know about this thing, so it's impossible that children would know it."
