## Discussion

### 1 — Dahak

Is there any difference between そのとき and Verb + ときに?   "At the time" is just short for "At the time when."

### 2 — dc

yes, they seem to be.<br/>
<br/>
at the time when (verb)<br/>
at that time...<br/>
<br/>
私が買ったときに、高いと思った、、<br/>
そのときは高かったけど<br/>
<br/>
at the time when i bought it, i thot it was expensive<br/>
at that time it was expensive<br/>

### 3 — Dahak

そのときは高いと思った?<br/>
At that time, I thought it was expensive.<br/>
<br/>
Basically use そのとき when you don't link the time (とき) to an action (verb).<br/>
<br/>
Correct?

### 4 — dc

seems like it. その時 is a standalone.　<br/>
当時 = at the designated time (more formal)
