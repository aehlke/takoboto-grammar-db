## Discussion

### 1 — francis

Think this is also like "nanode" with a noun preceding. Maybe more like due to , its a little stiffer than node.

### 2 — bamboo4

につき or について is also used for "regarding" or "concerning" such as 山田さんにつき（について）何かご存じですか(Do you know anything about Mr. Yamada?).<br/>
