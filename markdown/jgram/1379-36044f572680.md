## Meaning

Began/Begun, Started だす

The traffic started moving

## Notes

### 1 — sutiibu

車が(動き)出した - kurumaga(ugoki = movement)dashita = the car started moving - verb + dashita = started to

### 6 — KWhazit

There's a discussion of the difference between verb+出す (dasu) and verb+始める (hajimeru) over in the comments on <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b>. The consensus is that verb+出す is more of a sudden or explosive starting (like busrting into tears), while verb+始める is just simply starting.<br/>
<br/>
Note also that this use of 出す is NOT the same as the standalone verb 出す, which means to take something from a more enclosed/private place or situation and put it in a more open/public one. 出す only means "start to do" when it's tacked onto another verb stem... and even then it may still have the above meaning:<br/>
取り出す - take (an object) out (of a container)<br/>
追い出す - chase (someone) out
