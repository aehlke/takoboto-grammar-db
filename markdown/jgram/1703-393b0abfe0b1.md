## Meaning

When it comes to (special situation), Naturally, (X) follows

When it comes to the cherry blossom season, the park is packed with cherry blossom viewers

## Notes

### 1 — LR

<b>Ref <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=tomonaruto#">#</a></b></b> Kanzen Master Level 1 - p17 - no.15

### 2 — LR

<b>Ref <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=tomonaruto#">#</a></b></b> A Dictionary of Advanced Japanese Grammar p401

### 3 — LR

(X) + tomonaruto is used when a situation or thing expressed by (X) is perceived as special. This meaning comes from the use of the particle mo as in:<br/>
a. 夜も更けていた (It was late at night)<br/>
b. 夏も終わってしまった (Summer is over now)<br/>
<br/>
Note that the speaker feels special about yoru "night" in a. or natsu "summer" in b.<br/>
<br/>
mo can be omitted from tomonaruto but it takes away the special significance<br/>
<br/>
(Advanced Grammar Dictionary, p403)
