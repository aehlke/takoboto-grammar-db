## Meaning

Vte,  (Definitely, completely \[often to ones regret\] do V)

Now you've gone and broke it.

## Discussion

### 1 — Amatuka

Formed from Vte + shimau.<br/>
<br/>
Note that shimau conjugates as a normal godan verb.<br/>
<br/>
Vte = -te form verb (死ぬ -&gt; 死んで)

### 2 — Amatuka

Note that the Vte+shimau can be shortened in one of two ways depending on the kana used for the 'te' form verb.<br/>
<br/>
e.g. 死んでしまう → 死んじゃう<br/>
凍えてしまう → 凍えちゃう

### 3 — Amatuka

Not <b>always</b> used in negative nuance.

### 4 — tarun rana

this is very good
