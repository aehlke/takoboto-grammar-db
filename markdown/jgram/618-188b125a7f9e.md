## Meaning

not necessarily

Big men are not necessarily strong men

## Notes

### 1 — dc

This is usually used in the patterns:<br/>
<br/>
(a) does not neccesarily mean (b)<br/>
<br/>
(big) kanarazushimo (strong) <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b><br/>
(big) kanarazushimo (strong) to iu <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> de ha nai<br/>
(big) kanarazushimo (strong) <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> de ha nai<br/>
<br/>
(big) does not neccesarily mean (strong)
