## Meaning

more....more ...ば...ほど

Mountains are more higher temperature is more low.

## Examples

### 6551 — makara

<a href="http://www.csse.monash.edu.au/~jwb/cgi-bin/wwwjdic.cgi?9MHJ山は高くなるにつれて、気温が下がる。">
				山は高くなる<strong>につれて</strong>、気温が下がる。<strong></strong></a> <br/>
<strong>As </strong>
		mountains get higher, the temperature gets lower.
