## Meaning

completely; until the end 完全に〜する; 最後まで〜する

Parents of children who have absolutely no interest in studying are completely at a loss.

## Notes

### 1 — rubyhatchet

＊This is a strong expression.<br/>
<br/>
FORMATION:<br/>
Ｖ(minus ます) + ぬく

### 2 — deeana

- you do something completely/ till the end, but it takes great pain, effort, sacrifice to comlplete the action  ｛→せっかく｝<br/>
<br/>
ex：最後まで走りぬく。Run to the　end (with great effort). <br/>
<br/>
