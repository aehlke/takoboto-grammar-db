## Discussion

### 1 — Nick

理由（りゆう）の如何（いかん）によらず<br/>
This means "no matter what reason there is."<br/>
Usually, Japanese people say "理由が何であろうとも" instead.

### 2 — Miki

"理由が何であろうとも" would be used especially in speaking. In written form, "いかんにもよらず" is often seen.

### 3 — samboki

A better translation for <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=ikanniyorazu#">#</a></b> is "The number of municipalities where you can take the employment test regardless of nationalty is increasing.

### 4 — LR

@nikonniko Same meaning, different usage - as with a LOT of grammar. 名詞＋を問わず is a straight forward easy to use phrase, appropriate for most language situations.  <br/>
名詞＋の＋いかんを問わずis more formal, appropriate for written language, archaic?
