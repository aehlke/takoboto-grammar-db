## Meaning

not really

彼女はあまりきれいじゃない。　She isn't very pretty.

## Notes

### 1 — pingpongpaddle

noun + amari + neg adj. + <b><a href="http://jgram.org:80/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=desu">desu</a></b><br/>
<br/>
私は　あまり　つよくない　です。(watashiha amari tsuyokunai desu) I'm not that strong.
