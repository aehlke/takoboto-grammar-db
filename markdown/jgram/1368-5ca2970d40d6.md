## Meaning

(on  when) doing, if

after you see ...

## Notes

### 1 — tigert

S1と　S2　is a causative sentence connective that implies that because of S1 naturally S2 was the result and has an unintentional or undesired nuance to it<br/>
<br/>
For example：<br/>
この間、友達と一緒にキムチの食べほうだいをしたと　おなかが　痛くなりました.<br/>
<br/>
The other day, my friend(s) and I had an "all you can eat" kimchi party and as a natural result (of eating some much kimchi) my stomach started to hurt.<br/>

### 2 — pedro\_cristian

と　is preceded by the dictionary form.

## Discussion

### 1 — Amatuka

First example's /literal/ translation is "_On_ seeing `Romeo and Juliet' one is certain to be moved to tears."

### 2 — Miki

I think the 3rd exmpale looks different from others though it is still（接助）活用語の終止形に接続する。<br/>
This 「と」seems to imply remarks following.<br/>
「はっきり言うと、それは不可能だ」「この場合ですと、お値段が高くなります」「ここですと、安いテレビが買えます。」
