### 2 — blabby

The only time I have seen this grammar is on signs. My teacher says it is an old fashioned way of saying 〜してはいけない.  Obviously it is the negative form of 〜べき.<br/>

### 3 — Miki

This is the negative form of べし　;) (べから・べく（べかり）・べし・べき（べかる）・べけれ・○)<br/>
べきではない would be used in speaking.

### 4 — quendidil

### 5 — quendidil

### 6 — jimmyseal

&gt;&gt; quendidil<br/>
The dictionary form (終止形) would be べし, but in MJ, べし has been replaced by べきだ.  <br/>
So if we're talking about MJ, べからず is the negative of べきだ, but in CJ, it would be べし.
