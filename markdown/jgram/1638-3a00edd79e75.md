## Meaning

Not only ないだけでなく

He is not only talkative in the class moreover he disburbed other students

## Examples

### 6550 — makara

				彼は会社へ来ても仕事をし<strong>ないばかりか</strong><strong></strong>女性社員としゃべってばかりいる。</a> <br/>
		He is 
		<strong>not only </strong>
		come for not working moreover he is talking with other  ladies.
