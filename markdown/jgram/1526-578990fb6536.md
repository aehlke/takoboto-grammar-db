## Meaning

As soon as

As soon as the door opened, 8 people burst out of the car.

## Notes

### 1 — blabby

Verb(辞書形) + なり<br/>
<br/>
[AなりB] - B is often an unexpected or unusual thing.

## Discussion

### 1 — blabby

My book says that this is for a strange or unexpected event.<br/>
<br/>
X スイッチを入れるなり電気がついた。<br/>
O スイッチを入れると電気がついた。<br/>
O スイッチを入れるなり爆発が起きた。<br/>
<br/>
However, my book says it is only used for Verb(辞書形)+なり, but I have sometimes seen cases of Verb(た形)+なり from other sources. Does anyone know which is correct?

### 2 — Miki

Verb(た形)+なり  is used in a different way. It implies one movement is continuing. 〜したまま。<br/>
会社を出たなり、いつまでも帰らない。He left the office and hasn't come back.<br/>
彼女を見つめたなり、しばらくの間微動だにしなかった。He stared at her and didn't move an inch for a while.<br/>
You can find better translations.
