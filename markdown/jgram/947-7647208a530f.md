## Meaning

without thinking,  despite myself

oops, i said too much\!

## Notes

### 1 — KotatsuSama

Also means, just (now), or recently.<br/>

## Discussion

### 1 — Amatuka

Inadvertantly, unintentionally, etc.

### 2 — huess

I always thought that was ついに？ Am I wrong or can you drop the に？

### 3 — Miki

ついに is different from つい.<br/>
ついに can be replaced with やっと at last<br/>
ついに買っちゃった！<br/>
Finally, I got it!<br/>

### 4 — hiro

「つい」and「ついに」is different. <br/>
<br/>
「つい」means... "regretfully"<br/>
for example, you go shopping, you find a new PC. you already have a PC, but you buy the spanky new one anyway. then, you say<br/>
「つい、買った。」<br/>
in fact, you don't need that PC. <br/>
<br/>
「ついに」means..."at last"<br/>
you wanted a PC for a long time, and eventually you get one. then, you say,<br/>
「ついに買った。」<br/>
you really need that PC.<br/>
<br/>
:( tsui = ukkari<br/>
:) tsuini = yatto

### 5 — dc

ex1273 seems different from the others?

### 6 — Miki

Yes. The つい means すぐ。This emphases something happens very close in time/distance.<br/>
つい２、３時間前に as recently as a few hours <br/>
ついそこまで休憩に行ってきました。I went right over there for break.<br/>

### 7 — ashishjoshi

'tsui',according to me is used when you get indulged in your inner feelings so much that you forget what is going around.<br/>
ex-夢中で、つい大きい声で歌ってしまって、はずかしかった。
