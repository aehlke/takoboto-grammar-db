## Meaning

nothing but; merely; none other than まさに〜だ・である

War is nothing but death.

## Notes

### 1 — rubyhatchet

＊This is an expression of a strong conclusion or decision. It can only be used with nouns.<br/>
<br/>
＊ほかならぬ means "very important" or "none other." For example:<br/>
ほかならぬあなたの頼みだから、聞かないわけにはいきません。<br/>
"I can't help but listen since it's a request from none other than you."<br/>
<br/>
FORMATION:<br/>
Ｎ + にほかならない<br/>
ほかならぬ + Ｎ

### 2 — missshapoooo

PLAIN FORMS (Verb, i/na Adjectives, Noun) + にほかならない<br/>
But in case of Noun an na-Adjective だ　is not added<br/>
There are also Noun/na-Adjective + である<br/>
when expressing reason から　can also be added
