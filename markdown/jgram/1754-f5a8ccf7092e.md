## Meaning

even though ~ try / want to ~; although ~ be thinking of doing; A conjunctive phrase that indicates concession: "even though s.o. tries to do s.t., he/she cannot do it for some reason" しようとしてもできない; したいのにできない

I have a terrible headache, and I cannot get up even though I want to
