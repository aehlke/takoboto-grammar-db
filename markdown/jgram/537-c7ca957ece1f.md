## Meaning

in one's own way,  one's own style

she has her own way of doing it

### 5 — bamboo4

なりに does not always mean "one's own way" as shown by my example.

### 6 — bamboo4

なり could mean "accroding to" as shown in my examples, and it also could mean "even though not entirely satisfactory, but corresponding to the capability that one has" that could be equal to "one's own way" in a broad sense. However, don't forget that there is this modesty that is at the root of なりに.<br/>
