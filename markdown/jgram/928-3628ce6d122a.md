## Meaning

instead of / in place of

I will go instead of you

## Notes

### 1 — dc

<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> can be used for all the meanings below<br/>
<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> is mainly for "instead"<br/>
<br/>
- on behalf of<br/>
I will go on your behalf<br/>
<br/>
- instead of<br/>
I want coffee instead of tea<br/>
<br/>
- to make up for<br/>
I am going to enjoy myself in the daytime, but to make up for it I intend to study at night.<br/>
<br/>
<br/>

### 2 — dc

Usage:<br/>
<br/>
noun + no+kawarini<br/>
　先生の代わりに<br/>
　sensei no kawarini<br/>
<br/>
noun+ ni kawatte<br/>
　先生に代わって<br/>
<br/>
verb-masu + kawarini<br/>
　遊ぶ代わりに<br/>
　asobu kawarini<br/>
<br/>
adj+ kawarini<br/>
　高い代わりに<br/>
　takai kawarini
