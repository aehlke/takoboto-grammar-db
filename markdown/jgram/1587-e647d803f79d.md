### 1 — paulblay

### 2 — potaro

The listing for 元い (もとい) in my electronic dictionary is a link to 元へ (もとへ), so 元い  is probably a corrupted/alternate form of 元へ, which literally means "back to the beginning".  It's probably best to think of this phrase as meaning "take back", as in "Wednesday... no, I take it back -- Thursday".
