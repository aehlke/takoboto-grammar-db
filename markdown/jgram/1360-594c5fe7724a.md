## Meaning

as; in proportion to; with とともに; に伴って; に従って; と一緒に

As I ate, my headache improved.

## Notes

### 1 — dc

V-dic + 〜<br/>
食べるにつれて　 along with eating<br/>
<br/>
Noun + 〜<br/>
増加につれて　　along with the increase

### 2 — dc

<br/>
連れて行く means to take someone with (you)<br/>
<br/>
私は友達を連れて行きました<br/>
I took my friend along<br/>
<br/>
Will you take us for a drive next Sunday?<br/>
今度の日曜日にドライブにつれていってくれませんか。<br/>

### 3 — rubyhatchet

＊につれ(て) is a phrase used to indicate that a change occurs in accordance with another simultaneous change. Because of this, the verbs in the につれ(て) clause and the main clause have to be verbs of change or of a process, such as 'become proficient' and 'increase.'<br/>
<br/>
＊When と共に(とともに) means 'as,' it can be rephrased as につれて, に伴って(にともなって), or に従って(にしたがって).<br/>
EXAMPLE: 景気の回復[と共に/につれて/に伴って/に従って]失業率が下がってきた。(As business recovers, the unemployment rate is coming down.)<br/>
<br/>
＊The difference between Ｖ(inf・nonpast)にしたがって and Ｖ(inf・nonpast)につれて is that the former is more of a written style and the latter is more of a spoken style.<br/>
<br/>
FORMATION:<br/>
Ｖ(inf・nonpast) + につれて<br/>
Ｎ + につれて
