## Meaning

but

I'd like to make it easier to use but still not use frames.

## Notes

### 1 — Sakuratree

でも can never appear in the middle of a sentence like けど or other words meaning 'but.'<br/>
<br/>
You can say:<br/>
<br/>
友達の家に行くつもりでした。でも、彼女は病気です。<br/>
I was planning on going to my friend's house. But, she's sick.

### 7 — Miki

Both are ok as Japanese.<br/>
お茶でもいかがですか。is a polite way to ask tea or something. Also I heard this is used by a guy to start a converstaion with a girl. <br/>
お茶でもいい？ is a casual way to suggest. dc's understanding is ok.

### 8 — 誠

お茶でもいかがですか。is a polite form of asking "would you have some green tea?". Here でも is used to indicate that " the are other things,equally well to be offered to listener, but the offeror just make a single choice (which is お茶) out of the other offerings, the listerner can make other choices if he/she didn't want green tea", <br/>
If it is お茶でいいですか , it indicates that " there is only green-tea to be offered, and if the listerner doesn't want then the offeror doesn't have any other to offer"
