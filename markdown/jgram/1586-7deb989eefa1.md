## Meaning

finally

finally we meet

## Notes

### 1 — dc

つい <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b><br/>
you may need to be a little careful to use つい as it means that you did something without thinking too much.<br/>
       But sometimes it is used in a good way to express what one says is a gut feeling.<br/>
       Surely つい結婚した　is not good.<br/>
<br/>
結局  <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b><br/>
often implies that a negative thing follows afterwards.<br/>
<br/>
やっと <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b><br/>
is not always used with positive feelings.<br/>
Sometimes it is used to show an innuendo.<br/>
Like, やっと来た　= you are way too late.<br/>
