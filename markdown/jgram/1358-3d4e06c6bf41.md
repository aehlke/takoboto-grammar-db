## Meaning

can't help feeling

I can't help feeling sorry for them

## Notes

### 1 — 誠

怒りや悲しみなどの気持を抑えることができない

## Discussion

### 1 — dc

is this the same as <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b>

### 2 — Miki

Tenaranaiis not the same as 禁じえない. 抑えられない can be the similar to 禁じえない. <br/>
禁じえない would be used when you talk about feelings.　同情を抑えられない。

### 3 — blabby

I think the nuance is more towards "(I) can't control/supress".<br/>
It is often used with 怒り,同情,驚き,失望,涙,笑い

### 4 — yookoso

Is this just the verb 禁じる + 得る <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b>? If so, should it have it's own entry when <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> already exists?

### 5 — 誠

yookoso, you are right. 禁じる means 抑える or " to suppress", while 得ない means cannot or できない。禁じ得ない　literally means 禁じることができない／抑えることができない or "unable to supress", but this grammar always used together with feelings such as 同情、驚き、失望、涙,....
