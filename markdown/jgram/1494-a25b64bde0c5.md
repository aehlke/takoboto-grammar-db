## Meaning

(just) because of

only because he was a scholar, he could understand

## Notes

### 1 — dc

<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> is a connector meaning because. <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> adds emphasis.<br/>
<br/>
科学者だから、分かりました<br/>
He is a scholar, so he understood<br/>
<br/>
科学者だからこそ、分かりました<br/>
(only because) he is a scholar, he was able to understand

## Discussion

### 1 — srobertson

I think the english explanation for this grammar point should be "especially because of..." to avoid confusion with <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b>, which has a stronger sense of highlighting an exclusive reason.

### 2 — srobertson

Note that <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> is not usually (cannot be?) used to give a negative emphasis. You would not say that you *didn't* do something especially because of a particular reason. <br/>
<br/>
(X) 勉強しなかったからこそ、試験に落ちたんだよ。<br/>

### 3 — srobertson

One other thing - I think this entry should be changed to "karakoso", since the "da" copula is used only connect karakoso with a noun or noun adjective (or to refer back to a previous sentence, as in examples <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=dakarakoso#"></a></b>, <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=dakarakoso#"></a></b> and <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=dakarakoso#"></a></b>). You can use karakoso after verbs and い adjectives as well.

### 4 — winterkoninkje

I think the important thing is that it's a direct form, whether perfective or imperfective (and whether positive or negative, as I recall) plus から. As opposed to a gerund form which gives the meaning of a sequence of events (AてからB, " after A, B (happened)") instead of giving causality.<br/>
<br/>
As for the こそ part, doesn't that have it's own page yet? Seems like it'd make sense to separate that from the rest of the pattern, or just make a note about "if using こそ it follows the から"
