### 2 — Auxabois

It looks to me that instead of an appendage to a verb like 食べなくてければなりません, せねばならない　becomes the verb. It appears all the exp. sentances have a suru verb minus suru with せねばならない added? So does this only work with suru verbs?

### 4 — expandover

here you go how this is used:<br/>
1- verb in the negative form drop the ない and add ねばならない<br/>
ex: 行かない drop ない and attach ねばならない to いか, so it becomes いかねばならない which means must go.<br/>
2-for せねばならない is added to しない which means do not do by changing し==&gt;せ and dropping ない <br/>
ex: 勉強せねばならない which means must study.
