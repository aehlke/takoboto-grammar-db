## Meaning

(I/he/she/....) can not stand.....

I can not stand the noise from ......

## Notes

### 1 — expandover

We can use kanawanai with い-adj by dropping the I at the end of the い-adj and use くて<br/>
For な-adj we drop な and use で　instead...
