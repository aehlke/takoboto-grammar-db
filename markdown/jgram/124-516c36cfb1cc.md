## Meaning

upon, after

I will reply upon careful consideration.

## Notes

### 1 — hadv

「Vta＋上で」have the meaning of <strong>upon/after</strong>. <br/>
<br/>
Ex: <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=uede#">#</a></b><br/>
よく考えた<strong>上で</strong>ご返事いたします。 <br/>
I will reply <strong>upon</strong> careful consideration.  <br/>
<br/>
But, 「Vru＋上で」have the meaning of <strong>when</strong><br/>
<br/>
Ex: <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=uede#">#</a></b> <br/>
<br/>
メールを使う<strong>うえで</strong>注意すべきマナーって？ <br/>
<br/>
[What is the] Etiquette to pay attention <strong>when</strong> using email?

### 2 — mervyn

<br/>
「N + の + 上で」also means <strong>upon/after</strong><br/>
<br/>
e.g.<br/>
内容を<strong>ご確認の上で</strong>、サインをお願いいたします。<br/>
Please sign <strong>after confirming</strong> the details.<br/>
<br/>

## Discussion

### 1 — Amatuka

Careful not to get じょうで mixed up with うえで because they look exactly the same with kanji.

### 2 — Amatuka

Second example has an implied なんですか at the end of the sentence.

### 3 — Amatuka

Oops. On second thoughts kill that -&gt; ueha 'see also'

### 4 — Amatuka

(Too darn many 上's to get confused)

### 5 — Snake

I finally saw joude when i was installing debian on my laptop ^_^ it was something like <br/>
’このベースシステムのじょうで、ネットワークホストインストールすべきです。’<br/>

### 6 — Snake

*の上で even

### 7 — your name

I often hear people at work use <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> as in "PC jyou" on the PC...

### 8 — Miki

As for 'このベースシステムのじょうで、ネットワークホストインストールすべきです。' is not natural for me. このベースシステムじょう（上）で would be better. I think when 上 is followed by noun, 上 would be read as 'じょう'.

### 9 — bamboo4

You means when 上 is "precceded" by noun.

### 10 — 誠

I think there is a need for の after the 名詞, before the 上で（うえで）, 名詞＋の上で<br/>
If it is verb　動詞 , then it is 動詞（た形）+<br/>
上で

### 11 — 誠

Sometimes, you can omit the で　：　上（で）

### 12 — 誠

PC 上　means " about/for/relating about the PC" same as ex<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=uede#">#</a></b> "upon/relating about the using of email"<br/>
Like ex<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=uede#">#</a></b>,  here 上で means　" 〜に関連して ", whereas the remaining 3 examples mean ”〜してから”

### 13 — bamboo4

A minor point, but in <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=uede#">#</a></b>, "the necessary events" should be "the necessary particulars".
