## Meaning

Like, As if; resembelence, analogy, similarity 実際はそうではないが、まるで〜のような／に (文語体)

I want to fly like a bird.

## Notes

### 1 — blabby

Noun + (の) + ごとき + Noun<br/>
Noun + ごとく + Adjective/Verb

### 2 — mochabean

Not to be confused with &lt;b&gt;ことごとく&lt;/b&gt;, which is an unrelated word meaning 'entirely':<br/>
<br/>
　ことごとく信じる。<br/>
　Believe something totally.

### 3 — LR

<b>Ref <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=gotoki,gotoku,gotoshi#">#</a></b></b> Kanzen Master Level 1 - p62 - no.53

### 4 — LR

<b>Ref <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=gotoki,gotoku,gotoshi#">#</a></b></b> A Dictionary of Advanced Japanese Grammar p103

### 5 — LR

Written, Formal

### 6 — LR

ごとし indicates similarity. It changes form depending on where it is in a sentence, it's nuance/grammatical function, and whether it is attached to a verb or a noun.

### 7 — LR

One of the functions of ごとし / ごとき / ごとく is to mark a simile.

### 8 — LR

When か is inserted, it adds the nuance of doubt on the part of the speaker.

### 9 — LR

Used almost exclusively in written language.

### 10 — LR

<b>Set Phrases</b><br/>
<br/>
<strong>上述のごとく</strong><br/>
As stated above<br/>
<br/>
<strong>当然のごとく</strong><br/>
As if it were a matter of course

### 11 — LR

<table><br/>
<tr><td>{NOUN} + <strong>のごとし</strong></td><td>[魚のごとし - s.o. is like a fish]</td></tr><br/>
<tr><td>{VERBnon-past} + (が/かの) + <strong>ごとし</strong></td><td>[夢見るがかのごとし - as if s.o. were dreaming]</td></tr><br/>
<tr></tr><br/>
<tr><td>{NOUN} + (か) + のごとく</td><td>[魚かのごとく泳ぐ s.o. swims as if they were a fish]</td></tr><br/>
<tr></tr><br/>
<tr><td>{NOUN} + (か) + ごとき + {NOUN}</td><td>[夢のごとき人生 life like a dream]</td></tr><br/>
<tr><td>{NOUN である / VERBnon-past} + (が/かの) ごとき + {NOUN}</td><td>[山に登る (が/かの) ごとき人生 life like climbing a mountain]</td></tr><br/>

### 4 — bamboo4

ごとく or 如く is literal diction and not normally used (sounds pedantic) in conversational speech. ような or ように can usually replace ごとく. <br/>
As between 生けるがごとく and 生けるごとく, the former is the conventional usage, but the latter is more or less acceptable (with emphasis on "less").

### 5 — 誠

「ごとく、ごとき」は「ように、ような」の古い言葉で、名詞の前に付かれて、改まった場所での会話や、硬い文章に使われます。
