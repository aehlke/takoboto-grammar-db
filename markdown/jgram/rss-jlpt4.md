## 〜へ (he)

Publication date as displayed: Wed, 20 Nov 2019 01:10:01 PST

[Original entry](http://www.jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=he&date=2019-11-20)

<span>〜へ (he)</span><br/><br/>
       <span><b>Meaning</b>: indicates direction of verb. Also pronounced 'e' </span><br/>
    <strong>Example</strong>: <i>I will go abroad next year.</i><br/>
<br/>
     [ <a href="http://www.jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=he">View this entry online</a> ]<br/>
<br/>
<strong><strong>   Notes:  </strong></strong><br/>へ and に there is no difference. They can both be used and are considered interchangeable. I see both に and へ in writing and talking. <br/>
<br/>
Such as:<br/>
<br/>
えきにいきます。- I'm going to the train station.<br/>
<br/>
 へ can replace に and vice versa. <br/>
<br/><br/>

<li>In <b>theory</b> へ is for movement in a direction, while に implies arrival at a specific destination.<br/>
