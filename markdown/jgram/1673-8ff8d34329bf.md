## Meaning

Don't have to 〜　(and are happy because of it) なくてもいい

Above anything, employees are happy for not having to ride on overcrowded trains.

## Notes

### 1 — TMac

Also can appear as なくてすむ。<br/>
<br/>
Has the nuance that the thing you don't have to do is something you don't want to do in the first place, and by being able to avoid it, you're happy because of it.<br/>
<br/>
Conversational grammar
