## Meaning

be dying to do something,  to feel X strongly, can't help it... 非常に

I am dying to go home

## Notes

### 1 — jshaomao

"be dying to do something" only makes sense when --tetamaranai is connected with expressions such as<br/>
-takute/-hoshikute

### 2 — jiji\_the\_cat

This grammar primary translates as "X is unbearable" Such as: 痛くてたまらない (itakute-tamaranai) It's extremely/unbearably painful"
