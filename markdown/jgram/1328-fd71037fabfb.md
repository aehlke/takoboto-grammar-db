## Meaning

has value; it is worth doing 十分に〜に値する・どうにか〜することができる。

It is worth reading that book

## Notes

### 1 — dc

耐える = to put up with, be able bear.

### 2 — LR

<b>Ref <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=nitaeru#">#</a></b></b> Kanzen Master Level 1 - p67 - no.64

### 3 — LR

<b>注意</b><br/>
While the verb 耐える as a piece of vocabulary means being able to withstand / endure, the grammar phrase にたえる means to deserve, be worthy of, to have value in... <b><strong>にたえる is completely different from 耐える</strong></b>!

### 4 — LR

Customarily used with a set number of words such as  市販、鑑賞、読む、見る、聞く、批判、評価 (worthy of being sold, admired, read, seen, heard, criticism/comment, evaluation)
