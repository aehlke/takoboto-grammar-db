## Meaning

Don't do ~

Don't let this get out.

## Examples

### 494 — Amatuka

<a href="http://www.csse.monash.edu.au/~jwb/cgi-bin/wwwjdic.cgi?9MHJ問題が起これば、学校だけで何とかしよう、外にはもらすまい。">
				問題が起これば、学校だけで何とかしよう、外にはもらす<strong>まい</strong>。<strong></strong></a> <br/>
		If some trouble crops up, sort it out somehow or other in school - 
		<strong>don't let it </strong>
		leak outside.
