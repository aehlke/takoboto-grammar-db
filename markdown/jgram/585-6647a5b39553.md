## Meaning

as; in the capacity of

I'm using this room as a storeroom.

## Notes

### 1 — Tori

Noun + として

### 2 — Tori

indicates the capacity, role or function of someone or something

### 3 — rubyhatchet

＊This is a compound particle which indicates the capacity, role, or function of someone or something.<br/>
<br/>
FORMATION:<br/>
Ｎ + として<br/>
<br/>
＊Be careful! としては (<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b>) has a different connotation. See that entry for more.
