## Meaning

place, time, occasion



## Notes

### 1 — leanne.nz

Use: to say you are just about to do something/ are in the middle of doing something, have just done some thing.<br/>
Formation： Verb Plain form +ところ<br/>
<br/>
食べる　+　ところだ　I'm just about to eat<br/>
食べている　＋　ところだ I'm just in the middle of eating<br/>
食べた　+　ところだ I just eaten<br/>
<br/>
Compare Verb Plain past form +ところ with Verb Plain past form + ばかり<br/>
<br/>
たべた　ところだ　ＶＳ　たべた　ばかり　だ<br/>
<br/>
〜ところだ　means literally at that moment<br/>
〜ばかりだ can have a time lapse. As in Would you like a coffee? "I've just had one (drank one before I came here)
