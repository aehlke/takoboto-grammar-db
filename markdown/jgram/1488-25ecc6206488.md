## Meaning

Despite that それなのに

We had heavy rains this morning due to the typhoon. Despite that, it suddenly started getting sunny this afternoon and now it's perfectly lovely outside.

## Notes

### 1 — pan

それが is very similar in meaning and usage to それなのに and にもかからわず。

### 2 — devking64

sore ga can also be used to indicate to your conversation partner that the answer to his/her question has a different/unexpected result.
