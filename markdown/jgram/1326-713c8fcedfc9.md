## Meaning

not hard to ...(名詞＋にかたくない/　Vる＋にかたくない）

not hard to imagine it becoming a problem

## Notes

### 1 — dc

in certain circumstances, it would be possible.<br/>
often used with imagine "it would not be hard to imagine this happening"

## Discussion

### 1 — blabby

This is very often used with 想像する and 察する.

### 2 — khongbietboi

子供を亡くした親の悲しみは、察するにかたくない。
