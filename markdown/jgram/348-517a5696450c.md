## Meaning

just because

just because he is japanese...

## Notes

### 1 — missshapoooo

PLAIN FORMS of (Verb, Noun, i and na Adjectives) + からといって<br/>
　<br/>
文１　からといって、　文２<br/>
<br/>
<br/>
In general means that " just because　文１ , does not necessarily mean..". Although after the pattern in lots of cases there go negative sentenses (否定形）, it is not necessarily always so.<br/>
<br/>
What comes after the pattern showes or explains that in truth it is differ from what could be thought of 文１.
