you must call your parents

## Discussion

### 1 — Amatuka

see also すべき a contraction of する + べき

### 2 — Amatuka

does not always come before the cupola (da, desu etc)

### 3 — Amatuka

My preference would be to have the entry as べき, but it's your database. ;-)

### 4 — Amatuka

Formed from Verb + beki<br/>
Past tense, negative etc are possible.<br/>
V = verb

### 5 — prahlada

I was just going through the grammer "bekida". I find this grammer is figuring in both level 3 and level 2. I find that the expalnations are contradictory to each other. I feel the entry in level 3 should be deleted .

### 6 — tigert

this is the same べき that is in level 3 the only diffrence is the だ sentence final, which has nothing to do with the should or ought to part of the grammar. it is just the casual sentance ending. so I think this entry should be merged with the level 3 and then deleted.<br/>
