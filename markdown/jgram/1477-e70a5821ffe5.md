## Meaning

If I had my druthers

on balance

### 3 — ドニー

I suppose you could say, "If I had to say (one way or the other)" or "If I were to say..."<br/>
<br/>
As in, one way or the other.<br/>
<br/>
どちらかと言えば、彼女が作ったケーキはちょっと・・・<br/>
If I say which way is, she-made cake is a little...<br/>
<br/>
"If I had to say one way or the other, the cake she made is a little..."<br/>
<br/>
I think we use this sort of construction in English, too. We say, "If I had to say..." and then state an opinion or observation.<br/>
<br/>
In the case of stating one's desire (If I had my druthers, I'd like to go to the beach) maybe we would say "If I may say..." or something of this nature.<br/>
<br/>
Just my two cents.
