## Meaning

Without consideration for... / Despite... / Without thinking... 〜を考えず・〜と関わりなく・〜を無視して

Without considering his parents expectations, he dropped out of university.

## Notes

### 1 — blabby

Noun + をよそに<br/>
<br/>
Aは　Bをよそに  Cする。- "Despite B, A did C."

### 2 — LR

<b>Ref <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=woyosoni#">#</a></b></b> Kanzen Master Level 1 - p41 - no. 39

### 3 — LR

<b>Ref <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=woyosoni#">#</a></b></b> A Dictionary of Advanced Japanese Grammar p531

### 4 — LR

Indifferent to ~; contrary to ~; ignoring ~; despite<br/>
Compound particle exprsses s.o.'s indifference to warnings, criticism, advice, accusations, opposition, anxiety, wishes, eagerness, provocative actions, etc.

### 5 — LR

{NOUN / のNominalized NOUN PHRASE} + {をよそに}

よそに and ものともせず is not equal.<br/>
よそに implys neglecting/(despite) B, A did C.<br/>
ものともせず implyes overcome obstacles and do something. in defiance of [~womonotomosezuni]<br/>
