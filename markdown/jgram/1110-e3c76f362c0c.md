## Meaning

In the face of,  in defiance of, in spite of にもかかわらず

周囲の反対をものともせずに、二人は結婚した。

## Notes

### 1 — LR

<b>Ref <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=womonotomosezuni#">#</a></b></b> Kanzen Master Level 1 - p41 - no.38

### 2 — LR

<b>Ref <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=womonotomosezuni#">#</a></b></b> A Dictionary of Advanced Japanese Grammar p493

### 3 — LR

NOUN ((or nominalized phrase)) + をものともせずに<br/>
Sometimes the に is omitted

### 4 — LR

X をものともせずに "lit., without making something out of X" means that one does something bravely and fearlessly in spite of existing, unfavorable circumstances.
