## Meaning

the same way as, as well as 同じである事。

大都市と同様、地方でも交通事故が増えている

## Notes

### 1 — IMABI

To douyou is used when you show a relationship between things that are essentially the same. Used in situations where in one thing happened it also did ... the same way. I myself teach Japanese at www.freewebs.com/kanjiwebs/
