## Meaning

To decide on, to choose

Let's make it Sunday.

## Discussion

### 1 — Miki

The Japanese is not natural from the context. It should be 毎年9月の最終日曜日.<br/>
For example, if the Sunday was 26th, we don't say 9月末（９がつすえ）。<br/>
ちなみに、月末（げつまつ） is at the end of month. 下旬（げじゅん） is end of month.

### 2 — Miki

9月末 is read ９がつまつ.<br/>
hmm but I found some sites 毎年○月末の▲曜日…。

### 3 — bamboo4

I agree with Miki. It sounds more natural. If you don't like 最終日曜日、you can say 最後の日曜日.<br/>
