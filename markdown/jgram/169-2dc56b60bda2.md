## Meaning

from ... until

from spring until autumn

## Notes

### 1 — dc

from (time/place) to (time/place)<br/>
<br/>
there are other time/place descriptors like <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> and <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> but these all just take one place. <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> has a start and ending.

## Discussion

### 1 — Amatuka

NB. '3G' as in mobile phone technology.

### 2 — hatsumomo

so its basically the same as 'made' - right?

### 3 — Miki

Yes but nuance is different.<br/>
When you use "made", the end of time/place is very clear. While "nikakete" is the end is ambiguous.

### 5 — sagittarius

isn't <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=kara-nikakete#">#</a></b> a different grammar point?

### 6 — jpmandy

<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=kara-nikakete#">#</a></b> <br/>
（〜から〜にかけて』は始まりと終わりがそれほどはっきりしていない。後の文は一回だけのことではなく。連続的なこと。<br/>
だから、『彼は腰からひざまでびしょぬれになった』のほうが正しいと思います。
