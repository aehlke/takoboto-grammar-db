## Meaning

not at all; far from; anything but; out of the question

Cold is not the word. It's FREEZING\!

## Notes

### 1 — dc

dokoro de ha nai / dokoro de ha naku<br/>
<br/>
Dokoro dewa nai goes at the end of a sentence. Dokoro dewa naku goes in the middle.

### 2 — rubyhatchet

＊Use this grammar to strongly deny something.<br/>
<br/>
FORMATION:<br/>
Ｖ(plain form) + どころではない<br/>
いＡ + どころではない<br/>
なＡ・Ｎ + どころではない<br/>
<br/>
As for どころではなく, this grammar fits into the middle of a sentence, as in example <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=dokorodehanai,%20dokorodehanaku#">#</a></b>. どころではない is meant to end a sentence.

場合じゃない has a completely different context.The urgency of どころではない is not at all conveyed by 場合じゃない.<br/>
場合 has a case, but どころではない has no case; you look everywhere and it's not there.
