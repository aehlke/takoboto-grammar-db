## Meaning

despite a certain time / despite a certain situation 〜時（なの）に・状況（なの）に　・相手の状況を配慮する阜ｻとして、話の前置きに用いることも多い。

Thank you for seeing me despite being so busy.

### 3 — LR

Used most often when the action of one's speaking partner expresses considerateness towards someone. - We would like to thank you for coming <strong>in spite of</strong> the heavy rain etc.
