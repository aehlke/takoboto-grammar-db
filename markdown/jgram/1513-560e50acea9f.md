## Meaning

Full of / A lot of ばかり, 全部、全体がそればかりの状態であることを表す。

The meeting was full of good ideas.

## Notes

### 1 — blabby

Noun + ずくめだ。<br/>
Noun + ずくめ + の + Noun

## Discussion

### 1 — blabby

Similar in some ways to "だらけ" and "まみれ" and it seems to have the same meaning as "全て〜ばかり" or "〜ばかりたくさんある".<br/>
It often has the form of  Adjective+こと+ずくめ<br/>

### 2 — bamboo4

ずくめ also written 尽くめ added to a noun means 100% so.But there are a lot of nouns and noun phrases which to do accept ずくめ.<br/>
