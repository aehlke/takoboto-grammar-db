## Meaning

cannot help

I can't help feeling sad

## Notes

### 1 — dc

I can't help doing ...<br/>
I can't stop doing ...<br/>
<br/>
these are both the same:<br/>
verb+ ~zu ni ha irarenai<br/>
verb+~nai de ha irarenai<br/>
<br/>
(eg sezuni is a contraction of shinai)

### 2 — dc

Not used for "feeling" something, where you should use <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b><br/>
<br/>
-- doing<br/>
I can't help shopping<br/>
買い物せずにはいられない<br/>
買い物しないではいられない<br/>
<br/>
-- feeling<br/>
I can't help feeling anxious<br/>
NG: 私は不安しないではいられない<br/>
OK: 私は不安でならない <br/>

## Discussion

### 1 — your name

why is this NG? This sentence is not wrong.<br/>
I can't help shopping. 買い物せずには いられない。<br/>
note : せず is a transformation of する＋ず nega.<br/>

### 2 — bamboo4

買い物しないではいられない would be a normal, conversational way to say it, though.
