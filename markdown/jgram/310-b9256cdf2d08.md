## Meaning

if .. is possible, then...

if i can go

## Notes

### 1 — 誠

動詞可能形　＋　〜ものなら：　〜ことが可能なら　（実現が難しいことについて言うことが多い）<br/>
 "If only I could/may...,"  <br/>
<br/>
〜う／ようものなら：　もし〜ようなことをしたら．．．（大変なことになる） Once if (one) happen to ... , then (bad consequence)."

## Discussion

### 1 — halx

Used when one wishes to do something difficult.<br/>
Usually used together with the potential form.

### 2 — bamboo4

"if" does not quite get you there. For example, "If I come home late, he won't let me in" should perhaps be "Would I come home late, he won't let me in the house."<br/>
ものなら is not iffy.<br/>

### 3 — halx

ex <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=mononara#">#</a></b> has nothing to do with this grammar point.<br/>
甘いもの (= sweets) just happens to be suffixed with なら (group 3)

### 4 — halx

Shouldn't ex <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=mononara#">#</a></b> be better translated as: "Whatever you pick for me is fine"?

### 5 — mk

-ようものなら is quite iffy. it denotes a hypothetical condition. [if i came home late, he wouldn't let me in]

### 6 — 誠

〜ものなら：　"If only I could/may.....", "If only it was possible to ....", "If only it was possible that..."<br/>
<br/>

### 7 — 誠

ex <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=mononara#">#</a></b> is 〜もの(thing)　＋　ならば, and therefore does not belong to the usage of 〜ものなら .<br/>
From ex<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=mononara#">#</a></b> to ex<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=mononara#">#</a></b>, it doesn't give the meaning or correct usage of 〜ものなら, therefore treat it as 〜もの　＋　なら。 <br/>
〜ものなら　always follow the 可能形 potential form. and used to indicate something difficult to realize, or some ideal thinking of the writer,e.g. : "If only i was still a child, ...."      <br/>

### 8 — 誠

ex <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=mononara#">#</a></b> belongs to 〜う／ようものなら:<br/>
My father is very strict. Once if I happen to come home late, my dad won't let me in.<br/>
