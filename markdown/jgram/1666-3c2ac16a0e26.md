## Meaning

absent-minded(ly); blank(ly) ぼんやりと暮らす

idle one's time away

## Notes

### 1 — aoiaki

① Unclearly seen/Obscurely<br/>
<br/>
遠くに富士山がぼんやり見える。<br/>
Mt. Fuji can been seen dimly in the distance.<br/>
<br/>
この漢字をぼんやりとしたが覚えていない。<br/>
I have but a faint recollection of this kanji.<br/>
<br/>
朝ご飯食べなかったので、頭がぼんやりしているよ。<br/>
I can't think clearly because I didnt eat breakfast<br/>
<br/>
② Carelessness<br/>
<br/>
ぼんやりと海を眺めていた。<br/>
He was gazing vacently at the ocean.<br/>
<br/>
ぼんやりするな！<br/>
Be careful! (Don't space out!)<br/>
<br/>
③　Doing nothing at all<br/>
ぼんやりと暮らす<br/>
idle one's time away<br/>
<br/>
④ Insenstive remarks<br/>
<br/>
ぼんやりした人<br/>
dimwit, blockhead, slow, thickheaded
