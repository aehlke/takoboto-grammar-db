## Meaning

covered with; littered with; infested with,full of

This website is littered with mistakes.

## Notes

### 1 — rubyhatchet

だらけ refers to bad things or objects, such as "mistakes," "garbage," etc. It is only used after nouns, but can be used in one of two ways.<br/>
<br/>
N + だらけ<br/>
1. 「Aは(が)Bだらけだ」:　部屋はゴミだらけだ。<br/>
2. 「BだらけのA」: ゴミだらけの部屋<br/>
*Note that the 2nd use is not a complete sentence.
