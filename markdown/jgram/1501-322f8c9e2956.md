## Meaning

I guess, wonder, probably, isn't it?

I guess he will never come back the place.

## Notes

### 1 — Miki

だろう      spoken language<br/>
であろう。  is ofen used in articlel with colloquial flavour.<br/>
<br/>
でありましょう。 polite form of であろう。<br/>
