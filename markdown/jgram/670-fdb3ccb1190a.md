## Meaning

Vm、N slight tendency toward, seems like, feels so..

He is on the heavy side.

## Notes

### 1 — hana

<b>gimi</b><br/>
<br/>
Verbs = V conjunctive form + 気味<br/>
<strong>疲れ気味<br/>
<br/>
Nouns = N + 気味<br/>
<strong>きんちょうぎみ<br/>
</strong></strong>

## Discussion

### 1 — Amatuka

Formed from verb-masu base + gimi<br/>
or from noun + gimi<br/>
Vm = Verb -masu base<br/>
N = noun

### 2 — angelitosh2004

expresses a feeling that, although not serious at the moment, there seems to be a sign of or a tendency toward a particular condition, as in, feeling a cold coming on or sensing that one is a little overweight (see my examples above).

### 5 — erekose200

So what is the difference between 「がち」and 「ぎみ」？<br/>

### 6 — bluntcrayon

The main difference between 「がち」and ぎみ is that 「がち」usually describes a general tendency in someone or something while 「ぎみ」describes a visible indication of a tendency. <br/>
e.g. 1 田中は最近太りがちだ<br/>
Tanaka has a general tendency to gain weigh but is not necessarily overweight at the moment of speech。Compare with;<br/>
２）田中は最近太り気味だ<br/>
Tanaka is showing signs of being overweight。<br/>
<br/>
Also 「がち」Cannot express the idea of "a touch of" or " a little".<br/>
(taken from the Intermediate grammar dictionary)
