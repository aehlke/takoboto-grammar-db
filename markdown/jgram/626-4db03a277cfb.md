## Meaning

(location marker, time marker, direction marker)

There's a dog in that car.

## Notes

### 1 — Sakuratree

Ni can mark time such as:<br/>
<br/>
At 3:00 pm, I studied French.<br/>
午後三時間にフランス語を勉強しました。<br/>
<br/>
An object location marker:<br/>
<br/>
In Tokyo, there is the Tokyo Tower.<br/>
東京に東京タワーにあります。<br/>
<br/>
And direction:<br/>
<br/>
I'm going to the store tomorrow.<br/>
明日店に行きます。

## Discussion

### 1 — Amatuka

There are a few places where に can't be used.<br/>
×友だちにのプレゼント [tomodachi ni no purezento] BAD<br/>
○友だちへのプレゼント [tomodachi he no purezento] GOOD<br/>
<br/>
And some where only に can be used<br/>
事務所にパソコンがある [jimusho ni pasokon ga aru]

### 2 — arachanox

In the Notes section, the first sample talks about three hours, the time span, not three o'clock,<br/>
三時間 is not 三時<br/>
And the second means <br/>
It's in Tokyo *at the Tokyo Tower.<br/>
I think.<br/>
