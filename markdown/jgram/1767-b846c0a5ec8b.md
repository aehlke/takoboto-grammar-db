## Meaning

like, alike to mitai na= alike

Kodomo mitai na- like a child.   Otona wa kodomo mitai na hanashite imasu= the adult is talking like a child.  Tenshi mitai na= like an angel. Kodomo wa tenshi mitai na nete imasu- the child is sleeping like an angel.
