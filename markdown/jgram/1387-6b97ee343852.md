## Meaning

time and place prepositions at in on

On Friday at 3pm

## Notes

### 1 — dc

に <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> is a placement particle locating a point in time or space, で <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> indicates the domain in which the totality of an event takes place.

### 2 — dc

The particle に requires something physical, such as 東京に本店があります,whereas, で requires some activity, such as 東京で会合があります.
