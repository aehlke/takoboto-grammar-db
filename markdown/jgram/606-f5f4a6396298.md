<b>relative clause</b>: A relative clause is a sub-sentence that modifies the noun (also known as 'noun head') it is placed before.  For example 万足したユーザー. [mansoku shita yu-za-] A user who has been satisfied.

### 5 — Amatuka

<b>nominative</b>: The subject particle が <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> is also referred to as the <a href="http://dictionary.reference.com/search?q=nominative">nominative</a> an English grammar term defined as "Of, relating to, or being the case of the subject of a finite verb (as I in I wrote the letter) and of words identified with the subject of a copula, such as a predicate nominative"

### 6 — Amatuka

<b>Accusative</b>: The object particle を [wo / o] is also referred to as the <a href="http://dictionary.reference.com/search?q=accusative">accusative</a> an English grammar term defined as "Of, relating to, or being the grammatical case that is the direct object of a verb or the object of certain prepositions."

### 7 — Amatuka

<b>genitive</b>: The possessive particle の <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> is also referred to as the <a href="http://dictionary.reference.com/search?q=Genitive">genitive</a> an English grammar term defined as "1. Of, relating to, or being the grammatical case expressing possession, measurement, or source. <br/>
2. Of or relating to an affix or construction, such as a prepositional phrase, characteristic of the genitive case."<br/>

### 8 — Amatuka

<b>postposition marker</b>: Various particles may be referred to as 'postposition markers'.  This word is derived from the English grammar term <a href="http://dictionary.reference.com/search?q=preposition">preposition</a> and indicates a marker placed after a substantive to indicate the relation of that substantive to a verb, adjective or other substantive.

### 9 — Amatuka

<b>predicator</b>: The main constituent of a sentence - the verb or adjective at its end.
