## Discussion

### 1 — Amatuka

Formed from Vru + ijou.<br/>
Also from 'continuing action' e.g. 使ってる以上<br/>
Vru = dictionary form. （〜る for ichidan / regular group 2 verbs)

### 2 — Miki

以上は in context can be replaced with <a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=からには">からには</a>.

### 3 — dc

is this the same as <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> ?<br/>
<br/>
学生である限り、勉強します<br/>
学生である以上、勉強します<br/>
<br/>
are identical in meaning/nuance?

### 4 — shrutinarula

Since/Till I am a student, I will study<br/>
<br/>
Being a student,I will study.
