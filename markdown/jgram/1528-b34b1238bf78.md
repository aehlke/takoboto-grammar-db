## Meaning

even if you do... it will not result to.. 〜してもよい結果にはならない; 期待できない

Nothing will change even if I asked advice from my friend.

I have heard this grammar used a lot right at the point where the speaker gives up in a hopeless situation. Simlar to saying "Even if ... it's too late."<br/>
