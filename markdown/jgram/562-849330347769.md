## Discussion

### 1 — Amatuka

I don't really know what's 'special' about 'については' that isn't just 'について' + 'は'

### 2 — 誠

I agree with Amatuka, は is somehow an emphasis of the topic of sentence.<br/>
<br/>
ex3812, is interpreted as について　＋　はなしてください<br/>
<br/>
ex3819, i think 「危険はたとえあるとしても少ない」 would be natural. Otherwise, 「危険はほとんどない。」
