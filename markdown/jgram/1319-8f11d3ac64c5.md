## Meaning

without (the thing "without" is s.t. that should naturally have been there) 〜をしないで • がないままで

You cannot enter university without the proper forms

## Notes

### 1 — LR

<b>Ref <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=nashini#">#</a></b></b> Kanzen Master Level 1 - p17 - no.17

### 2 — LR

<b>Ref <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=nashini#">#</a></b></b> A Dictionary of Advanced Japanese Grammar p363

### 3 — LR

nashi ni is usually used in written Japanese or in formal spoken Japanese

### 4 — LR

when wa follows nashi ni, the final predicate has to be negative.

### 5 — LR

before nashi ni can be either a noun or a "koto"-nominalized noun phrase
