## Meaning

easy to V 〜が使ってる動詞をする場合が簡単だと言う意味です

a beautiful woman is easy to fall in love with

## Notes

### 1 — tigert

this is a simple way to say something is easy to do because of some reason.<br/>
<br/>
美しい女性が　好き合いやすいですね<br/>
Beatiful women are easy to love. <br/>
<br/>
おいしくて安いピザが食べやすいです。<br/>
Pizza that is both deliciouse and affordable is easily eaten.<br/>
