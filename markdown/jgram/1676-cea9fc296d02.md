## Meaning

surrounding, concerning, about \[/ focusing on / in connection with\] \[〜\] を中心点にして、どんな議論や対立関係が起こっているかを言う時

There is much debate "concerning...etc" ......X

## Notes

### 1 — COUNTFUNKULA

I came across this in one of my study sessions<br/>
and had a hard time with a clear meaning. I took<br/>
it to mean concerning/surrounding but was'nt sure<br/>
*THANKS WWWJDIC*. I hope this helps someone!!

### 2 — ZekkDistor

Changed the title from (o) megutte to wo megutte because I had problems adding examples.<br/>
Also, you'll probably won't ever find it without the wo particle, so might as well not put it between brackets ^^

### 3 — sureshnihon

〜を中心にそれに関係あることになる〜<br/>
