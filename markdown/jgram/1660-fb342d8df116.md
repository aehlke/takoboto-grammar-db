## Meaning

not much, too much

あまり面白くない =\> not very interesting / あまり遅い =\> too late

## Notes

### 1 — bluntcrayon

とても..から　(x)　<br/>
とても...ので (x)<br/>
...しすぎるので (x)<br/>
Expresses that a lot of or too much of (something) was the reason for (x)<br/>
<br/>
(Dictionary form) verb + あまり<br/>
Noun　＋の＋あまり <br/>
EG.痛さのあまり、涙が出てきた。<br/>
その会社では働きすぎたあまり、病気で倒れる社員が増えている。

### 5 — jmadsen

I think the JLPT 2 version of this has been confused with the lower forms. JLPT 2 form means "too much, in an unusual or unexpected way"<br/>
<br/>
ex. 驚き　の　あまり、声も　出なかった。<br/>
His surprise was so great, he couldn't even shout out.
