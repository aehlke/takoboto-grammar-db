## Discussion

### 1 — Amatuka

Formed from [Verb -masu base] + gari<br/>
[Vｍ] for 'Verb -masu base'

### 2 — Miki

We say 怒りがち, not 怒りがり。<br/>
私のボスは、短気で怒りがち。<br/>
watashi no boss wa, tanki de okori gachi. <br/>
My boss is short-tempered and tends to get angry easily.

### 3 — Miki

彼は暑がりで、冬の間も半袖を着ている。<br/>
kare wa atsugaride,fuyu no aida mo hansode wo kiteiru.<br/>
He is sensitive to heat and wears half-sleevs shirt even in winter.

### 6 — bamboo4

You don't say 怒りがち or 怒りがり. They don't exist in the Japanese vocabruary. It has to be 怒りっぽい. You can say 曇りがち、遠慮がち etc. It is used to mean "there is the tendecy to" or "it is frequent that...."

や in 恥ずかしがり屋 is a dimunitive giving a status to a particular tendency. It cannot be used in all situations, and does not change the basic meaning of がり.
