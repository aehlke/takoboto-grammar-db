## Meaning

no way to

no way to eat the soup

## Notes

### 1 — dc

used with the masu-stem + ようがない<br/>
<br/>
<b>食べ</b>ます →　食べようがない<br/>
<b>し</b>ます　⇒　しようがない

### 2 — dc

note that the word 用 (you) on its own means "something to do". As in <br/>
　ようがある<br/>
　i have to do some errands<br/>
<br/>
so that leads to this confusion:<br/>
<br/>
行く用がない = I have no *reason* to go there<br/>
(Lit: I have nothing to do when i get there)<br/>
<br/>
行きようがない = I have no *way* to get there<br/>
<br/>
1) 連絡するようがない<br/>
　　no business to contact them for<br/>
2) 連絡しようがない<br/>
　　no way to contact
