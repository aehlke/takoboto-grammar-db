## Meaning

even; only; just

I can't even see it

## Notes

### 1 — dc

Verb-masu stem<br/>
　尋ねさえ　　＞ just ask<br/>
　待ってさえ　＞ just wait<br/>
<br/>
V-te<br/>
　使ってさえ = just use<br/>
<br/>
Noun<br/>
　あなたさえよければ = if its OK with you<br/>
　<br/>
irregular:<br/>
　する　＞　しさえ = if you just do..<br/>
　　　　　　勉強しさえ　= only study<br/>
　くる　＞　来さえ　（きさえ）＞　just come<br/>
　　　　　　ついてきさえ　＞ just follow <br/>
<br/>
often used with ーば <br/>
　意見を述べさえすればよい。<br/>
　If you just tell us your opinion it would be great.<br/>
<br/>
negative<br/>
　見えます　＞　見えさえしない<br/>
　can NOT even see<br/>
<br/>

### 2 — dc

note this usage:<br/>
<br/>
○　見ることさえしない<br/>
　　cannot even see<br/>
<br/>
○　見さえしない<br/>
　　did not even look<br/>
<br/>
×　見るさえない　<br/>
　　incorrect

### 3 — dc

<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> and <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> are slightly formal. an informal way of saying "even" is with <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b><br/>
見ることもできない - cannot even see
