## Meaning

When I think it may

When I think that it may continue this way I really get depressed.

## Discussion

### 1 — Amatuka

When you think [___] you get [certain feeling].

### 2 — Amatuka

I don't think that this usage is limited to かと思うと.  と思うと probably has plenty of examples.

### 3 — dc

often this is used when a second, contradicting thought follows.

### 4 — Amatuka

I don't think that last example is the same usage.  <br/>
What the others have in common are <br/>
1. Continuing, every time type occurrence (e.g. When [every time] I think X ...)<br/>
2. The result is feeling / emotion related.<br/>
<br/>
The third example is a) Past tense, once off.<br/>
b) The second half of the sentence /isn't/ the result of the first.<br/>
c) Isn't かおもうと but かおもったら

### 5 — Exrulez

Kono joutai ha kono mama da to tsudzuku ka to omou to ochikonde shimau.<br/>
When i think this situation might continue on like this i get really depressed.<br/>
What do you guys think about how this sounds?<br/>
(roomaji de Gomen ne ^^')
