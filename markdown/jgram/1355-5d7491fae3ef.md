## Meaning

combine with

combine work and play

## Notes

### 1 — blabby

Noun + を + 兼ねて<br/>
Noun + を + 兼ねる<br/>

## Examples

### 1116 — bamboo4

<a href="http://nihongo.monash.edu/cgi-bin/wwwjdic?9MHJ大阪の仕事を兼ねて京都見物に行く">
				大阪の仕事を<strong>兼ねて</strong>京都見物に行く<strong></strong></a> <br/>
		In going to Osaka on business, I'll 
		<strong>combine it with </strong>
		a sightseeing visit to Kyoto.

		the portfolio of Foreign Minister.
