## Meaning

won't (do something)

I won't tell her

## Notes

### 1 — rubyhatchet

＊This grammar has the connotation of resolving not to do something.<br/>
<br/>
FORMATION:<br/>
Ｖ(dict. form) + まい<br/>
Ｖ(minus ます) + まい<br/>
ALSO USED:<br/>
する -&gt; しまい<br/>
くる -&gt; こまい

## Discussion

### 1 — Amatuka

Not used in typical spoken Japanese.

### 2 — Amatuka

N.B. Derivation of ~まい involves uncertainty - extension to 'negative violition' arrived the same way as the +ve violition did.  <br/>
Therefore, literally, it's more like "I probably won't tell ..." "I don't think I'll tell ..."

### 3 — Jon

What about a past tense? How would one say: "He wouldn't tell me." ?

### 4 — Miki

彼は言わなかっただろう。<br/>
I don't think there is a past tense of まい version.<br/>
言わないだろう。/ 言わないようにしよう。is more popular spoken style of 言うまい。
