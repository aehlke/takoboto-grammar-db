## Meaning

in sharp contrast with; compared to に比べると反対に

The son, in stark contrast to his meticulous father, was slovenly.

## Notes

### 1 — LR

<b>Ref <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=nihikikae#">#</a></b></b> Kanzen Master Level 1 - p44 - no.42

### 2 — LR

<b>Ref <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=nihikikae#">#</a></b></b> A Dictionary of Advanced Japanese Grammar p380

### 3 — LR

Should indicate <b>sharp</b> contrast, as opposed to small differences
