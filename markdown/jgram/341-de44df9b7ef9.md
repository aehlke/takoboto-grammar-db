## Meaning

Very; Quite; The 〜 thing is that....

To my great surprise...

## Notes

### 1 — rubyhatchet

いＡ + ことに<br/>
なＡ + な + ことに<br/>
Ｖた(past plain form) + ことに

### 2 — rubyhatchet

This grammar should be used at the head of the sentence. It should be used to emphasize feelings, emotions, or sentiments.

## Discussion

### 1 — 誠

I think the English translation should be better with 「The 〜 thing is that, .....」for 「〜ことに」. So for<br/>
ex<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=kotoni#">#</a></b> 嬉しいことに、四月から給料が一割ほど上がる<br/>
「The happy/hearthening thing is that, from April onwards my salary will go up by around 10%.」I prefer to use "hearthening" in this case.<br/>
Notice the comma is at the ことに<br/>
<br/>
ex<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=kotoni#">#</a></b>「The unfortunate thing is that, I dropped and lost all my change through a hole in my pocket.」

### 2 — rubyhatchet

Example <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=kotoni#">#</a></b> does not belong in this entry. This must be "koto ni (ha)." Also, you can only use the plain past tense of a verb before it, not plain dictionary form.
