### 4 — anna.kendall

Yeah I think the above is right　concerning a verb, VERBたいのに気がついた

### 5 — Darrkangel

Actually, for a verb, it's the verb + の or こと＋に気が付いた。

### 6 — 赤毛

I think this entry should be 気が付いた rather than と気が付いた. Maybe rather JPLT1 than JPLT2 too. If somebody agree with it, I will change the entry.

### 7 — tigert

Hey 赤毛､　I agree with you. the と　in 納豆が美味しいと気が付いた　is a quotation marker. it is the same と used in 納豆が美味しいと思います. but it has nothing to do with the second 気が付ける｡<br/>
<br/>
<br/>
I think there should also be a note about the diffrence between 気が付ける　and 気を付ける　their meanings are completely diffrent. the first being "I realized" and the second being "being safe" or "taking care"<br/>
<br/>
