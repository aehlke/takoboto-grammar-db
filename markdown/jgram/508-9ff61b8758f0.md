## Meaning

when; on the occasion of; at (the time of); in; upon (doing something)

When I transferred to Osaka, I left my wife and children behind in Tokyo.

## Notes

### 1 — rubyhatchet

＊The crucial difference between に当たって/に当たり and に歳して is that the former indicates an occasion with a formal sentence, but the latter indicates a special occasion.<br/>
＊Therefore, do NOT use に際して when speaking of trivial, everyday occurences.<br/>
<br/>
FORMATION:<br/>
Ｎ + に際して
