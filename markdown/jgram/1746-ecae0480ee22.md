## Meaning

Coupled with, together with といっしょになって

In many countries, water and air polution is coupled with progressive environmental destruction

## Notes

### 1 — LR

<b>Ref <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=toaimatte#">#</a></b></b> Kanzen Master Level 1 - p40 - no.36

### 2 — LR

{NOUN1} + {と} + {NOUN2} + (と) + {が} + {相まって} is a frequent pattern.<br/>
実力<b>と</b>運<b>とが</b>相まって、彼を成功に導いた。<br/>
(His ability coupled with luck brought about his success.)
