## Meaning

not to stop

Kids don't stop seeking their parents' love.

## Examples

### 1114 — bamboo4

<a href="http://www.csse.monash.edu.au/~jwb/cgi-bin/wwwjdic.cgi?9MHJ子供たちは親の愛を求めてやまない。">
				子供たちは親の愛を求めて<strong>やまない</strong>。<strong></strong></a> <br/>
		Kids 
		<strong>don't stop </strong>
		seeking their parent's love.

### 3368 — dc

<a href="http://www.csse.monash.edu.au/~jwb/cgi-bin/wwwjdic.cgi?9MHJ空模様からすると、雨はしばらくはやまないだろう。 ">

		From the look of the sky I'm afraid the rain 
		<strong>won't let up </strong>
		for a while.

		I 
		<strong>(never stop) </strong>
		wish you good health.

Formed from verb-te + yamanai.  (Note that Vte is usually associated with feelings &amp; desires).<br/>
<br/>
Vte = Verb, -te connective form.

### 3 — bamboo4

### 6 — blabby

〜てやまない.  This is often used with words such as 求める, 希望する, 祈る and 願う.

### 7 — kuma

This grammar is indeed for 1kyuu and does not always belog to the written language. If you hold a speech the form "-teyamimasen" 「てやみません」<br/>
<br/>
It comes from the word「やむ」that means stop. For example you can say 「雨いつごろやむかな」, which corresponds to when you in English want to say "When will it stop raining, I wonder".<br/>
<br/>
Or this <br/>
A&gt;「あれ、ピアノの音がやんだ。レッスン終わったんだ」Eh? The sound of the piano stopped. The lesson is over.<br/>
<br/>
B&gt;　「そうね」Yes, indeed.<br/>
