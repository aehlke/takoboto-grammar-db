## Meaning

each time,  whenever,  either way

whenever i see my mum knitting a sweater..

## Notes

### 1 — rubyhatchet

＊A tricky piece of grammar. The meaning in Japanese is as follows:<br/>
〜ときは、いつも<br/>
〜ときも、ときも<br/>
<br/>
＊「Ａにつけ、Ｂにつけ」 is a common construction. A and B are often pairs or two of a kind, such as strong/weak, good/evil, etc.<br/>
<br/>
FORMATION:<br/>
Ｖ(plain form) + につけ<br/>
いＡ + につけ

### 3 — missshapoooo

tabini and nituke have a slight differense. In "nituke" after the pattern there come phrases expressing feelings/emotions or emotional patterns. So that the meaning of "nituke" - whenever someone happens to be in same situation, always has certain feelings and thus does something.
