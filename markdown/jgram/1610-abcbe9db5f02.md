## Meaning

Express admiration/horror; eg, how +adjective, so+adjective

My son's room is so dirty, it's dreadful.

### 1 — shiho-chan

### 2 — kadoka66

Hmm to make one of the usages of this grammar item a little more understandable there is a Japanese drinking game which I often play with friends.<br/>
<br/>
It goes like this:<br/>
<br/>
なにものといったら、なにもの<br/>
What would you think of if I said ??<br/>
<br/>
例：人A：バナナと言ったら、黄色い。<br/>
人B:黄色いと言ったら、色。<br/>
人C:色と言ったら、虹<br/>
<br/>
Etc etc<br/>
<br/>
It's a bit like Mallets Mallet for those of you who are familiar with the British 80's Morning show.<br/>
<br/>
