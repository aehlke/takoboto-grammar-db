## Meaning

even if; assuming

Even if it was true, I wouldn't believe it of him\!

### 3 — Wes

This entry is confused.<br/>
CrayonShin's Toshite is entirely different for our purposes here and should be separated from this grammar point。<br/>
人間として - as a human<br/>
人間としても - even as a human<br/>
these are different than<br/>
人間だとしても - even if I were human<br/>
<br/>
Also, Verbs need TA form, unlike　としたら。<br/>
<br/>
消えたとしても誰も気づかない気がする -　I feel like even If I were to disappear nobody would notice.<br/>
<br/>
る　＋　としても has a completely different meaning than たとしても and is used very very rarely.<br/>
<br/>
here is an example<br/>
<br/>
地球温暖化が行われてるとしても気分がかなりいいです。Despite global warming, i feel ok.<br/>
<br/>
one is EVEN IF IT WERE, one is  EVEN IF IT IS OR MAY BE.
