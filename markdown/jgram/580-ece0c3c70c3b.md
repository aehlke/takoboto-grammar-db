## Meaning

this, that, the other

this is mine, that is yours and the other is anybody's guess.

## Notes

### 1 — blutorange

The kanji are just given for reference. Kana spelling is normal today. <br/>
These words are part of what is called kosoado. The ko, so and a indicate the relation to the speaker, the re turns them into the pronouns for a certain object.<br/>
The most important thing to remember is that you cannot these words to say, for example, "I want this apple" = "kore ringo ga hoshii." That is wrong. You can only use them for "I want this."<br/>
("this apple" would be "kono ringo")

## Discussion

### 1 — Amatuka

kore : near to speaker<br/>
sore : near to spoken to<br/>
are : far from both speaker and spoken to

### 2 — Amatuka

There are more words than these in the 'set'.
