## Meaning

only; because of that



## Notes

### 1 — dc

科学者にして出来事<br/>
<br/>
other similar meanings:<br/>
<br/>
<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> だけ only：<br/>
　科学者＿だけ＿出来事<br/>
　a thing only a scholar could do<br/>
<br/>
<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> だから so<br/>
　科学者＿だから＿出来た<br/>
　he is a scholar so he could do it<br/>
<br/>
<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> だからこそ due to/because of<br/>
　科学者だからこそできた<br/>
　because he is a great scholar he could do it
