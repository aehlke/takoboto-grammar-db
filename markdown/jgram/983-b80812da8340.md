## Meaning

equivalent

this is equivalent to that

## Notes

### 1 — dc

noun+ni soutou suru<br/>
　There is no English equivalent for this Japanese<br/>
　この日本語に相当する英語はない

### 3 — bamboo4

等価 is often a mathematic terminology or something of equivalent value.<br/>
同等 is often used to "something that can be substituted, e.g., 一等賞または同等品=The first prize or a substitute.<br/>
均等 is often used to indicate substantive equivalence in terms of purpose, use, characteristics, etc. 均等論=Theory of equivalents, often used in patent infringement cases.<br/>
