## Meaning

moreover; what's more; additionally; not only

as well as not liking him...

## Notes

### 1 — dc

ue ni is also used to say something exists on top of something else<br/>
Formation<br/>
A no ue ni B ga aru.<br/>
A のうえに　B がある。<br/>
つくえのうえにほんがある
