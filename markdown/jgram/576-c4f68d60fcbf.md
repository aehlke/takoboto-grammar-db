## Meaning

also

this is also mine

## Notes

### 1 — Sakuratree

Just like how in English, we say, "me too!" Japanese uses the particle も<br/>
<br/>
It rained today too.<br/>
今日も雨が降っていました。

## Discussion

### 1 — Amatuka

も replaces は in the first example sentence.<br/>
A previous <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> being mine is implied.<br/>
(e.g. 'あれは私のです。ええと、これも私のです。'<br/>
That (over there) is mine.  Erm, and this is also mine. )
