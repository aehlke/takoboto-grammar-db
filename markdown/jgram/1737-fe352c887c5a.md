## Meaning

by \_\_\[day, date, time, etc.\]\_\_

Turn in the report by Friday.

## Notes

### 1 — Chad

までに is giving a time limit which must be kept. Finishing the required action before is also fine.<br/>
<br/>
In opposition まで denotes an action etc. which continues until the given time and does not finish before.

### 2 — american57

Cannot be used with continuous action/state, such as "aru/iru".  See made de for subtle difference.
