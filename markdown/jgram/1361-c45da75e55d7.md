## Meaning

No matter what; Even if; No matter how

no matter what kind of person

## Notes

### 1 — Ayumi

For most verbs that end in iru/eru (except for a few verbs I mean), remove the &lt;b&gt;ru&lt;/b&gt; and add dearou. <br/>
For other verbs that end in, ru (exceptional verbs are in this group), tsu, u ku, gu, mu, and bu, change the last syllable to the &lt;b&gt;i&lt;/b&gt; section of it (as in ru = ri, tsu = chi, u = i, etc.). For example...<br/>
taberu = tabedearou<br/>
miru = midearou<br/>
kaeru* = kaeridearou (one of the exceptional verbs)<br/>
nugu = nugidearou

### 4 — Miki

「であろうと」and 「だろうと」are both ok in writing and speaking. 書く時も話す時にも、両方使えます。だろうと is usually used in speaking and であろうと sounds with a self-important air, I feel. 重々しい響きがある。
