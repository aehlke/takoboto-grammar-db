## Meaning

rather,  more than

cold rather than cool

## Notes

### 1 — rubyhatchet

「Ａというより、Ｂ」: B is the speaker's judgement or opinion.<br/>
<br/>
＊むしろ is often used with this grammar. However, it's simply emphasis and doesn't translate very well. For example, 「彼は、天才というより、むしろ変人だ。」(He's *more* an oddball rather than a genius.)<br/>
<br/>
FORMATION:<br/>
Ｖ(plain form) + というより<br/>
いＡ + というより<br/>
なＡ・Ｎ + (だ)というより
