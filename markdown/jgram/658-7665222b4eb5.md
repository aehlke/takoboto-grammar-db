## Meaning

Informal Imperative (request form)

Sit down.

## Notes

### 1 — sarruby

Drop the ーます from the ーます form and add なさい to make this form.

## Discussion

### 1 — Amatuka

I guess this is half way between a 'polite command' and a 'fairly forceful request'.

### 2 — Amatuka

[V-masu base] + nasai

### 3 — Amatuka

'[The teacher]' isn't explicitly in the Japanese version, but the English might look odd without it.<br/>
<br/>
'(just)' wouldn't be natural in the English - the だけ in the Japanese doesn't explicitly tranlate.

### 4 — Miki

The Japanese translation is quite nice except the tense.<br/>
「…。」と、（先生は）指示しました／指示した。

### 5 — dc

thanks, updated..

### 6 — bamboo4

In the English version of the first example, perhaps "just those" instead of "(just) people." <br/>
Also, I don't know how they do it in schools abroad, but in Japan, the teacher normally says "should stand up" instead of "should sit down."

### 7 — anon

The coloring in ex <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=nasai#">#</a></b> is very confusing. The 'し' looks to be part of '早く', which is not the intention, if I understand correctly.

### 8 — dc

fixed.
