## Meaning

\! (exclamation mark, add emphasis, denote new information being imparted)

The bus is coming\!

## Discussion

### 1 — Amatuka

Generally not to be used to 'out group' / 'superiors'.

### 2 — Amatuka

よ <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> is used at the end of the sentence (e.g. a sentence ending particle).<br/>
<br/>
S = Sentence.
