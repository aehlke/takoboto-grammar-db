## Meaning

Whether (it/he/she~) is~~~or not,

Whether it looks good or not,...........

## Notes

### 1 — expandover

This is used with:<br/>
Noun は ともかく Noun は/が.......... <br/>
Also you can add として after ともかく:<br/>
Noun は ともかく[として] Noun は/が..........
