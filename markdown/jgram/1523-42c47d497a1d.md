## Meaning

I think I will (do)

今から銀行へ行こうと思います。/ I think I will go to the bank right now.

## Notes

### 1 — 赤毛

Verb in volitional form (意向形)+と+思う is used to inform the interlocutor of the speaker's intention.<br/>
思う/思います is used to express this intention in the moment the speaker is speaking.<br/>
思っている/思っています is used to express that the decision of the speaker have been taken sometimes ago, but is still valid now.<br/>
と思っている may be use to express the intention of a third person.
