## Meaning

on the other hand, on the other side, one-way

It's one-way traffic only on this street.

## Notes

### 1 — dc

一方 seems to be used at the start of sentences whereas <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> seems more used to separate two clauses.<br/>
<br/>
