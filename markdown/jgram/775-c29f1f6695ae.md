## Meaning

? (basic question particle)

Is this your book?

## Notes

### 1 — Sakuratree

か has many uses but it can be used as a question mark, just like what we have in English:<br/>
<br/>
Did he eat dinner?<br/>
彼は夕方ご飯を食べましたか。<br/>
<br/>
Did he go to Japan last year?<br/>
来年彼は日本に行きましたか。
