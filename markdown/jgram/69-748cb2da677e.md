## Meaning

since; ever since; from here on

Ever since graduation, I haven't met with any of my classmates.

## Notes

### 1 — rubyhatchet

「Ａて以来、Ｂ」: B should be a continuous action, current circumstances, or a habit.<br/>
<br/>
FORMATION:<br/>
Ｖて + 以来

### 6 — tigert

Hey Dr Dave バラ　is the word for rose。　the kanji after it is the kanji for いろ or color. the kanji that duplicates the first sound as in <br/>
”いろいろ”　is 々. 　色々 or 時々　or 様々.<br/>
but バラバラ is a 擬音　or sound effect thats used to represent a word an action etc...<br/>
<br/>
other good examples are.<br/>
どきどき　＝　緊張する<br/>
むしむし　＝　hot and humid<br/>
チンする　＝　Eating a microwave dinner<br/>
ワワする　＝　込んでいる　or 煩いところ<br/>
さらさら　＝　rustling; murmuring; fluently<br/>
パンパン　＝　腫れている､Swollen<br/>
ふわふわ　＝　I am not sure exactly what this means, but 変態な意味かも so be careful using it<br/>
フーア　＝　急にとか､驚いたとか<br/>
<br/>
I know there are more, but thats all I can think of now. <br/>
<br/>
If you want to sound natural when speaking japanese. By all means learn the 擬音<br/>
