## Meaning

toward; to; in contrast to; whereas; in regard to; in; per

Up until now, Japan has taken a closed policy toward foreign countries.

## Notes

### 1 — 誠

に対して　：１．〜に、〜へ、〜に向けて<br/>
　　　　　　　　（動作の対象、方向を示す）<br/>
               (towards, to) <br/>
　　　　　　 ２．対比を表す<br/>
              (in contrast, in comparison)

### 2 — rubyhatchet

＊「Ｎに対して」 is used to express someone's attitude/action toward someone/something as in example <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=nitaishite#">#</a></b>; contrast/comparison as in example <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=nitaishite#">#</a></b>; confrontation as in example <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=nitaishite#">#</a></b>; interest as in example <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=nitaishite#">#</a></b>; and the sense of 'per' as in example <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=nitaishite#">#</a></b>.<br/>
<br/>
＊「Ｎ1 に対して Ｎ2」 means 'N2 in regard to N1," as in example <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=nitaishite#">#</a></b> (although, the 'a' in American should be capitalized). It can also mean 'against' as exemplified by example <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=nitaishite#">#</a></b>.<br/>
<br/>
＊「Ｎに対して」 and 「Ｎのに対して」 are different in meaning. The latter means 'whereas' (see example <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=nitaishite#">#</a></b>), but the former doesn't.<br/>
<br/>
FORMATION:<br/>
Ｎ + に対し(て)<br/>
Ｖ(plain form) + に対し(て)<br/>
いＡ + に対し(て)<br/>
なＡ(な/である/だった/であった) + のに対して<br/>
Ｎ1 + に(対する/対しての) + Ｎ2

## Discussion

### 1 — sagittarius

It seems that there are two basic meanings of に対して. First に対して by itself, which means 'regarding' or 'towards' (<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=nitaishite#">#</a></b>) and のに対して, which means  "whereas" (<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=nitaishite#">#</a></b>). do you think the two forms deserve separate entries?
