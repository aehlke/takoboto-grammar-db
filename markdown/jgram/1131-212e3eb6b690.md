## Meaning

while; during; as

みるみるうちに太った。/ He gained weight just while I was watching him.

## Notes

### 1 — dc

うち　（内） means inside. so this is "inside the time" or while.

### 2 — minutiae

I found this helpful:<br/>
うちに indicates a period of time WITHIN WHICH an action occurs, and corresposnds to English "while still", or in negative constructions "before".<br/>
<br/>
It is different to あいだ, which indicates a period DURING OR THROUGHOUT WHICH an action occurs, and is equivalent to English "while".<br/>
<br/>
学生のうちに、よく勉強ひておきなさい。<br/>
Study hard while you are still a student.<br/>
<br/>
Different to: 学生の間に、いろんな所を、旅行するつもりです。　I plan to travel to lots of places during the time I am a student.<br/>
<br/>
うちに is also used to indicate an event building up or reaching a climax AS some action is being done.<br/>
<br/>
先生の話を聞いているうちに、眠くなってきた。<br/>
I grew tired as I listened to the teacher speak.<br/>

### 3 — Chad

Another way to keep うちに and あいだ apart is:<br/>
<br/>
うちに: An abstract timespan with diffuse start and end time; e.g. childhood, rainy season<br/>
<br/>
あいだ: A timespan where you can pinpoint start and end time; e.g. lecture, summer holiday<br/>

### 7 — 赤毛

Minutiaeさん&gt; Please correct the notes:<br/>
勉強ひて-&gt;勉強して as in ex<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=uchini#">#</a></b>.<br/>
Why are they 2 different grammar entry うちに+うちに-2?<br/>
In my point of view:<br/>
One use of うちに describe a progressive change in the 2 clauses before and after うちに. This use is associated with progressive form of verbs like Vテ+行く(change in the future) and Vテ+来る(change in the past) or sudden change like Vイ+出す and Vイ+始める. In this use, うちに cannot be substituted by あいだに. These examples like ex <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=uchini#">#</a></b> are the best for <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b>.<br/>
The other use of うちに make an opposition between  a temporary state in the clause before うちに which permits the clause after うちに, and the end of this temporary clause when it will be too late. This use should be in <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> but there are various example here in <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b>.<br/>
I think it will be great to rearrange these 2 entry. So I moved Ex <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=uchini#">#</a></b> from here to <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=uchini#">#</a></b> of <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> and I changed it with the example from the notes which fits better.
