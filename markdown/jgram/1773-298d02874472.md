## Meaning

(A type of question mark) 質問に

Will you go as far as Kita Senju?

## Notes

### 1 — american57

Equivalent to a question mark. Used mostly in informal male speech. Grammatical only for questions that can be answered "yes/no" (see also "dai"). Unsuitable to ask a superior a question. To the cab driver: "Kita Senju made itte kureru kai?"  Will you go as far as Kita Senju for me?
