## Meaning

V,  asking someone to ask someone else to do something (indirect order)

please ask him to call me

### 3 — dc

actually hmm. it is more often 伝えて as in leaving telephone messages, so could be other verbs too? though they would probably be a limited set around tell/ask/instruct.. but the verb itself is not part of the grammar construct..

### 4 — Amatuka

They're all centred around 'ように'

### 5 — Amatuka

Formed from 'verb + you ni Vte'<br/>
<br/>
V = verb

### 6 — dc

<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> is the same. <br/>
this ends in -te (continuous form) because it is usually finished with -kudasai.<br/>
<br/>
suru you ni itte kudasai - tell to do (please)<br/>
suru you ni iu  - tell to do
