## Meaning

because of too much

心配のあまり =\> because of too much anxiety

## Notes

### 1 — dc

can be used for both good and bad things.<br/>
<br/>
心配のあまり <br/>
because of too much anxiety.<br/>
<br/>
嬉しさのあまりとび上がった。 <br/>
I was so glad I couldnt help jumping up

### 2 — yuenannie

[動-辞書形 / た形；な形-な；名-の]＋あまり
