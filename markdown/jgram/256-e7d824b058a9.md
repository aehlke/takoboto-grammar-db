## Meaning

when

when you fill in the form

## Notes

### 1 — rubyhatchet

Ｖ(plain form) + 際<br/>
Ｎ + の + 際<br/>
Ｎに際して <br/>
この/その/あの際<br/>
<br/>
Be careful with the verb conjugation before 際. Only use Ｖinf・nonpast or Ｖinf・past, which indicate incompletion or completion, respectively. For example, 買う/買った + 際(に).<br/>
<br/>
Also, Ｎに際して is more formal than Ｎ + の + 際. Ｎに際して must be used when speaking about a very, very special occasion.<br/>
<br/>
One final note: 〜際に is a formal expression that is often used with Sino-Japanese words. In sentences in which neither a Sino-Japanese verb/noun nor a honorific-polite verb is used, 際に is unacceptable.

## Discussion

### 1 — Amatuka

Generally 際に.

### 2 — halx

Has the meaning of ~時 or ~場合.  際は also used.

### 3 — skinny trash

is this used mainly in writing or something? I can't say I see it too often..

### 4 — Miki

This would be used mainly in writing or say 時/場合 in an official tone.<br/>
この際 konosai is often used.

### 5 — Pan

In some cases, 際に is read "ぎわ," such as in 彼は窓際に座っている. "He is sitting by the window." Here it means, "by".

### 6 — bamboo4

際 also has the connotation of "at the juncture of ...."

### 7 — Demian

Example :<br/>
家を建てる際は、不動産屋には大変世話になりました。<br/>
or<br/>
家を建てる際に、不動産屋には大変世話になりました。<br/>
It seems the first one is correct but I wonder why...
