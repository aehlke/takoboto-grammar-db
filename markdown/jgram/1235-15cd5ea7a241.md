## Meaning

Even though; Even if;  たとえ〜ても ・ 〜でも

Even if you are busy, it is not good not to eat.

## Notes

### 1 — dc

use it like this<br/>
<br/>
(although A) -nishiro- (you should not B)<br/>
(poor) -nishiro- (you should not steal)<br/>
<br/>
often <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> (even) is used as a prefix.

### 2 — rubyhatchet

FORMATION:<br/>
Ｖ(plain form) + にしろ・にせよ<br/>
いＡ + にしろ・にせよ<br/>
なＡ・Ｎ + (である) + にしろ・にせよ

### 3 — leonardo.stylish

I ordered the entries ni "nishiro,niseyo,nishitemo" and "nishiro,niseyo,nishitemo-2" and which were a little of a mess.<br/>
<br/>
I differentiated them since they had opposite different meanings and there's and I think there is an extra one (ni shitemo) which is not needed, since it equals the same as the "nishiro,niseyo,nishitemo". I request to unify them in such a case.

### 4 — leonardo.stylish

接続：<br/>
[動詞 ・ い刑 ・ な刑 ・ 名] の普通刑 + にしろ]<br/>
<br/>
*ただし[な刑]と[名]の「だ」はつかない。[な刑-である][名-である]も使う
