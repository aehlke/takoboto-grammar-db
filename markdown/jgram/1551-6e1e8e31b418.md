## Meaning

I'm not sure if it is \[possible/so\] but...

It's possible that an expert could do this but there I no way I can do it.

## Notes

### 1 — blabby

名詞句 + ならいざ知らず, B

## Discussion

### 1 — blabby

You can also say ならともかく which has the same meaning.

### 2 — 誠

ex<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=naraizashirazu#">#</a></b> ものではない　means : " shouldn't". So,I feel that it may be わけがない　(impossible, by no means) or ことができない

### 3 — blabby

&gt;誠<br/>
Thanks for your great contributions to this site!<br/>
Yes you are correct that わけがない and ことができない are good translations for "by no means"/"impossible". <br/>
<br/>
However, this example uses "読める" which is like saying "できるものではない" rather than just "ものではない". Therefore I stand by my translation.

### 4 — otenba

You can also put 「は」 or 「だったら」 before 「いざしらず」, not only 「なら」.
