## Meaning

(In mathematics), roughly "is/equals"

2 plus 3 is?  Ni tasu san wa? 二足す三は？

## Notes

### 1 — Rinji\_HalfElf

This is from my Barron's Japanese Vocabulary Book.<br/>
<br/>
It appears as though the function is similar to -i adjective, where desu is dropped. Or desu is not needed due to the operators plain verb form.<br/>
<br/>
That or "ha" functions opposite of contrast in this case, and functions as "comparable" as the last example may indicate.
