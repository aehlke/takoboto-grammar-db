## Meaning

As much as; As fast as; To the extent of.

Have as much as you want.

## Notes

### 1 — dc

Has no negative form. Dake no is always used before a noun.<br/>
<br/>
see <a href="http://www.geocities.com/easykanji/jlptgrammar.html">http://www.geocities.com/easykanji/jlptgrammar.html</a>
