## Meaning

(often it'd be better to do \_\_) Such-and-such an option is something

You'd better study then.

## Notes

### 1 — Roxsan

Format: ta-form of verb or nonpast, negative form of verb + ほうがいい（です）<br/>

## Discussion

### 1 — Amatuka

Two forms, <br/>
"<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> no hou ga ___" (e.g. ３月１日の 方が 都合がよい)<br/>
"<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> no hou ga ___" (e.g.  電話した方が安い)<br/>
If the verb is positive it is usually <b>past tense</b>, even if you are talking about actions to be taken in the future.<br/>
If the verb is negative it does not need to be put into past tense.

### 2 — Amatuka

Several forms, <br/>
"<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> no hou ga ___" (e.g. ３月１日の 方が 都合がよい)<br/>
"<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> na hou ga ___" (e.g. 着実な 方が 競争に勝つ)<br/>
"<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> no hou ga ___" (e.g. 電話した方が安い)<br/>
If the verb is positive it is usually past tense, even if you are talking about actions to be taken in the future.<br/>
If the verb is negative it does not need to be put into past tense.
