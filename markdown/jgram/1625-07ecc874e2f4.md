## Meaning

There are times when 動作・状態がときどき起こるという意味

there are times when Father cooks dinner

## Notes

### 1 — claytonian

made of the dictionary form of a verb plus koto ga aru<br/>
辞書形+ことがある

### 2 — illegalwaffles

-ta form + koto ga aru/arimasu means "ever" or "once"<br/>
-ta form + koto ga nai/arimasen means "never" or "not once"<br/>
<br/>
Affirmative:<br/>
I once studied Spanish.<br/>
スペイン語を勉強したことがある。<br/>
<br/>
Negative:<br/>
I've never studied Spanish.<br/>
スペイン語を勉強したことがない。

## Discussion

### 1 — tigert

wouldn`t this translate a little better as `there are times when`, sometimes already has a word 時々<br/>
<br/>
and since the grammar point uses  the existance verb ある, including it in the translation makes it a little easier to remember.  <br/>
<br/>
I hope you don`t mind but I made some changes to the above examples.<br/>
<br/>
nosebleed is a noun so it requires a preposition article<br/>
"solution way" is redundant because a solution is a way<br/>
相談する means to consult, 会話する means to conversate.<br/>
研究室 translates to research room. of course it can also mean the professor`s office, but one would never say "at your professor`s office"
