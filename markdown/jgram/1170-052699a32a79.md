## Meaning

Ab ~seems (of somebody else's emotions & desires)

He (looks like) he wants a new car.

## Notes

### 1 — aitri

- Formed by removing the い from i-adjectives and replacing it with がる <br/>
- がる congugates as a normal Regular Group 1 / godan verb.<br/>
- Formed from adjective base + garu<br/>
<br/>
(Amataku)<br/>
