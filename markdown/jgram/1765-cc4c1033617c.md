## Meaning

An easy method to conjugating verbs into te form.

Kau (to buy)→ kaimasu→katte

## Notes

### 1 — RyukyuBoss

In regular I(ru-verbs) verbs such as 食べる one simply needs to drop the る and add て.<br/>
In regular II(u-verbs) verbs such as 買う one then can conjugate the verb into it's ます form. From there you simply follow the chart below.<br/>
<br/>
e.g. <br/>
買う→買[い]ます→買って<br/>
書く→書[き]ます→書いて<br/>
勉強する→勉強[し]ます→勉強して<br/>
遊ぶ→遊[び]ます→遊んで<br/>
<br/>
Irregulars: 来て、して<br/>
<br/>
※This can be sung to help you remember, use the tune of "London Bridge is Falling Down"※<br/>
<br/>
い,ち,り→って<br/>
き→いて<br/>
し→して<br/>
ぎ→いで<br/>
に,み,び→んで<br/>
い,ち,り→って<br/>
に,み,び→んで　<br/>
________________________________________________________________<br/>
To make a verb past tense simply change て to た.<br/>
