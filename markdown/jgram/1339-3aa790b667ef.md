## Meaning

let alone B; not to mention; of course A but also B;  は 言うまでもない;  はもちろん; 「AはおろかBも/さえ/まで」のように強調して表現されることが多い。

He can't read hiragana let alone kanji.

## Notes

### 1 — 誠

〜はおろか：〜というだけでは不十分で．．．も、<br/>
　　　　　　 〜はもちろん

### 2 — LR

<b>Ref <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=haoroka#">#</a></b></b> Kanzen Master Level 1 - p45 - no.44

### 3 — LR

<b>Ref <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=haoroka#">#</a></b></b> A Dictionary of Advanced Japanese Grammar p694

### 4 — LR

A phrase that is used when s.t. is not only true with one thing or person, but also with another thing or person that is less expected or understandable

### 6 — 誠

〜はおろか　is more strong/ have a strong emphasis or feeling than 〜はもちろん　and 〜はもとより. In Chinese (何只是〜／那只是〜／別説〜、就連．．．）, it is translated as " it is of course not just that 〜, but also... " or "... , not to mention 〜"
