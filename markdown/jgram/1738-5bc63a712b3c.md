## Meaning

In examples such as "Without A, I can't do B" where, without Action A, there is no possibility of B 〜（し）ないで、Aの動作をしないでは、Bの可能性はないという内容を表す

If you don't obtain the permission of the teacher in charge, you cannot change your registered classes
