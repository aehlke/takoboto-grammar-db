## Meaning

because: a reason for or to do  (x).

（私は日本にいきまして、）なぜなら、三人の友達が日本にいたからです。I went to japan, because I had three friends living there.

## Discussion

### 1 — blabby

The example for this grammar is strange Japanese.<br/>
<br/>
日本に行きました - I went to Japan<br/>
三人の友達 - Three friends<br/>
<br/>
なぜなら is used to explain the reason why a certain action was talken. It is often used in speeches and lectures and almost always paired with 〜からだ。
