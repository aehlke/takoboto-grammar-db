## Meaning

keep on doing ...

keep on eating (sweet stuff)

## Notes

### 1 — maittana

Take the root of the masu-form and replace masu with "tsuzukeru" in any conjugation<br/>
<br/>
example: taberu<br/>
tabemasu -&gt; tabe -&gt; tabetsuzukeru, tabetsuzukenai

## Discussion

### 1 — your name

'dsu' is not a generally used romaji variant.<br/>
I think it should be 'du'.
