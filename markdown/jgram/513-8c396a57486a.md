## Meaning

as well as; with; along with; at the same time; when; as; while

As I'm recovering from my illness, my appetite is coming back.

## Notes

### 1 — Viola

There are several usages:<br/>
<br/>
A<br/>
meaning: with<br/>
form:    noun + totomoni<br/>
eg. Oshyougatsu ha kazoku totomoni sugoshitai.<br/>
    I want to spend New Year (party) with my family.<br/>
<br/>
B<br/>
meaning: at the same time<br/>
form:    verb (dict. form) + totomoni<br/>
         i adj. + totomoni<br/>
         noun + de aru + totomoni<br/>
         na adj. + de aru + totomoni<br/>
eg.Kobayashisan ha gakusei wo shidou suru totomoni, kenkyuu nimo chikara wo ireteiru.<br/>
   Mr. Kobayashi coaches the students and puts his strenght in making some researches at the same time.<br/>
<br/>
   Kono seihin no kaihatsu ha, konnan de aru totomoni, hiyou ga kakaru.<br/>
   Regarding the development of this product, it is difficult and needs money at the same time.<br/>
<br/>
C<br/>
meaning: is used when a change leads to another one.<br/>
form:    verb (dict. form) + totomoni<br/>
         noun + totomoni <br/>
eg. Toshi wo toru totomoni, tairyoku ga otoroeru.<br/>
    As you become older, your physical strength becomes weaker.<br/>
<br/>

### 2 — rubyhatchet

＊The meaning of とともに changes depending on the sentence pattern and context.<br/>
<br/>
1) 「ＡはＢとともにＣ」: when A and B share the common property C, とともに means "as well as." (See example <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=totomoni#">#</a></b>.)<br/>
<br/>
2) 「ＡはＢとともにＣ」: when A and B are the actors of the action C, とともに means "with; along with." (See example <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=totomoni#">#</a></b>.)<br/>
<br/>
3) When とともに is preceded by a noun representing an action or event, とともに means either "at the same time as; when" or "as; with" depending on the event or action represented by the noun. (See example <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=totomoni#">#</a></b>.)<br/>
<br/>
4) When とともに is preceded by a verb, とともに means "as; while." (See example <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=totomoni#">#</a></b>.)<br/>
<br/>
5) When とともに is preceded by Ｎ・なＡ(stem) + である, とともに means "as well as." (See example <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=totomoni#">#</a></b>.)<br/>
<br/>
FORMATION:<br/>
Ｖ(dict. form) + とともに<br/>
Ｎ・なＡ(stem) + である + とともに<br/>
いＡ + とともに<br/>
Ｎ + とともに

## Discussion

### 1 — dc

when a verb comes before it, とともに means "as" - As I am recovering...

### 2 — dc

when a noun comes before, とともに means "as well as" -<br/>
As well as Tokyo...

### 3 — Miki

A noun case is OK.<br/>
と共に of 病気が回復すると共に can be replaced by ni turete.<br/>
病気が回復するにつれて、食欲も出てきた。<br/>
風が吹く とともに/につれて、雲が出てきた。<br/>
As it blows, clouds begin to appear.

### 4 — Miki

Verb + と共に　means <br/>
 at the same time.

### 5 — ppmohapatra

Can we conclude that<br/>
if there is little time difference then we will use ni turete<br/>
and<br/>
When there is hardly any time difference then we will use to tomoni!!

### 6 — akiosama

病気が回復するとともに、食欲が出てきた。I think the english would sound better if it was: "My appetite came back as I recovered from my illness"

### 7 — Nabeshin

From my point of view, the most difficult part of this grammar point is the slim difference in meaning between 「noun+であるとともに」and「noun+とともに」<br/>
<br/>
成長であるとともに妹とけんかが少なくなった。<br/>
While growing up, quarrel with my sister has lessen.<br/>
<br/>
成長とともに妹とけんかが少なくなった。<br/>
As I have grown up, quarrel with my sister has lessen.<br/>
<br/>
(If I am wrong please correct me)<br/>
