## Meaning

according to

according to the news

## Notes

### 1 — dc

(person/source) によると (information)<br/>
according to (person), (information)<br/>
<br/>
彼によると、明日は雨<br/>
According to him, it will rain tomorrow<br/>
<br/>
---<br/>
Don't get confused with によります <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> which means depending on.<br/>
<br/>
天気によっては来る<br/>
depending on the weather, I will come<br/>
<br/>
天気予報によると、明日は雨<br/>
according to the weather report, it will rain tomorrow
