## Meaning

as always

its him, so he will always finish it

## Notes

### 1 — 誠

(名詞)＋〜のことだから:〜という特徴のある．．．だから．．．（〜と予想される）<br/>
<br/>
Therefore, it has the meaning of :<br/>
<br/>
"It is therefore typical of he/she to ....,being (serious, ect) all this while"<br/>
<br/>
"Being a (characteristics,e.g. serious) person, it is typical of he/she to..."<br/>
<br/>
"As it is known for his/her 〜, he/she will therefore .... "<br/>
<br/>
"Known for his/her 〜, he/ she will therefore .... "

### 2 — rubyhatchet

This is the only acceptable use of this grammar:<br/>
Ｎ + の + ことだから<br/>
<br/>
「Ａ(の)ことだから、Ｂ」<br/>
The case where Ａ is a person occurs often. Ｂ predicts or anticipates the nature or usual circumstances of Ａ.

## Discussion

### 1 — 誠

Only ex<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=kotodakara#">#</a></b> fits into this category of grammar, ex <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=kotodakara#">#</a></b>, ex <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=kotodakara#">#</a></b> , ex <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=kotodakara#">#</a></b>  all not examples of this grammar pattern, they are こと(事）　＋　だから。Therefore is :<br/>
<br/>
ex<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=kotodakara#">#</a></b> : そういうこと　＋　だから<br/>
ex<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=kotodakara#">#</a></b> : 大事なこと　＋　だから<br/>
ex<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=kotodakara#">#</a></b> : やったこと　＋　だから<br/>
The above 3 examples, こと can be replaced by 事

### 2 — bamboo4

In modern day Japanese, you can say ことだから without using 事.

### 3 — rubyhatchet

誠 is correct. This grammar only means "as always" when の is used in front of it. The other uses of ことだから, such as the examples that 誠 pointed out, have a different meaning.
