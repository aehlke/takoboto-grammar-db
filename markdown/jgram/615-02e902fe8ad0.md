## Notes

### 1 — natsumi

there are all together 3 forms of verbs in japanese..namely, <br/>
godan, ichidan and irregular verbs...<br/>
<br/>
ichidan verbs are typically verbs that have their stem retained combining with masu, nai, reba, you and te form

### 2 — GanjarHendrian

助ける , 助けます、助けない、助けて、<br/>
<br/>
(v1) to help, to save, to rescue, to give relief to, to spare (life), to reinforce, to promote, to abet, (P)<br/>
