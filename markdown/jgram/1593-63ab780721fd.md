## Meaning

wish

I wish today's weather was as nice as yesterday's

## Notes

### 1 — ziaz

I believe this use of noni is interchangeable with のだけれでも

## Discussion

### 1 — Miki

ziaz, re text, if you say that, it is だけれども instead of だけれでも.

### 2 — james2c19v

I feel like this should be listed as ればいいのに or at least いいのに. I would say that のに within the construction, by itself, is actually functioning within its "in spite of" sense.
