## Meaning

commensurate with,  accordance with,  depending on

exercise according to your fitness level

## Notes

### 1 — dc

に応じて]has these meanings,<br/>
[depending on], [in accordance with], [in answer to].<br/>
<br/>
体力に応じて（depending on）<br/>
（ex）あなたの体力に応じて走るスピードを変えましょう。<br/>
     Depending on your physical fitness,change your running speed.<br/>
<br/>
<br/>
命令に応じて（in accordance with）<br/>
（ex）上司の命令に応じてその計画を変更した。<br/>
      We changed the plan in accordance with boss's instructions.<br/>
<br/>
質問に応じて(in answer to)<br/>
（ex）彼の質問に応じて、彼女はこう言った。<br/>
      She said that in answer to his question.<br/>
   　 「応」＝「答」

### 2 — dc

usage<br/>
<br/>
noun + nioujite <br/>
体力に応じて<br/>
命令に応じて<br/>
<br/>
when with adjectives, you add -sa after the adjective to turn it into a type of noun.<br/>
eg <br/>
　高い  high<br/>
→高さ  high-ness/height<br/>
<br/>
高&lt;b&gt;さ&lt;/b&gt;に応じて    depending on your height<br/>
速&lt;b&gt;さ&lt;/b&gt;に応じて    depending on your speed<br/>
<br/>
This is for true adjectives, which are mostly "na" adjectives. for "-i" adjectives, you often can't use -sa as they dont turn into nouns.<br/>
<br/>
See &lt;a href=<a href="http://www.timwerx.net/language/jpadj/lesson1.htm">http://www.timwerx.net/language/jpadj/lesson1.htm</a>&gt;this page&lt;/a&gt; for an explanation on true/quasi na/i adjectives.

### 3 — dc

に応じて is for things that vary<br/>
<br/>
depending on (noun/variable item), <br/>
change (verb)<br/>
<br/>
depending on (your strength),<br/>
change (the way you you exercise)<br/>
<br/>
depending on (your driving speed),<br/>
it will change (your gas consumption)<br/>
<br/>
of course being japanese the sentence order is backward<br/>
<br/>
(noun/variable) ni oujite, (verb) <br/>
(体力) に応じて (運動する）
