## Meaning

Is diffcult to do 出来るごとが難しいです。

He's difficult to understand, isn't he?

## Notes

### 1 — ninja\_k

formed with Vm + （難い) にくい, making it an i adjective.

### 3 — crbennett

What's the difference between むずかしい and にくい?

### 4 — Groove

The different is that 難い is added to a verb stem to change it to an adjective, whereas 難しい is an existing adjective meaning 'difficult', and thus can't be conjugated to a verb stem.<br/>
<br/>
For example:<br/>
<br/>
That word is difficult to remember (lit. That word's remembering is difficult.)<br/>
その言葉が思い出し難いな。<br/>
<br/>
as compared to:<br/>
<br/>
Remembering that word is difficult (lit. That word's remembering is difficult).<br/>
その言葉は思い出すことが難しいだ。<br/>
<br/>
