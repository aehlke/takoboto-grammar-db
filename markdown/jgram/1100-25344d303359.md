## Meaning

doing things like V+ing Vたり、　Vたり　します。

Yesterday I did things like watching tv and playing video games.

## Notes

### 1 — dc

The <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> form is used when a sequence of verbs occur in a row. The last one cannot be a tari, it needs to conclude the sentence.<br/>
<br/>
normal:<br/>
　食べたり、飲んだり、のんびりしました。<br/>
　Eating and drinking, I relaxed.<br/>
<br/>
neg:<br/>
　読まなかったり、行きたくなかったり、何もしなかったり、悪い人でした。<br/>
　not reading, not going, not doing anything, he was a bad guy.<br/>
<br/>
with adjectives:<br/>
　サービスは良かったり、値段安かったり、、いいんじゃない？<br/>
　good service, low prices... alright eh?<br/>
<br/>
neg with adjectives:<br/>
　サービスが悪かったり、値段が高くなかったり、、、<br/>
　the service is not bad, the prices aren't high...<br/>

### 2 — Ayumi

Is also used fo repeated actions, like:<br/>
Watashi wa nomitari tabetari shimasu.<br/>
(I drank and ate repeatedly)<br/>
You must add "suru/shimasu" at the end.

### 3 — tigert

I changed this grammar note a little. [tari suru]<br/>
form means "doing things like". the Verb takes the plain past tense form IE 食べる become 食べた and then you add the り<br/>
買う will become 買った　then you add the り,etc...<br/>
<br/>
きのう、私は　テレビを見たり、ゲームをしたり　しました。<br/>
