## Meaning

despite

Despite running, I didn't catch the train.

## Notes

### 1 — BB

I think this can better be translated as 'despite' or 'in spite of' or 'even though' rather than 'although' or 'but' as there are many forms of 'but' which is rather confusing.<br/>
<br/>
EG: Even though I dashed down the stairs/ despite dashing down the stairs, I missed the train by one minute.<br/>
