## Meaning

Although it is said that, even though it is said, admitting that, that being said, however, nevertheless 「A とはいえ B」は「Aは事実だが、しかし実際はB」の意味

Even admitting that Jane didn't have much time, I don't believe that she couldn't write a simple report like this

## Notes

### 1 — 赤毛

Same as といっても but:<br/>
といっても is for spoken clauses, and<br/>
とはいえ is for written clauses.<br/>
<br/>
way of use: P1 とはいえ P2.<br/>
P1 is the sentence in question, P2 is the reason why.

### 2 — LR

<b>Ref <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=tohaie#">#</a></b></b> Kanzen Master Level 1 - p28 - no.30

<b>Ref <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=tohaie#">#</a></b></b> A Dictionary of Advanced Japanese Grammar p665

### 4 — LR

とはいえ is primarily used in writing

### 5 — LR

とはいえcan be used at the beginning of a sentence to double back on the previous sentence

		<strong>but having said that, </strong>
		computers are so expensive that I can't buy one.

<a href="http://www.csse.monash.edu.au/~jwb/cgi-bin/wwwjdic.cgi?9MHJ８０才の祖母は、この間階段で転んで足を痛め、歩くのが不自由になってしまった。とはいえ">

### 7906 — LR

<a href="http://www.csse.monash.edu.au/~jwb/cgi-bin/wwwjdic.cgi?9MHJ地球温暖化を防止するための国際会議の結果、京都議定書が作成された。とはいえすべての国がその基準を守るまでには、まだ年月がかかりそうだ。">
				地球温暖化を防止するための国際会議の結果、京都議定書が作成された。<strong>とはいえ</strong>すべての国がその基準を守るまでには、まだ年月がかかりそうだ。<strong></strong></a> <br/>

		<strong>Having said that, </strong>
		it seems like it will take a long time before every country abides by the standards it set out.

<a href="http://www.csse.monash.edu.au/~jwb/cgi-bin/wwwjdic.cgi?9MHJ今度の学会が楽しみだ。とは言え、まだ発表準備は全然できていない。">

		<strong>That being said, </strong>
		I haven't prepared for my presentation at all.
