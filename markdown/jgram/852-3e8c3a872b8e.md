## Meaning

with effort

I took the trouble to go and see him, but he wasn't there

## Examples

### 1068 — Amatuka

<a href="http://www.csse.monash.edu.au/~jwb/cgi-bin/wwwjdic.cgi?9MHJせっかく会いに行った">
<strong>せっかく</strong>会いに行った<strong>のに、</strong>友達はいませんでした。</a> <br/>
<strong>I took the effort </strong>
		to go and see my friend, but he wasn't there

### 1069 — Amatuka

<a href="http://www.csse.monash.edu.au/~jwb/cgi-bin/wwwjdic.cgi?9MHJせっかくいい大学に入った">
<strong>せっかく</strong>いい大学に入った<strong>のだから</strong>よく勉強するつもりです。</a> <br/>
<strong>I worked hard </strong>
		to get into a good college, so I intend to study hard

### 6201 — euphoriafish

<a href="http://www.csse.monash.edu.au/~jwb/cgi-bin/wwwjdic.cgi?9MHJ人がせっかく作ってくれた料理を食べないのは失礼だ。">
				人が<strong>せっかく</strong>作ってくれた料理を食べないのは失礼だ。<strong></strong></a> <br/>
		It's impolite not to eat food which someone has taken the trouble of preparing for you.  (from An Integrated Approach to Intermediate Japanese) 
		<strong> </strong>

### 7008 — hua

<a href="http://www.csse.monash.edu.au/~jwb/cgi-bin/wwwjdic.cgi?9MHJせっかく彼女に手紙を書いたのに、送るのを忘れちゃった。">
<strong>せっかく</strong>彼女に手紙を書いたのに、送るのを忘れちゃった。<strong></strong></a> <br/>
		I took the effort to write a letter to her and forgot to send it. 
		<strong> </strong>

### 8601 — cletcher

<a href="http://www.csse.monash.edu.au/~jwb/cgi-bin/wwwjdic.cgi?9MHJせっかく母親が買ってきたシャツをどうしても着ようしなかった。">
<strong>せっかく</strong>母親が買ってきたシャツをどうしても着ようしなかった。<strong></strong></a> <br/>
		After all of the trouble my mother went to buy the shirt, I did not wear it. 
		<strong> </strong>
