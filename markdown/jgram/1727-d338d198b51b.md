## Meaning

of course, expected result 〜（の）は当り前だ　 〜（の）は当然の結果だ

Of course it's cold. ;  As you would expect...

## Notes

### 1 — lenzras

The speaker's conclusion obtained through deductive, logical judgement or calculation on the basis of what he has heard or read.<br/>
<br/>
Formation<br/>
<br/>
(i) {V / Adj} (i) inf わけ　だ<br/>
　　｛話す・話した｝わけ　だ　(I take it that s.o. talks / talked)<br/>
　　｛食べる・食べた｝　わけ　だ (I take it that s.o. eats / ate)<br/>
　　｛高い・高かった｝わけ　だ (I take it that s.o. is expensive / was expensive)<br/>
<br/>
(ii) Adj (na) stem ｛な・だった｝わけ　だ<br/>
　　｛静かな・静かだった｝わけ　だ　(I take it that s.o. is / was quiet)<br/>
<br/>
(iii) N　｛という・だった｝わけ　だ<br/>
　　｛先生という・先生だった｝わけ　だ (I take it that s.o. is / was a teacher)
