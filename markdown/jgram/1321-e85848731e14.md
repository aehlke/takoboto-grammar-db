## Meaning

absolutely not...

I am not going to give up

## Notes

### 1 — hana

強い否定を表す。<br/>
Expresses strong denial or rejection.

### 2 — yara

Formation: <br/>
V  <br/>
Aい<br/>
Aな ＋ものか/もんか/ものですか/もんですか<br/>
Nな
