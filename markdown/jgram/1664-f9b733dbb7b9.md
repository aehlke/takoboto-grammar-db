## Meaning

BEGIN TO〜；BE ABOUT TO DO〜；ALMOST　DO〜

死にかかった= almost died

## Notes

### 1 — deeana

VBryk（ます）<br/>
→　an event is about to take place<br/>
→　the VB is usually intransitive and has to be a Non-volitional, punctual VB<br/>
→　can be replaced by KAKERU without a substantial change of meaning<br/>
　　　　死にかかった＝faced death<br/>
　　　　死に掛けた＝was half dead<br/>
<br/>
(Punctual VBs: <br/>
：a Punctual VB with the auxiliary VB いる expresses a repeated action or a state after an action was taken or something took place:<br/>
  知る（=&gt;知っている）(get to know after studying)<br/>
　死ぬ（=&gt;死んでいる）(die after living)<br/>
　忘れる（=&gt;忘れている）(as a result of the passage of time)<br/>
　飛ぶ、乗る、起きる、結婚する、困る,...  ）<br/>
(Non-volitional VBs:<br/>
：a Non-volitional VB usually does not take the voltional form, the imperative form and the potential form.<br/>
-Non-volitional VBs are classified into  emotive VBs and non-emotive VBs<br/>
a) emotive VBs: 喜ぶ、悲しむ、好む、怒る、嫌う、苦しむ、…<br/>
b).non-emotive VBｓ：　出来る、要る、知る、見える、聞こえる、似合う、違う、疲れる、分かる）<br/>
