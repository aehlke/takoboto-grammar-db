## Meaning

Show a strong feeling of opposition.

I would much rather...

## Examples

<a href="http://www.csse.monash.edu.au/~jwb/cgi-bin/wwwjdic.cgi?9MHJ友達を傷つけるくらいなら、自分が我慢">
