## Meaning

as it is, leave as it is, (remain) unchanged

I'll leave this place as it is.

## Notes

### 1 — Nihonjin

Vinf+past <strong>mama</strong><br/>
Adj(i) <strong>mama</strong><br/>
Adj(na)stem <strong>na mama</strong><br/>
N <strong>no mama</strong>
