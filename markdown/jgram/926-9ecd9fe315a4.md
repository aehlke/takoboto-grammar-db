## Meaning

while doing A, do B

I went to the shop on the way home

## Notes

### 1 — dc

Since doing one thing anyway, do something else at the same time.<br/>
<br/>
<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> since, anyway doing A, do B<br/>
郵便局へ行く<b>ついでに</b>、この手紙を出してくれる？<br/>
= since you're going to the post office <b>anyway</b>, can you mail this letter for me?<br/>
The most casual form in daily use.<br/>
<br/>
<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> = on the opportunity of A, did B<br/>
more formal and used written, but similar meaning to tsuideni<br/>
大統領が中国に 行ったの を契機に  万里の長城を訪問しておりました。<br/>
= When the president visited China, he took the chance to visit the Great Wall.<br/>
<br/>
<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> = implies more cause and effect. Often used when a major event occurs and gives you a chance to also do something else:<br/>
子供が生まれたの をきっかけに  タバコを止めました<br/>
= Since I had kids, I took the opportunity to quit smoking<br/>
= as a trigger<br/>

### 2 — dc

It’s easy to confuse <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> with <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b>. <br/>
<br/>
<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b>, both actions are of equal importance, <br/>
<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> implies doing one thing, and then just adding something else at the same time, "since its there anyway"

### 3 — dc

<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> implies combining, not just time-related. <br/>
combined the sightseeing and business trip<br/>
mixing work and pleasure

### 4 — dc

<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> is similar to <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> but more formal
