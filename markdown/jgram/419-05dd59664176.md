## Meaning

on the one hand . . . \[can also mean "on the other hand"\]

On the one hand, you are right . . .

### 1 — Amatuka

See also 一方

### 2 — adamstudio

Compare the possible meanings of the following.<br/>
<br/>
一方では <br/>
<br/>
1. (On) the other hand<br/>
2. One side<br/>
3. One way<br/>
4. One direction<br/>
5. One party<br/>
6. Meanwhile<br/>
<br/>
他方では<br/>
<br/>
1. Another side<br/>
2. Different direction<br/>
3. (On) the other hand<br/>
<br/>
<br/>
We can conclude that when both 一方では and 他方では are used together, 一方では is always used first. 一方では can mean both "On the one hand" and "On the other hand" -- but 他方では always suggests "On the other hand."
