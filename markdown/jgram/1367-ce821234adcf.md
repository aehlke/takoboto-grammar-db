## Meaning

If thats not ... I don't know what is

If that's not a secret society, i don't know what is

## Notes

### 1 — dc

always follows a noun. written only

### 2 — 誠

〜でなくてなんだろう literally means : "If it is not 〜,then what it is/ what can it be?"
