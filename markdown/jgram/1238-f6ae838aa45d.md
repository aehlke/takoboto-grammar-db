## Meaning

tend to

he tends to be late

## Notes

### 1 — dc

verb masu stem + gachi<br/>
　遅れます　⇒　遅れがち = tends to be late<br/>
<br/>
noun + gachi<br/>
　怒りがち = tends to anger (get angry)<br/>
<br/>
note: many verbs can be turned into nouns by using a -ri ending.<br/>
<br/>
　怒る【いかる】(v5r) to get angry, to be angry<br/>
　怒り【いかり】(n) anger, hatred, (P)<br/>
　　⇒　怒りがち

〜がち: tendency; inclination<br/>
This suggests that a situation tends to occur often or easily, as in, 'to be absent often' or 'to become ill easily'. <br/>
Both 〜ぎみ and 〜がち are almost always used in a neagtive sense.
