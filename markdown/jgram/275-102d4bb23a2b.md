## Meaning

(It's been) decided; expected; definitely

He'll definitely lose the match.

## Notes

### 1 — rubyhatchet

＊This is a strong way of saying something clearly.<br/>
<br/>

いＡ + にきまっている<br/>
なＡ + にきまっている
