## Meaning

it's already too late

It's too late to say you're sorry\!

## Notes

### 1 — dc

this is used like<br/>
[ima goro ni natte] <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b>+mo [too late]

## Examples

### 3003 — dc

<a href="http://www.csse.monash.edu.au/~jwb/cgi-bin/wwwjdic.cgi?9MHJ今ごろになって勝訴して">

		Even though we won the lawsuit, 
		<strong>it's too late </strong>
		to bring the dead back.
