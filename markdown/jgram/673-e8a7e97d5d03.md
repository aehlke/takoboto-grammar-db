## Discussion

### 1 — Amatuka

Formed from noun (or noun phrase?) + ijou<br/>
N = noun

### 2 — Miki

500g以上 : 500 grams and over<br/>
500g未満 : less than 500 grams<br/>
500g以下 : 500 grams and below
