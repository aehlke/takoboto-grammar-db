## Meaning

fake, pretending

it seems like he is faking his injury

## Notes

### 1 — tigert

I ran into this grammar in a song I was translating.<br/>
<br/>
it is a set phrase, using わざと　meaning intentionaly and らしい it appears or it seems. to imply that what ever the case may be, it is being faked. <br/>
<br/>
this is the kind of Japanese that is best wispered that spoken. 使ったら気を付けってね
