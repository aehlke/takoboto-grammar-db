## Discussion

### 1 — blabby

Does anyone know the difference in nuance between 依存(いぞん), 依頼(いらい) and 頼る(たよる).<br/>
I think 依頼 involves requesting (maybe to a superior) while 依存 is maybe a little stronger? Usually I hear に頼ってる in casual speech.<br/>
<br/>
By the way, should the Japanaese title be changed to に依存? The ro-maji and Kanji don't match.

### 2 — bamboo4

依頼 means "to request."<br/>
依存 means "to rely upon"<br/>
頼る means "to depend on" or "count on"<br/>
依存 is stronger than 頼る
