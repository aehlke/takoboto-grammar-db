## Meaning

Vru,  (negative command - sentence ending particle)

Don't do that\!

## Notes

### 1 — yutenji

Group 1: Change the last sound of the ます-form into the え sound. E.g. やすみ「ます」-&gt;　やすめ<br/>
Group 2: Append ろ to the ます-form. E.g. ねろ / 寝ろ<br/>
Group 3: します-&gt;しろ   きます-&gt;こい<br/>
<br/>
Quite 'rough' speech, normally only used by men or those in senior positions.

### 2 — FekketCantenel

This construct expresses a rough negative command ("Don't you dare do X!"). It takes the [dictionary form] of the verb and appends 〜な to it.

## Discussion

### 1 — Amatuka

Formed from dictionary form verb + na<br/>
<br/>
Vru = 'Dictionary' form verb (e.g. 歩く、する、いる)

### 2 — hassan

ふざけるな！　(they often say it in anime etc. means<br/>
something like... how to say it... 'stop that!',<br/>
'don't make me a fool')

### 3 — anon

ふせげるな？

### 4 — Miki

We don't say ふせげるな! but we say ふせぐな！ 防ぐ+な<br/>
ふざける is 'Dictionary' form verb and not potential form. ふざけるな can be also said as ふざけるんじゃない

### 5 — 誠

ふざけるな　or ふざけないで meaning : " don't/ stop messing around and behave yourself"<br/>
ふざける = basic meaning is " to play around", "to mess about" but can also mean " to talk nonsense".
