## Meaning

based on 「いか(如何)に」の略。どのようであろうか。

based on the exam results

## Notes

### 1 — dc

〜いかん　is the same as によって<br/>
<br/>
Noun(の)＋いかん

### 2 — dc

Look at this subtle difference:<br/>
<br/>
１　結果いかんで、次のレベルを決める<br/>
　　based on the result, the next level will be decided<br/>
<br/>
２　結果いかんで&lt;b&gt;は&lt;/b&gt;、次のレベルに行ける<br/>
　　based on the result, you will go to the next level<br/>
<br/>
in (1) a clear result comes out, so the で particle is used.<br/>
in (2) a process will start, so では is used to link the two parts.<br/>
<br/>
もっとはっきり説明を聞きたい。<br/>
<br/>

### 3 — dc

Negative case "without regard to"<br/>
<br/>
　いかんによらず<br/>
　いかんにかかわらず<br/>
　いかんを問わず

### 4 — bob

As far as I know, いかんで means that one thing depends on another. i.e. We'll decide what to do depending on the results. <br/>
<br/>
いかんでは　means that something has already happened, so the speaker is using those results to predict/suggest something else . i.e. Looking at these results we should do this.

### 5 — 誠

〜いかんだ　: Based on how/whether 〜<br/>
<br/>
名詞　＋　いかんだ

### 6 — 誠

成績いかんで、大学に入学できるよ。<br/>
Depending on the results, it is possible to enter university.<br/>
<br/>
成績いかんでは、大学に入れないこともあるよ<br/>
Depending on the results, it is possible(there are times where one) not being able to enter university.<br/>

### 9 — LR

いかん is a classic form of the adverb  どのように  "in what way"<br/>
It's written / formal Japanese

### 10 — blutorange

いかん is an abbreviation of いかに, which is 如何 + the particle に. 如何 is a classical word meaning どのよう・どういうふう. I think this should make remembering this word and its meaning easier.
