## Meaning

An unforseen change that occured during a described time frame. はじめは予想しなかったような変化が現すことを表す。

一週間の外国出張のうちに、近くの公園の桜が全部散ってしまった。Ｗhile overseas on a one-week business trip, all of the cherry blossoms in the nearby park had fallen.
