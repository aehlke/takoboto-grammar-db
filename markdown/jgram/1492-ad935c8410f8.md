## Meaning

at (time / momen) 期限を表す。〜で。

today's business will close at 7

## Notes

### 1 — deltafour

[名]＋をもって

### 2 — deltafour

This is a formal expression.

### 4 — LR

<b>On the difference between <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> and <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b></b><br/>
<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> is a Time phrase<br/>
meaning "at"  - marks beginnings, ends, or borders between times.<br/>
It is often used as a greeting to お客 with the extended form  をもちまして.<br/>
<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b> is the method by which you do something - victory achieved via effort, announcement made via a blackboard.<br/>
<br/>
Both are formal, both are NOUN + をもって<br/>
be careful.
