## Meaning

I'm not sure if it is \[possible/so\] but...

It's possible that an expert could do this, but I can't.

## Notes

### 1 — blabby

名詞句 + ならともかく, B

## Examples

### 5803 — blabby

<a href="http://www.csse.monash.edu.au/~jwb/cgi-bin/wwwjdic.cgi?9MHJ君ならともかく、俺はそんなことをしない。">
				君<strong>ならともかく</strong>、俺はそんなことをしない。<strong></strong></a> <br/>
<strong>Maybe </strong>
		you can do it, but I don't do such a thing.

### 5804 — blabby

<a href="http://www.csse.monash.edu.au/~jwb/cgi-bin/wwwjdic.cgi?9MHJ外人ならともかく、日本人はそんなことは絶対にしない。">

<strong>Maybe </strong>
		it's possible for a foreigner, but Japanese people would never do that.
