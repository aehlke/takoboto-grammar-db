## Meaning

I told you so, Serves you right, See\! 意味：私が言った通りじゃ、自分のせいじゃないか　定義：失敗した様の醜さを知らせ、人の失敗を嘲っていう語。

Person A: Something bad happened to me B: I told you so\! / See\!/ I warned you that might happen/Its your own fault
