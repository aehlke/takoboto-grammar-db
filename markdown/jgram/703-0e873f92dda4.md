## Meaning

Vm,  -ing

even knowing I need exercise, I don't

## Notes

### 1 — dc

masu-stem + tsutsu<br/>
食べつつ<br/>

### 2 — rubyhatchet

〜つつ(1)<br/>
「Ａつつ、Ｂ」: A and B are done simultaneously.<br/>
＊This expression is more "solid" than ながら. See examples <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=tsutsu#">#</a></b>, <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=tsutsu#">#</a></b>, and <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=tsutsu#">#</a></b>.<br/>
FORMATION:<br/>
Ｖ(minus ます) + つつ<br/>
<br/>
〜つつ(2)・つつも<br/>
＊This expression is used alongside negative emotions. The forms used most often include: 「〜と思いつつ」 and 「〜と知りつつ」. See examples <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=tsutsu#">#</a></b>, <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=tsutsu#">#</a></b>, and <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=tsutsu#">#</a></b>.<br/>
FORMATION:<br/>
Ｖ(minus ます) + つつ・つつも
