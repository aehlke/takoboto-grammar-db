## Meaning

goes without saying/not to mention

he speaks vulcan, not to mention geek.

## Notes

### 1 — kanoko

* iu made mo naku is used only in the midle of the phase.<br/>
* iu made mo nai or "iu mo oroka" is used in the end of the phase.<br/>
