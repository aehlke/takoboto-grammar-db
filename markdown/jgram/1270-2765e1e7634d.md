## Meaning

from the standpoint of

from the grammar point of view, chinese is easy

## Notes

### 1 — rochin

noun　+ からいうと<br/>

### 2 — rochin

-からいえば/-からいって　are also used instead of -からいうとbutthe meaning is same <br/>
<br/>
