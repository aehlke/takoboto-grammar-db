## Meaning

If s.o. does s.t for even a moment, the following result will necessarily occur • Once someone starts something, the person can’t stop it 〜したら • いったん〜たらそのまま • いったん〜たら必ず

If the boss even touches  the mic for a second, he won't let anyone else sing karaoke ALL night

## Notes

### 1 — dc

see <a href="http://www.geocities.com/easykanji/jlptgrammar.html">http://www.geocities.com/easykanji/jlptgrammar.html</a><br/>
<br/>
Once someone starts something, the person can’t stop it.<br/>
<br/>
Notes: Easily confused with ~kiri, but the meanings are completely different and ga saigo doesn’t mean ‘for the last time’. Always used with past tense verbs.<br/>
<br/>
