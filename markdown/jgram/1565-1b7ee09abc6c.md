## Meaning

Try to do something, but the nuance is that it was unsuccessful. 試してみるだけで結果がともなわないニュアンスがある。

I tried to kiss her, but she said no.

## Notes

### 1 — nagoya1wan

### 2 — claytonian

uses the conjectural/hortatory form of the verb
