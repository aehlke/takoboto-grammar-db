## Meaning

even though (it is) ; for

its a cold day for June

## Notes

### 1 — hana

Formation:<br/>
<br/>
V/i/na/N[普通形] + にしては<br/>
それにしては<br/>
<br/>
意外な気持ち - An unexpected feeling

## Discussion

### 1 — dc

closer to "wari ni" than "nimokakawarazu"

### 2 — halx

Meaning: contrary to one's (natural) expectation

### 3 — halx

I think the にしてはいけない examples sentences don't belong here.

### 4 — adamstudio

X にしてはいけない must mean, "Even though you are doing X, that's not okay."

### 5 — bamboo4

I think there are two types of にしては mixed up in these examples.<br/>

### 6 — hana

I also agree that the <b>にしてはいけない</b>　examples should be moved.

### 7 — 赤毛

I don't understand "乱魔ﾉ" in [ex <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=nishitewa#">#</a></b>]. Perhaps it means "乱麻の"?<br/>
I don't understand the English translation of [ex <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=nishitewa#">#</a></b>] and [ex <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=nishitewa#">#</a></b>]. <br/>
Perhaps an alternative translation for にしては is despite (Ex: despite being a foreigner, his Japanese is very good. ).
