## Meaning

not just,  limited to

not only women, but also men...

## Notes

### 1 — rubyhatchet

＊に限り means 'limited to' (examples <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=nikagirazu#">#</a></b> and <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=nikagirazu#">#</a></b>) whereas に限らず means 'not limited to; but also' (example <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=nikagirazu#">#</a></b>).<br/>
<br/>
FORMATION:<br/>
Ｎ + に限り・に限らず
