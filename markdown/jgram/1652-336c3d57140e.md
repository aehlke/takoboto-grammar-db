## Meaning

In a flash, before one knows it, in a blink of an eye, in no time, flash by, (time) flies 時間が自分のと思うより早かったの意味です

Time flies when you're having fun

## Notes

### 1 — tigert

I`m not sure if this should be entered as grammar or as vocabulary, but it is a very important saying. Japan has a lot of set phrases and in order to sound natural its best to memorize as many as you can. <br/>
<br/>
あっという間　lit. translates to <br/>
"In the middle of saying あぁ”<br/>
<br/>
before you could finish your sentence time had run out
