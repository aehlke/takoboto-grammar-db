## Discussion

### 1 — Amatuka

Formed from verb -masu base + au.<br/>
Note that au conjugates as a normal godan (regular group 1) verb.<br/>
<br/>
Vm = verb -masu base

### 2 — Amatuka

Seen in lots of dictionary entries, with varying degrees of direct relation to the 'together / mutually' meaning.<br/>
話し合う <b><a href="http://jgram.org:80/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=hanashiau">hanashiau</a></b> for example is to consult or talk over a matter with someone.<br/>
Compare ...<br/>
例の事件をもう話しました。[rei no jiken wo mou hanashimashta.] I've already told about <b>that</b> incident.<br/>
例の事件をもう話しあいました。[rei no jiken wo mou hanashiaimashta.] I've already talked with someone about <b>that</b> incident.
