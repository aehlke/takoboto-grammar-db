## Meaning

AT〜; ON〜

犬が子供に飛び掛った。A dog jumped at the child

## Notes

### 1 — deeana

VBryk (masu)+ kakaru<br/>
- a directed movement works on something/ someone<br/>
<br/>
ex: 道を歩いていたら,男が僕に殴りかかってきた。When I was walking along the street, a man came to hit me.<br/>
<br/>
ex: 花子は太郎の肩にもたれかかった。<br/>
Hanako leaned on Taro's shoulder<br/>
<br/>
ex：壁により掛かるな。 Don't lean against the wall. <br/>
