## Meaning

Regardless of

Regardless of the cost - I must buy it.

## Notes

### 1 — dc

regardless of<br/>
<br/>
can be used with verbs or nouns<br/>
<br/>
N−<br/>
　年齢にかかわらず、、、<br/>
<br/>
V-る　V-ない<br/>
　成功するしないにかかわらず、、、<br/>
<br/>
A-い　A−くない<br/>
　結果の良し悪しにかかわらず、、、

## Discussion

### 1 — halx

also ~に(は)かかわりなく

### 2 — dc

is the "mo" in this optional, or is there a logic to when it is/not used?<br/>
<br/>
それに「も」かかわらず彼女は魅力的だ。<br/>
She is charming for all that.

### 3 — Miki

Mostly, に or にも + かかわらず    I think the "mo" is optional.<br/>
Exception I realized are<br/>
それにもかかわらず<br/>
-た／だ + にもかかわらず　ex<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=nikakawarazu#">#</a></b>, ex<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=nikakawarazu#">#</a></b>, ex<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=nikakawarazu#">#</a></b>

### 4 — dc

the も would seem to be emphasis. <br/>
<br/>
来るにもかかわらず<br/>
"even if they come"<br/>
<br/>
来るにかかわらず<br/>
regardless of whether they come <br/>
来るか来ないかにかかわらず<br/>
whether they come or not<br/>
<br/>

### 5 — blabby

I good translation I use is<br/>
にかかわらず    - Regardless of<br/>
にもかかわらず  - In spite of<br/>
<br/>
雨にもかかわらず出かけました。 Similar to なのに<br/>
In spite of the rain, I went out.<br/>
<br/>
曜日にかかわらず入ることができます。 Similar to を問わず<br/>
Regardless of which day it is, you can enter.
