## Meaning

it's already too late, It's no use doing s.t. now

It's too late to explain why.

## Notes

### 1 — dc

This is usually followed by a negative.<br/>
<br/>
　imasara (verb-ta) tokorode, (verb2-nai)<br/>
　imasara setsumei shita tokorode, yurusanai.<br/>
<br/>
= its too late to explain - you won't be forgiven

### 4 — LR

Kanzen Master has this listed under <strong>~たところで</strong>, which apparently on it's own can also express that the first part of the sentence would be a wasted effort, e.g.<br/>
約束の時間にこんなに遅れては、行ってみ<strong>たところで</strong>、だれもいないだろう<br/>
(what with it being so late after the agreed time, even if I went now, surely there'd be no one there.)<br/>
<br/>
<strong>さらに</strong> really emphasizes how late in the day such an ineffective action would be. Nevertheless, keep in the back of your mind that <strong>たところで</strong> can exist on it's own with a similar meaning.<br/>
On past test papers I've had questions that lead with <strong>さらに</strong> and the correct answer they wanted for the blank that came after was <strong>たところで</strong> so maybe it's worth learning these as a pair, this will also help you distinguish it from [toshitatokorode toshitatte nishitatokorode] etc. which has a slightly different nuance meaning even though the first part of the sentence is NOT a wasted effort, the final result doesn't satisfy.

### 5 — LR

For reference, Dictionary of Advanced Japanese Grammar has this listed under <strong>さらに</strong>, key sentence D

		I'd like to visit the village where I spent my childhood, but 
		<strong>even if I went there now after so long, </strong>
		I guess nothing of the old times would still be there.

## Discussion

### 1 — dc

not sure how this is different from <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE="></a></b>

### 2 — Miki

They are almost the same.　<br/>
今頃になって説明したところで、…。<br/>
今頃になって説明したって、…。

### 3 — Miki

ex<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=imasara-tatokorode#">#</a></b> can be also said as 今さら騒いでもどうにもならないよ。…。

### 4 — Miki

imasara-demo/temo

### 5 — 誠

A better translation would be " Even if (one) had 〜ed now, ... still ..."

### 6 — godfrey

I agree with 誠.  ex<b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=imasara-tatokorode#">#</a></b> translation makes more sense as: "Even if you make a fuss now, nothing can be done (about it).  It's too late!"

### 7 — nickm758

I sometimes hear the slang version without the ところで at the end, for example: 「いまさら言うなよ」
