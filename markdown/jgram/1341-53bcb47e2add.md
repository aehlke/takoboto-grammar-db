## Meaning

not only (A) but also (B) Not only a singular ((problem)) but a wider ((problem))

The trend in declining birth rates is not just Japan, but is similarly seen in other countries too.

## Notes

### 1 — abhijeet

普通、「ひとりＡだけでなくＢも」の形で用いられ、「ただＡだけでなくＢも」の意味。Ａ、Ｂには対象的、並立的あるいは類似の内容がくる。

### 2 — LR

<b>Ref <b><a href="http://jgram.org/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=hitori-dakedenaku,hitori-nominarazu#">#</a></b></b> Kanzen Master Level 1 - p45 - no.45

### 3 — LR

Formal, written

### 4 — LR

Not only a singular (problem) but a wider (problem)<br/>
Makes the problem seem more abstract? (It's not just a problem with Lee, this is a problem suffered by all exchange students)

### 5 — LR

ひとりdoesn't necesarily mean one person - it means a singular out of a whole (e.g. ひとり日本, ひとり上海 out of the rest of the world)

### 8 — samboki

A little off the grammar topic, delving into questions of vocab..... but while truancy and skipping are good suggestions, they don't quite cover it.  As the example suggests this is a 'special phenomenon' in Japan that is considered to be becoming a real social problem (aren't they special?).  It refers to a situation where a student refuses to attend school at all -  As in, they lock themselves in their room and never go out.  It is not a singular event, but an ongoing and open-ended one.  Dropping out/drop outs would probably be the most appropriate translation.

### 9 — mochabean

I've edited the translation and gone for 'non-attendance'. This term seems to be used a lot, at least here in the UK, when describing the problems of children who are 'serial truants' and essentially refusing to go at all, rather than simply skipping school for a day here and there (it's in the news a lot since it became law that the parents can be prosecuted and even sent to prison for failing to ensure their child attends school). 'Drop-out' to me suggests someone who is no longer enroled at the school as a result of non-attendance, which is perhaps a little different (?)<br/>
If anyone disagrees, feel free to edit!
