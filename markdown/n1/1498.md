# は言うまでもなく

Source: [Takoboto #1498](https://takoboto.jp/bunpo/1498/)  
Label: haiumademonaku  
JLPT: N1  
Credits: dc  
Retrieved: 2026-10-03T05:42:43+00:00

Other forms: はいうまでもなく

## Meaning

goes without saying/not to mention

he speaks vulcan, not to mention geek.

Meaning credits: dc

## Formation

- Noun + は言うまでもなく
