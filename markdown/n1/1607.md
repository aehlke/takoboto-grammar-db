# 不調法 or 無調法

Source: [Takoboto #1607](https://takoboto.jp/bunpo/1607/)  
Label: buchouhou  
JLPT: N1  
Credits: kadoka66  
Retrieved: 2026-10-03T05:45:44+00:00  
License: [CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/)

Other forms: ぶちょうほう

## Meaning

A rudeness or blunder, carelessness

(1)ainiku osake ha buchouhou deshite - I must apologize but I don't drink (2) karaoke wo utatte agerareru to ii n desuga, ainiku buchouhou na mono desu.　If I could sing karaoke to you I would but sorry unfortunately I can't.

Meaning credits: kadoka66

## Examples

### Example 8646

「一杯如何？」「ごめんね。まったく不調法なの」

「いちはいいか？」「ごめんね。まったくふちょうほうなの」

en: How about another ? sorry I cant cope with alcohol at all

### Example 8647

口は不調法だが、腕は確かだ

くちはぶちょうほうだが、うではたしかだ

en: I am not well spoken but can do a great job
