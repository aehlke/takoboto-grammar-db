## Meaning

besides doing a main thing, one is also involved in another activities.

the king sat beside the queen

ja: ~という本業をしながら、別の活動もしている

Meaning credits: dc

## Formation

- Verb dictionary form + かたわら
- Noun + の + かたわら

## Examples

### Example 3478

メアリーは読書をしており、１匹の猫が**かたわら**で眠っていた。

メアリーはどくしょをしており、１ひきのねこがかたわらでねむっていた。

en: Mary was reading, with a cat sleeping beside her.

en: The umpire sits in a high chair at the side of the court.

en: As well as studying Spanish, I decided to study flamenco as a hobby.

en: In addition to learning how to knit, my mother also teaches it **.**

en: My little sister studies English at a trade school at night while working at a company.

### Example 4749

en: **Besides** working, I'm planning to teach the children french.
