## Meaning

has value; it is worth doing

It is worth reading that book

ja: 十分に〜に値する・どうにか〜することができる。

Meaning credits: dc

## Discussion

### Comment 1 — dc

耐える = to put up with, be able bear.

### Comment 2 — LR

<b>Ref #</b> Kanzen Master Level 1 - p67 - no.64

### Comment 3 — LR

<b>注意</b><br/>While the verb 耐える as a piece of vocabulary means being able to withstand / endure, the grammar phrase にたえる means to deserve, be worthy of, to have value in... <b><strong>にたえる is completely different from 耐える</strong></b>!

### Comment 4 — LR

Customarily used with a set number of words such as 市販、鑑賞、読む、見る、聞く、批判、評価 (worthy of being sold, admired, read, seen, heard, criticism/comment, evaluation)
