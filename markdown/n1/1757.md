## Meaning

By means of, via, through - method by which you do / achieve something

Inform by letter

ja: 道具や手段を表す。〜で。

Meaning credits: LR

## Formation

- Noun + をもって

## Examples

### Example 8029

彼は人一倍の努力**をもって**優勝カップを手にすることができた

かれはひといちばいのどりょくをもってゆうしょうカップをてにすることができた

en: with his amazing efforts, we were able to get the champion's cup

Credits: LR

### Example 8030

こんな難問は、優秀な彼の頭脳**をもって**しても解決できないだろう。

こんななんもんは、ゆうしゅうなかれのずのうをもってしてもかいけつできないだろう。

en: Even with his brilliant mind, such a difficult problem cannot be resolved.

Credits: LR

### Example 8031

身**をもって**その仕事の大変さを知った

みをもってそのしごとのたいへんさをしった

en: I learned \[from experience / firsthand\] how hard the work was.

Credits: LR

### Example 8032

彼の迫ﾍ**をもって**すれば，この問題の解決は容易であろう

かれのさこをもってすれば，このもんだいのかいけつはよういであろう

en: With his ability, he should be able to solve the problem easily.

Credits: LR

### Comment 2 — LR

<b>On the difference between [womotte1] and [womotte2]</b><br/>[womotte1] is a Time phrase<br/>meaning "at" - marks beginnings, ends, or borders between times.<br/>It is often used as a greeting to お客 with the extended form をもちまして.<br/>[womotte2] is the method by which you do something - victory achieved via effort, announcement made via a blackboard.<br/><br/>Both are formal, both are NOUN + をもって<br/>be careful.
