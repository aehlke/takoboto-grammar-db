# と言わんばかりに

Source: [Takoboto #1314](https://takoboto.jp/bunpo/1314/)  
Label: toiwanbakarini  
JLPT: N1  
Credits: Amatuka  
Retrieved: 2026-10-03T05:43:42+00:00  
License: [CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/)

Other forms: といわんばかりに

## Meaning

as if to say

She winked at me, as if to say , I love you

Meaning credits: Amatuka

## See also

- [ばかりに](../n1/835.md)

## Examples

### Example 199

私がうそをついた**と言わんばかりに**ののしった

わたしがうそをついたといわんばかりにののしった

en: He spoke ill of me **as if to say** I had lied about it.

Credits: Amatuka

### Example 238

「あなたを愛しているのよ」と**言わんばかりに**彼女は僕にウィンクした。

「あなたをあいしているのよ」といわんばかりにかのじょはぼくにした。

en: She winked at me, **as much as to say** , I love you.

Credits: Amatuka

### Example 652

これ以上の議論は無駄だ**と言わんばかりに**、A国の代表は席を立った。

これいじょうのぎろんはむだだといわんばかりに、こくのだいひょうはせきをたった。

en: **As if to say** that further discussion is pointless, the representative from country A got up from his seat.

Credits: Amatuka

### Example 3740

彼は同意する**と言わんばかりに**うなずいた。

かれはどういするといわんばかりにうなずいた。

en: He nodded **as much as to say** , I agree.

Credits: dbx

### Example 3741

彼らは「かわいそうな奴」**といわんばかりに**私達をじっと見た。

かれらは「かわいそうなやっこ」といわんばかりにわたしたちをじっとみた。

en: They looked at us, **as much as to say,** "Poor creature."

Credits: dbx

### Example 3742

彼女は万事承知**と言わんばかりに**私に目くばせした。

かのじょはばんじしょうちといわんばかりにわたしにめくばせした。

en: She winked at me **as much as to say** she knew everything.

Credits: dbx

### Example 3743

友人は「不可能」**と言わんばかりに**首を横に振った。

ゆうじんは「ふかのう」といわんばかりにくびをよこにふった。

en: My friend shook his head **as much as to say** "impossible".

Credits: dbx, Miki

### Comment 2 — LR

<b>On the difference between <strong>とばかりに</strong> and <strong>と言わんばかりに</strong></b><br/><br/>と言わんばかりに"as if to say", and というように"in such a way that, as if to say" are synonymous with とばかりに and can be used in place of とばかりに in some cases.<br/><br/>However, you can't say と言わんばかりabout yourself: "The fog finally cleared so I ran outside as if to say this is my only chance" would be weird in Japanese (surely you don't need to guess your own thinking), but in this sentence you could use とばかりに and it would be more like "The fog finally cleared so I ran outside as if it was my only chance"<br/>see sentences at [tobakarini]
