## Meaning

For the purpose of; To \_\_\_\_, In order to

I studied hard in order to become an engineer.

Meaning credits: dc

## Examples

### Example 3310

エンジニアになる**べく**一生懸命勉強した

エンジニアになるべくいっしょうけんめいべんきょうした

en: I studied hard **in order to** become an engineer.

Credits: dc

### Comment 9 — bamboo4

べく is 連用形 of べし. It could oerate as an imperative or as firm presumption, indication of probability or expression of a plan.<br/>Imperative = 全員参加すべく<br/>Presumption = 後日登城の御裁可あるべく候(archaic)<br/>Indication = 期待にこたえるべく努力する<br/>Plan = 犯人を逮捕すべく<br/><br/>This expression is basically archaic, but it is still used in conversation in such set phrases as 成るべくして成った or 勝つべくして勝った.
