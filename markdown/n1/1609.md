## Meaning

no other way to do something　しかたがない

この大雪では、試合を中止にするのもやむをえない There's no other way but to cancel the game because of the snowfall

ja: そうするより方法がない

Meaning credits: Amanda

## Examples

### Example 6310

**やむを得ず**雨天で試合が中止になる場合はある。

やむをえずうてんでしあいがちゅうしになるばあいはある。

en: regretfully there may be situations where the game **has to** be cancelled due to bad weather.

Credits: kadoka66

### Example 6311

**やむを得ず**雨天で試合が中止になる場合はある。

### Comment 1 — kadoka66

The meaning I think is much stronger than しかったが無い. It expresses the fact that in certain special situations an action or event which is forthcoming is unavoidable. On the other hand although the former is used in similar situations it expresses more the idea that something always pans out in a certain way. <br/><br/>Coming I expect from the grammar construct 〜ざるをえない<br/><br/>You can also say やむにやまれず to mean the same thing. <br/><br/>I added my own example to show an alternative usage.

### Comment 2 — tmkain

if anyone reads the above comment, be careful. しかったが無い is incorrect; the correct phrase is 仕方が無い（しかたがない）, without the small "tsu."
