# 故に, 故の

Source: [Takoboto #1743](https://takoboto.jp/bunpo/1743/)  
Label: yueni  
JLPT: N1  
Credits: LR  
Retrieved: 2026-10-03T05:45:29+00:00  
License: [CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/)

Other forms: ゆえに, ゆえの

## Meaning

Due to, therefore (formal, written)

She also has many worries due to her good looks.

Meaning credits: LR

## Formation

- Phrase + が + ゆえに
- Noun + (の) + ゆえに/ゆえの

## Examples

### Example 7888

動かぬ証拠がある**ゆえに**、有罪が確定した

うごかぬしょうこがあるゆえに、ゆうざいがかくていした

en: **because of** the rock-solid evidence, his guilt was proven

Credits: LR

### Example 7889

彼女はその美貌の**故に**、悩みも多い。

かのじょはそのびぼうのゆえに、なやみもおおい。

en: **Because of** her good looks, she also has many things to worry about.

Credits: LR

### Example 7890

結果にこだわりすぎる**がゆえに**、小さなことに心を奪われることになる。

けっかにこだわりすぎるがゆえに、ちいさなことにこころをうばわれることになる。

en: **Because** you want the end result to be perfect, you end up being distracted by the little things,

Credits: LR

### Comment 4 — american57

The second clause cannot usually be in the past tense, unless the result (2d clause) is ongoing. So, になった is OK for the predicate because it tells of the circumstances today.

### Comment 5 — american57

I have my doubts about example 7888 because it is past tense and the proving has finished. So, I think "yue ni" cannot be used here. However, if the predicate were "yuuzai hanketsu ni natta" or "yuuzai de aru" ("is a guilty verdict" or "is guilty"), it may be grammatical.<br/><br/>The second clause cannot be a completed action, per Dictionary of Advanced Japanese Grammar p.747.
