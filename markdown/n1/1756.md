Other forms: 〜にたえない

## Meaning

Emphasizing a strong emotional reaction either good or bad

I'm absolutely furious about the way he treats me.

ja: ~をとても強く感じる・話してがそのことに対して強い感情を持っているときに用いる。

Meaning credits: LR

## Examples

### Example 8021

色々お世話になりまして、感謝**にたえません**

いろいろおせわになりまして、かんしゃにたえません

en: You've been so good to me, I **really**  want to thank you

Credits: LR, Pomu

### Example 8022

氏の当会へのご援助はまことに感謝**にたえません**

しのとうかいへのごえんじょはまことにかんしゃにたえません

en: We **cannot be too** grateful to him for the help he has given our society.

Credits: LR

### Example 8023

彼の私に対する態度は憤慨に**堪えない**

かれのわたしにたいするたいどはふんがいにこたえない

en: I'm absolutely furious about the way he treats me.

Credits: LR

### Comment 1 — LR

<b>On the difference between [nitaienai1] and [nitaienai2]</b><br/>There are two たえないs of note in the Japanese language... hurray.　How to distinguish them?<br/>[nitaienai1] is for saying you just can't stand something and should be constructed from VERB辞書形 + にたえない<br/>[nitaienai2] is for saying that you have a strong emotional reaction to something - "氏の当会へのご援助はまことに感謝に堪えません｜We cannot be too grateful to him for the help he has given our society." and should be constructed NOUN + たえない.<br/><br/>Both are on the old JLPT 1 syllabus, if anyone knows what's on the new N1, please advise
