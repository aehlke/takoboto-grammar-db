## Meaning

my utmost, at most

try my utmost

ja: できるだけ。精一杯。最大限。

Meaning credits: dc

## Examples

### Example 1111

この仕事だったら**せいぜい**三日もあればできる。

このしごとだったらせいぜいさんにちもあればできる。

en: If it's this type of work, it can be done in about three days **at the outside.**

Credits: bamboo4

### Example 3064

この仕事の完成に**せいぜい**努力します。

このしごとのかんせいにせいぜいどりょくします。

en: I will exert **my best** in finishing this work.

Credits: bamboo4

### Comment 3 — bamboo4

You guys must mean 最悪の場合. You would not say さいていの場合、三日掛かる but could say さいてい三日掛かる, which would mean "takes the minimum of three days."The former says that it could be done in less than three days, but the latter would say it takes three days or more.

### Comment 4 — bamboo4

Note that せいぜい also has the meaning of "at th emost." For example, せいぜい３日もあればできる would mean "It can be done in about three days at the very outside," In this sense it has the meaning similar to たかだか.

While I can't comment on any of the Japanese - "at the outside" is very informal in English. It refers to the expression "an outside chance" (a term from horseracing).
