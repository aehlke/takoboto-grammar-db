## Meaning

Used to express passive and active actions happening at once.

push and be pushed. go and come back, chase and be chased

Meaning credits: LR

## Examples

ラシューアワーの車内で乗客は押し**つ**押され**つ**している

en: in a rush-hour train, the passengers **push and are pushed**
