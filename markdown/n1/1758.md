## Meaning

Ever Since s.t. changed

Ever since the daughter returned, her aging father has really perked up

ja: てから後はずっと

Meaning credits: LR

## Formation

- Verb て-form + からというもの

## Examples

### Example 8033

娘が帰って来**てからというもの**、年老いた父親を見違えるほど元気になった

むすめがかえってきてからというもの、としおいたちちおやをみちがえるほどげんきになった

en: Ever since the daughter returned, her aging father has really perked up

Credits: LR

### Example 8034

水泳を習い始め**てからというもの**、冬でも風邪を引かなかった。

すいえいをならいはじめてからというもの、ふゆでもかぜをひかなかった。

en: Ever since I started learning how to swim, even in winter, I don't catch a cold.

Credits: LR

### Comment 2 — LR

This expression means "ever since ~", and is appended to the V て form to indicate that a behavior, perception, or situation has undergone a major change as the result of a certain occurence. It conveys surprise, admiration, or similar feelings regarding the change.
