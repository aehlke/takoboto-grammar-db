# や否や

Source: [Takoboto #1303](https://takoboto.jp/bunpo/1303/)  
Label: yainaya  
JLPT: N1  
Credits: Amatuka  
Retrieved: 2026-10-03T05:42:48+00:00  
License: [CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/)

Other forms: やいなや

## Meaning

just as soon as

the baby cried as soon as it saw me

ja: 動詞の終止形に付いて、同時にまたは引き続いてすぐに物事が行われる意を表す。．．．するとすぐに。．．．するが早いか。

Meaning credits: Amatuka

## Formation

- Verb (dictionary form) + やいなや
