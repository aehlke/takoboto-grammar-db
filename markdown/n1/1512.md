# 始末だ

Source: [Takoboto #1512](https://takoboto.jp/bunpo/1512/)

JLPT: N1  
Credits: blabby

Other forms: しまつだ

## Meaning

Unfortunately, (it has become ~)

Unfortunately, it came to the point where he had to borrow money.

Meaning credits: blabby

## Formation

- Verb dictionary form + 始末だ

## Examples

### Example 5435

あの二人は犬猿の仲で、ちょっとしたことでも、すぐ口論になる**始末だ**。

あのににんはけんえんのなかで、ちょっとしたことでも、すぐこうろんになるしまつだ。

en: Those two don't get along very well. **Unfortunately** , at the drop of a hat, they start arguing.

Credits: blabby

### Example 5436

友達に借金までする**しまつだ**。

ともだちにしゃっきんまでするしまつだ。

en: Unfortunately, it has got to the point where he has to borrow money from friends.

Credits: blabby

### Example 5437

この**始末**は、今まで述べたとおりです。

このしまつは、いままでのべたとおりです。

en: Just as I said, it turned out like this\!

Credits: blabby

### Example 5984

離婚したうえに、首にまでなる**しまつだ**。

りこんしたうえに、くびにまでなるしまつだ。

en: On top of getting divorced, I got fired from my job

Credits: khongbietboi

### Comment 2 — khongbietboi

Meaning: Finally, on top of that. Used to describe when something bad becomes worse.

In #5437,始末 means "from the beginning to the end" whereas in all other examples, 始末 is used to mean "situation" or "circumstances."
