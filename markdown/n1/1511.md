## Meaning

Like, As if; resemblance, analogy, similarity

I want to fly like a bird.

ja: 実際はそうではないが、まるで〜のような／に (文語体)

Meaning credits: blabby

### Comment 1 — blabby

Noun + (の) + ごとき + Noun<br/>Noun + ごとく + Adjective/Verb

### Comment 2 — mochabean

Not to be confused with &lt;b&gt;ことごとく&lt;/b&gt;, which is an unrelated word meaning 'entirely':<br/><br/>　ことごとく信じる。<br/> Believe something totally.

Written, Formal

### Comment 6 — LR

ごとし indicates similarity. It changes form depending on where it is in a sentence, it's nuance/grammatical function, and whether it is attached to a verb or a noun.

### Comment 7 — LR

One of the functions of ごとし / ごとき / ごとく is to mark a simile.

### Comment 8 — LR

When か is inserted, it adds the nuance of doubt on the part of the speaker.

### Comment 9 — LR

Used almost exclusively in written language.

### Comment 10 — LR

<b>Set Phrases</b><br/><br/><strong>上述のごとく</strong><br/>As stated above<br/><br/><strong>当然のごとく</strong><br/>As if it were a matter of course

### Comment 17 — 誠

「ごとく、ごとき」は「ように、ような」の古い言葉で、名詞の前に付かれて、改まった場所での会話や、硬い文章に使われます。

### Comment 18 — bamboo4

ごとく or 如く is literal diction and not normally used (sounds pedantic) in conversational speech. ような or ように can usually replace ごとく. <br/>As between 生けるがごとく and 生けるごとく, the former is the conventional usage, but the latter is more or less acceptable (with emphasis on "less").
