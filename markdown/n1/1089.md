# 今さら〜たところで

Source: [Takoboto #1089](https://takoboto.jp/bunpo/1089/)

Other forms: いまさら〜たところで

## Meaning

it's already too late, It's no use doing s.t. now

It's too late to explain why.

Meaning credits: dc

## Examples

### Example 3007

**今さら**説明し**たところで**先生は許してくれないと思う。

いまさらせつめいしたところでせんせいはゆるしてくれないとおもう。

en: **It's too late** to explain, I don't think the teacher will forgive you now.

Credits: dc

### Example 3980

**今さら**騒い**だところで、**どうにもならないよ。後の祭りだよ。

いまさらさわいだところで、どうにもならないよ。あとのまつりだよ。

en: Yelling about it **won't help now** . You should have done that when it counted.

Credits: Miki

### Example 6961

**今さら、**戻っ**たところで、**彼はもうおまえを愛しないようになった。

いまさら、もどったところで、かれはもうおまえをあいしないようになった。

en: **It’s too late to** come back, he no longer loves you.

Credits: amenya

### Example 7883

この問題を**いまさら**議論**したところで**無意味でしょう

このもんだいをいまさらぎろんしたところでむいみでしょう

en: To discuss the issue **at this late stage** would be meaningless.

Credits: LR

### Example 7884

子供時代に過ごした村を訪ねたいと思っていますが、**いまさら**行っ**たところで、**古いものは何も残していないでしょうね。

こどもじだいにすごしたむらをたずねたいとおもっていますが、いまさらおこなったところで、ふるいものはなにものこしていないでしょうね。

en: I'd like to visit the village where I spent my childhood, but **even if I went there now after so long,** I guess nothing of the old times would still be there.

Credits: LR

### Comment 4 — LR

Kanzen Master has this listed under <strong>~たところで</strong>, which apparently on it's own can also express that the first part of the sentence would be a wasted effort, e.g.<br/>約束の時間にこんなに遅れては、行ってみ<strong>たところで</strong>、だれもいないだろう<br/>(what with it being so late after the agreed time, even if I went now, surely there'd be no one there.)<br/><br/><strong>さらに</strong> really emphasizes how late in the day such an ineffective action would be. Nevertheless, keep in the back of your mind that <strong>たところで</strong> can exist on it's own with a similar meaning.<br/>On past test papers I've had questions that lead with <strong>さらに</strong> and the correct answer they wanted for the blank that came after was <strong>たところで</strong> so maybe it's worth learning these as a pair, this will also help you distinguish it from [toshitatokorode toshitatte nishitatokorode] etc. which has a slightly different nuance meaning even though the first part of the sentence is NOT a wasted effort, the final result doesn't satisfy.
