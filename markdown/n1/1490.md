## Meaning

like / ish

his sarcastic-ish way of speaking

ja: ish

Meaning credits: dc

## Formation

- Noun + めく

## Examples

### Example 5118

社長の皮肉**めいた**言い方を、社員は不快に感じている

しゃちょうのひにくめいたいいかたを、しゃいんはふかいにかんじている

en: because of the President's sarcastic **like** way of speech, the employees felt uncomfortable.

Credits: dc

### Example 5321

きら**めく**太陽に青い海、考えだけでも胸がとき**めく**わ。

きらめくたいようにあおいうみ、かんがえだけでもむねがときめくわ。

en: The glisten **ing** sun on the blue sea...Ahhh, just thinking about it makes my heart beat fast.

Credits: blabby

### Example 5322

俺たちは十年来の友人じゃないか、そんな他人**めいた**言い方をするなよ。

おれたちはじゅうねんらいのゆうじんじゃないか、そんなたにんめいたいいかたをするなよ。

en: Hey, we've been friends for many years right? Don't use such a strangerish/stranger- **like** style of speaking with me\!

Credits: blabby

This grammar is quite difficult because it is hard to translate into English. The best translation I can come up with is<br/><br/>* to become ~like (山は春めく - "The mountain became spring-like")<br/>* to show signs of (show signs of spring)<br/>* ~ish (他人めく - "strangerish")？<br/>* ~ly (冗談めく - Jokingly/皮肉めく - sarcastically)<br/>* ~ing (謎めく - Puzzling)
