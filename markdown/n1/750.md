## Meaning

Vn, As well not to ~

as well not to buy them

Meaning credits: Amatuka

## Examples

### Example 512

サッカーで日本が勝って、韓国人の金さんがどんな気持ちかなんて、そんなこと、言わ**ずもがな**のことだよ。

サッカーでにほんがかって、かんこくじんのきむさんがどんなきもちかなんて、そんなこと、いわずもがなのことだよ。

en: When Japan wins at football (soccer), **you don't even need to** ask to know how the Korean Mr. Kim feels.

Credits: Amatuka, kimchi314

### Example 537

新しい電気製品なんて、買わ**ずもがな**で、買うとろくなものがない。

あたらしいでんきせいひんなんて、かわずもがなで、かうとろくなものがない。

en: You **might as well not** buy those new electronic gadgets. If you do then nothing good will come of it.

Credits: Amatuka

### Example 3254

あの人の書く文章には**無くもがな**といえる言葉が多い。

あのひとのかくぶんしょうにはむくもがなといえることばがおおい。

en: In his writings, there are a number of words which might **better have been left out.**

Credits: bamboo4

### Comment 1 — blutorange

### Comment 2 — Amatuka

Two general uses, 1. Something doesn't need doing / saying.

### Comment 3 — Amatuka

2. Something should not be done / said [although may be anyway to ones regret]

Formed by V-nai base + ずもがな<br/>e.g. 買う→買わない→買わずもがな<br/><br/>Vn = V-nai base

### Comment 7 — bamboo4

もがな dates bqck to the Heian era, and it means that what precedes もがな would be the desire of the speaker. It is an archaic Japanese which only finds its use in some set expressions, such as 言わずもがな,which literally would mean "I desire that I do not say that" that would include the case of my regret for having said what I said.<br/>My 広辞苑 shows the following:<br/>あらずもがな、言わずもがな、無くもがな、やらずもがな and 由もがな.<br/>While 買わずもがな is grammatically correct, I am not sure if it is actually used.<br/>In any event, except perhaps 言わずもがな, all other instances of the use of もがな are pretty archaic.
