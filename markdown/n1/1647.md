## Meaning

because/since - explains the cause or gives a reason for

Since it was early morning, there wasn't even a dog around.

ja: 他でもない〜の(ひと・こと・もの）だから

Meaning credits: crlane

## Formation

- Verb -ない stem + ぬ + こととて
- Noun + の + こととて

## Discussion

### Comment 1 — crlane

Not normally used in speech. A more formal, written word for letters and whatnot. Explains the reason for something with the sense that it is precisely because of (person,thing) that( ). <br/>昼間はにぎやかなこの道も、早朝のこととてあたりに犬一匹いなかった。<br/>Because it was early in the morning, there wasn't even a dog around this street that is so lively during the day.<br/>〜のことだから、〜のことなので [kotodakara]
