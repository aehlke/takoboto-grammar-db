## Meaning

How the hell is it possible, that ...?

How the hell could someone like him, who never studied during school, become a teacher?

Meaning credits: yousei42

## Examples

### Example 8679

**こんな大きなケーキをよくもひとりで食べられるものだ**

こんなおおきなケーキをよくもひとりでたべられるものだ

en: **How the hell could he eat a cake that big all by himself?**
