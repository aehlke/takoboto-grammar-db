## Meaning

Don't have to 〜　(and are happy because of it)

Above anything, employees are happy for not having to ride on overcrowded trains.

ja: なくてもいい

Meaning credits: TMac

## Examples

### Example 7046

何よりも、満員電車に乗ら**なくてもすむ**のはうれしい。

なによりも、まんいんでんしゃにのらなくてもすむのはうれしい。

en: Above anything, employees are happy for not having to ride on overcrowded trains.

Credits: TMac

### Example 7047

今度の試験は易しいから、あまり勉強し**なくてもすむ**。

こんどのしけんはやさしいから、あまりべんきょうしなくてもすむ。

en: The upcoming test should be easy, so I think I can get by without studying much.

Credits: TMac

### Comment 1 — TMac

Also can appear as なくてすむ。<br/><br/>Has the nuance that the thing you don't have to do is something you don't want to do in the first place, and by being able to avoid it, you're happy because of it.<br/><br/>Conversational grammar

### Comment 3 — Radish

Remember that this can also be translated as "...and it's good that..."<br/><br/>See Unicom N2 Dokkai, pg 20. The reason "sumu" works this way is because the Chinese character's (original) meaning is "to relieve.".
