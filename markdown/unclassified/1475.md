## Meaning

Vaguely, somehow

Vaguely; somehow

ja: どことなくに通じる

Meaning credits: bamboo4

## Examples

### Example 4920

あの人は**なんとなく**母を想いださせる。

あのひとはなんとなくははをおもいださせる。

en: She **vaguely** resembles my mother.

Credits: bamboo4

### Example 4921

**なんとなく**京都に行きたくなった。

なんとなくきょうとにいきたくなった。

en: **Somehow** I wanted to go to Kyoto.

Credits: bamboo4

## Discussion

### Comment 1 — bamboo4

なんとなく and どことなく can be used interchangely in many cases. However, なんとなく refers to something that is vague and not capable of pinpointing when it deals with something more or less abstract, whereas どことなく refers to vagueness that deals more or less with physical subject matter.

### Comment 2 — yookoso

A Japanese friend once told me that you could use なんとなく by itself as a reply to a question you don't feel like answering or don't know how to answer specifically. Any native speakers care to agree/disagree with this?

### Comment 3 — kateinnakajima

one of my students used this as a reply in just such a context - when they didn't want to commit to any answer in particular and so I think this usage is generally accepted
