## Meaning

yet / as yet / so far/ not yet

you are STILL very young

ja: あなたは未だとても若い。

Meaning credits: marina.levanto

## Examples

### Example 6487

あなたは未だとても若い。**未だ**

あなたはまだとてもわかい。まだ

en: you are still very young **still**

Credits: marina.levanto
