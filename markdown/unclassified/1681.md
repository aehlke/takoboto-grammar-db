## Meaning

which in turn

This is good for \[subject\], which in turn is good for \[subject\]

Meaning credits: ZekkDistor

## Examples

### Example 7077

それは母体に良く、**ひいては**お腹の赤ちゃんにも良い。

それはぼたいによく、ひいてはおなかのあかちゃんにもよい。

en: It is good for the mother's body, which in turn is good for her coming baby.

Credits: ZekkDistor

### Example 8832

ひいては国際化に繋がるのだ ろうか?

ひいてはこくさいかにつながるのだろうか

en: ENG Will this in turn lead to internationalization?
