# 時間がたつ

Source: [Takoboto #1719](https://takoboto.jp/bunpo/1719/)  
Label: jikangatatsu  
JLPT: unclassified  
Credits: Dom  
Retrieved: 2026-10-03T05:46:38+00:00

Other forms: じかんがたつ

## Meaning

passing of time;

time passes fast

Meaning credits: Dom

## Examples

### Example 7506

**時間がたつ**のも忘れちゃったよ

じかんがたつのもわすれちゃったよ

en: I lost track of time.
