## Meaning

when it comes to~ (neg. sense)

Speaking of my son, he really isn't very good at sports

Meaning credits: jmadsen

## See also

- [といったら](../n2/1610.md)

## Examples

### Example 7063

うちの息子**ときたら**、全然勉強しないで、ゲームばっかりしてるのよ。

うちのむすこときたら、ぜんぜんべんきょうしないで、ゲームばっかりしてるのよ。

en: Speaking of my son, he is always playing games without studying.

Credits: jmadsen

### Example 7064

私**ときたら**、料理はまったくだめなのよ。

わたしときたら、りょうりはまったくだめなのよ。

en: I really can't cook at all.

Credits: jmadsen

## Discussion

### Comment 1 — jmadsen

This is like [toittara], but only used in a negative, often self-deprecating sense.

### Comment 2 — claytonian

Since this is always negative, one should only use it when talking their in-group members.
