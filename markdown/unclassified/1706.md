## Meaning

or else, otherwise

One should keep one's promise; otherwise no one will trust him or her

Meaning credits: Dom

## Examples

### Example 7281

謝りなさい。**さもないと**、怒りますよ。

あやまりなさい。さもないと、おこりますよ。

en: **Say you're sorry right now. Otherwise, I will be angry.**

Credits: 8241Murayama
