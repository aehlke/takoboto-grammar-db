## Meaning

throughout, through, during all

She cried all day.

Meaning credits: Amatuka

## Examples

### Example 883

彼女は一日**中**泣いてました。

かのじょはいちにちちゅうないてました。

en: She cried **all** day.

Credits: Amatuka

### Example 4996

その犬は一晩**中**ほえ続けた。

そのいぬはいちばんちゅうほえつづけた。

en: The dog kept barking **all night.**

Credits: Miki

### Example 4997

会議**中（ちゅう）**にお腹がグーグー鳴っていて困ったよ。

かいぎちゅう（ち）におなかがグーグーなっていてこまったよ。

en: My stomach started growling right there **in** the meeting. It was embarrassing.

Credits: Miki

### Example 4998

冬**中**暖房無しで過ごした。

ふゆちゅうだんぼうなしですごした。

en: I did without heating **all through** the winter.

Credits: Miki

### Example 4999

en: My wife often rang me up **during** her travel abroad.

what are the rules for it being juu rather than chuu?

### Comment 2 — sekaiget\_dominic

I thought the rule was as follows:<br/><br/>Chuu = indicates the continuation of an activity or a time period<br/>juu = indicates a space/time period without a break<br/><br/>So 営業中 (chuu)　indicates the activity of sales continuing. 春休み中 (chuu) indicates the period of time "Spring holiday"<br/><br/>夏休み中　(juu) indicates the entire summer holidays. 　<br/><br/>世界中　(juu) indicates the entire world<br/><br/>so to distinguish between the summer and spring examples above:<br/><br/>春休み中（ちゅう）は旅行に行くつもりです。<br/>夏休み中（じゅう）テレビゲームばかりしていた。<br/><br/>The spring example defines a period of time and allows us to mention activities that will occur in that time etc.<br/><br/>The summer example indicates that for the entire period of the summer holidays a single event occured. <br/><br/>What's confusing me is that I thought Miki's summer holidays example should be (juu), because of the zutto. perhaps if the emphasis was more on the fact that the entire holiday had been taken up by camping, we could have used juu?
