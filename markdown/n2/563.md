# 恐れがある

Source: [Takoboto #563](https://takoboto.jp/bunpo/563/)  
Label: osoregaaru  
JLPT: N2  
Credits: dc  
Retrieved: 2026-10-03T05:41:17+00:00  
License: [CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/)

Other forms: おそれがある

## Meaning

worry about, there is a possibility, there is fear that

i worry this illness might be contagious

Meaning credits: dc

## See also

- [きらいがある](../n1/1543.md)

## Examples

### Example 1209

戦争になる**おそれがある**

せんそうになるおそれがある

en: **There is the likelihood** of a war.

Credits: dc

en: This method of sale has **the likelihood of** violating the Antitrust Law.

Credits: dc

### Comment 2 — bamboo4

戦争に なる おそれがある literally means "There is the likelihood of a war breaking out" and does not mean "I worry there might be a war."<br/>I also added an example.

### Comment 3 — dc

### Comment 4 — dc

Is this always used for things that have a negative outcome?

### Comment 5 — bamboo4

Yes, おそれ is related to "fear" so that the expression generally relate to those that have a negative consequence.

### Comment 6 — bamboo4

この車は高いおそれがある is awkward and not normally used. <br/>〜高すぎるおそれがある is okay but still somewhat awkward. <br/>恐らくこの車は高すぎる may be acceptable..

### Comment 7 — MM

I ran these examples past a native speaker (a teacher of English). From what he said, if it refers to something physically happening (a war starting, a house collapsing, etc) then おそれがある is natural. If it refers to a situation (as in 3294) then だろう is natural sounding.

### Comment 8 — MM

I should add that he said that all the examples were gramatically acceptable - only that some didn't sound entirely natural.
