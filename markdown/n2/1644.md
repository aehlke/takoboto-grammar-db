# 一息

Source: [Takoboto #1644](https://takoboto.jp/bunpo/1644/)

JLPT: N2  
Credits: tigert

Other forms: ひといき

## Meaning

a short period of time

Don\`t rest now work is almost finished.

ja: 我慢することです

Meaning credits: tigert
