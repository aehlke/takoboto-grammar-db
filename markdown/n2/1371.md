# に依存

Source: [Takoboto #1371](https://takoboto.jp/bunpo/1371/)  
Label: niizon, niison  
JLPT: N2  
Credits: dc  
Retrieved: 2026-10-03T05:37:57+00:00  
License: [CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/)

Other forms: にいぞん, にいそん

## Meaning

depending on, relying on

depending on the maker

Meaning credits: dc

## Examples

### Example 4467

オーサリングツールとなると結局ツールメーカー**に依存**する

en: when it comes to authoring tools, it depends on the tool maker
