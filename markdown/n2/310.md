## Meaning

if .. is possible, then...

if i can go

Meaning credits: dc

## Discussion

### Comment 1 — 誠

動詞可能形　＋　〜ものなら：　〜ことが可能なら　（実現が難しいことについて言うことが多い）<br/> "If only I could/may...," <br/><br/>〜う／ようものなら： もし〜ようなことをしたら．．．（大変なことになる） Once if (one) happen to ... , then (bad consequence)."

### Comment 2 — halx

Used when one wishes to do something difficult.<br/>Usually used together with the potential form.

### Comment 3 — bamboo4

"if" does not quite get you there. For example, "If I come home late, he won't let me in" should perhaps be "Would I come home late, he won't let me in the house."<br/>ものなら is not iffy.

### Comment 4 — halx

ex #3724 has nothing to do with this grammar point.<br/>甘いもの (= sweets) just happens to be suffixed with なら (group 3)

### Comment 5 — halx

Shouldn't ex #3722 be better translated as: "Whatever you pick for me is fine"?

### Comment 6 — mk

-ようものなら is quite iffy. it denotes a hypothetical condition. [if i came home late, he wouldn't let me in]

### Comment 7 — 誠

ex #3719 is 〜もの(thing)　＋　ならば, and therefore does not belong to the usage of 〜ものなら .<br/>From ex#3719 to ex#3724, it doesn't give the meaning or correct usage of 〜ものなら, therefore treat it as 〜もの　＋　なら。 <br/>〜ものなら always follow the 可能形 potential form. and used to indicate something difficult to realize, or some ideal thinking of the writer,e.g. : "If only i was still a child, ...."

### Comment 8 — 誠

ex #3228 belongs to 〜う／ようものなら:<br/>My father is very strict. Once if I happen to come home late, my dad won't let me in.

### Comment 9 — 誠

〜ものなら： "If only I could/may.....", "If only it was possible to ....", "If only it was possible that..."
