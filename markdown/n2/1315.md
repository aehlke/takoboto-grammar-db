## Meaning

cannot help

I can't help feeling sad

Meaning credits: dc

## Formation

- Verb nai-form (without nai) + ずにはいられない

### Comment 1 — dc

I can't help doing ...<br/>I can't stop doing ...<br/><br/>these are both the same:<br/>verb+ ~zu ni ha irarenai<br/>verb+~nai de ha irarenai<br/><br/>(eg sezuni is a contraction of shinai)

### Comment 2 — dc

Not used for "feeling" something, where you should use [tenaranai]<br/><br/>-- doing<br/>I can't help shopping<br/>買い物せずにはいられない<br/>買い物しないではいられない<br/><br/>-- feeling<br/>I can't help feeling anxious<br/>NG: 私は不安しないではいられない<br/>OK: 私は不安でならない
