# 〜しかない

Source: [Takoboto #259](https://takoboto.jp/bunpo/259/)  
Label: shikanai  
JLPT: N2  
Credits: dc  
Retrieved: 2026-10-03T05:41:08+00:00

## Meaning

no alternative, no option but to...

nothing to do but try...

Meaning credits: dc

## Formation

- Verb (dictionary form) + しかない
- Noun + しかない

### Example 1218

かのじょとけっこんするしかなかった。

en: **I had no choice but** to marry her.

### Example 6133

### Example 6891

### Example 6892

息子は桜大学に進学することを希望している。しかし、今の息子の学力ではとても無理だから、別の大学を受験させる**しかない**。本人の希望とは違ってもしかたがないことだ。

むすこはさくらだいがくにしんがくすることをきぼうしている。しかし、いまのむすこのがくりょくではとてもむりだから、べつのだいがくをじゅけんさせるしかない。ほんにんのきぼうとはちがってもしかたがないことだ。

en: My daughter hopes to enter the Sakura University. However, her capacity is very poor, so **I have no choice but to compel her** take the exam of another university. Although it's not her hope, there is no way.

Credits: 赤毛

### Comment 1 — rubyhatchet

＊Be very careful as to when you replace one of these phrases with another.<br/><br/>Ｖ + しかない : used in a situation where one has no choice but to do something.<br/><br/>Ｖneg + ざるを得ない : used in a situation where one has no choice but to do something.<br/><br/>Ｖneg + なければならない : used in a situation where one has to do something because s/he has an obligation to do it OR where one has no choice but to do something.<br/><br/>Ｖneg + わけに(は)いかない : used in a situation where one has to do something because s/he has an obligation to do it OR where one has no choice but to do something. It also expresses the unwillingness of the speaker to do Ｖ.
