# に加えて

Source: [Takoboto #225](https://takoboto.jp/bunpo/225/)  
Label: nikuwaete  
JLPT: N2  
Credits: dc  
Retrieved: 2026-10-03T05:36:40+00:00  
License: [CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/)

Other forms: にくわえて

## Meaning

in addition to

Well, in addition to that, he also paid for the food.

Meaning credits: dc

## Formation

- Noun + に加えて

## Examples

### Example 438

サイズ的魅力**に加えて**画質的なノープロブレム感もある「ペンタックス Optio S」.

サイズてきみりょくにくわえてがしつてきなノープロブレムかんもある「」

en: The "Pentax Optio S" : **In addition to** the attraction from its size, the picture quality also has a 'no problemo' feeling.

Credits: Amatuka

en: **in addition to** not knowing Japanese, i also don't understand English grammar

en: **Along with** this increase, there has been a change in the world's economic organization.
