## Meaning

over; span(ning)

His interests span many fields.

ja: の間(の); に広がって; の間続いて;その範囲の全体にその状態が広がっている、続いていると言いたいときも使われる。

Meaning credits: dc

### Comment 19 — yuuhi2525

Shidareさん。<br/><br/>The reason why it is にわたる is because it defines what the noun is.<br/>2年にわたる新しい道路の建設工事がやっと終わった<br/>This can be separated like this:<br/>建設工事 = Construction work<br/>道路の建設工事 = Construction work of the highway<br/>新しい道路の建設工事 = Construction work of the new highway<br/>2年にわたる新しい道路の建設工事 = Construction work of the new highway that spanned 2 years<br/><br/>So the real noun here is 道路の建設工事, but 「2年にわたる新しい」defines some properties to the noun as well. This grammar is very common when you describe something about a noun. Other examples may be:<br/><br/>「これはネズミを食べる猫です」<br/>This is a cat that eats mice.<br/><br/>You need to have the verb in it's dictionary form to define something about the noun that comes after ^^
