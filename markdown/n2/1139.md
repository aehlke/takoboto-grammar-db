## Meaning

do (humble)

I will present this to you

ja: 致すと言う言葉はするの意味と一緒です

## Discussion

### Comment 1 — dc

itashimasu is "to do" spoken in a modest manner, or which the Japanese call 謙譲語 (kenjyougo or humble language).<br/><br/>私がいたします would be directed to your superior meaning that "I will do that." <br/>It has nothing to do with "giving" or "receiving" which would be involved in 貰う and あげる.

### Comment 2 — tigert

致します is the keigo form of suru. Although it can mean I will do, it can also take the past tense form and mean I have done. IE 致しました
