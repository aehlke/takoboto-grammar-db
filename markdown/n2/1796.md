## Meaning

When it comes to

When it comes to my mother's cooking, it doesn't lose to anyone.

Meaning credits: bgaskin

## Examples

### Example 8768

母は料理に**かけると**だれにも負けない自信がある

はははりょうりにかけるとだれにもまけないじしんがある

en: When it comes to my mother's cooking, I'm confident she doesn't lose to anyone.
