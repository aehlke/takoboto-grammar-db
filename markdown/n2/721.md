## Meaning

Vte, (te-form verb + contrastative ha wa)

Don't die\! (Lit: Your dying would be terrible)

Meaning credits: Amatuka

- [しまう (仕舞う・終う・了う)](../n3/718.md)

## Examples

### Example 855

死ん**じゃ**、ダメ！

しんじゃ、ダメ！

en: Don't die\! (Lit: your dying would be terrible)

Credits: Amatuka

### Example 856

落とし**ては**まずい。

おとしてはまずい。

en: Dropping (this) would be unwise.

Credits: Amatuka

### Example 8605

**じゃ**、また来週！

じゃ、またらいしゅう！

en: **Well (Let's stop here),** see you next week\!

Credits: DrJones
