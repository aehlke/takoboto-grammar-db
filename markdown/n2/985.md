## Meaning

No sooner than; just as, at that instant

just as the doors opened they rushed in

Meaning credits: dc

## Formation

- Verb (casual form) + か + Verb (ない-form) + うちに/かのうちに

## Examples

### Example 318

電車のドアが開く**か**開か**ないかのうちに**乗客が飛び出して来る

でんしゃのドアがひらくかひらかないかのうちにじょうきゃくがとびだしてくる

en: **just at the moment when** the train doors are opening, the passengers fly out.

Credits: dc

en: **Just as** the pistol sounded, they started

### Example 4603

そのいっこうがしゅっぱつするかしないかのうちに、あめがふりだした。

en: The party had hardly left when it began to rain.

en: No sooner had I entered the hall than the ceremony began.

en: I had hardly fallen asleep when the telephone rang.
