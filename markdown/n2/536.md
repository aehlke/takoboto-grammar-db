### Comment 4 — 新丸子

the way of using 見せる in N2 is not just "to show". That's the common 使役形 of using the verb 見る that you learn in N5/N4. The way it's meant to be used here is in the form A-て見せる form. There, it means "definitely" doing A<br/><br/>Like in:<br/>今度こそちゃんと勝って見せるよ！<br/>こんどこそかってみせるよ！<br/>Next time I'll definitely win. (literally, "Next time, I'll show (someone) that I'll win.")
