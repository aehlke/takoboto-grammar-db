Other forms: ともかく

## Meaning

anyhow; anyway; somehow or other; generally speaking; in any case

anyhow,I don't care about the result, ...

ja: 「それはともかく」、「ともかく無事でよかった」など、“

Meaning credits: sureshnihon

## Examples

### Example 7165

試合の結果は**ともかく,**最後までみんなよくがんばった。

しあいのけっかはともかくさいごまでみんなよくがんばった。

en: **No matter what** the result was, every one has done their best.

Credits: sureshnihon

en: Whether we're winning or not, at any rate I'm just happy to get in the game.
