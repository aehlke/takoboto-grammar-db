# 〜程

## Meaning

the more... the more... as along with, to the extent

the colder it is, the more delicious sake can be made

Meaning credits: dc

## Examples

### Example 263

寒ければ寒い（referring to 'weather'）冷たければ冷たい（referring to 'Sake')**ほど**お酒がおいしくなると言われている

さむければさむい（つめたければつめたい（ほどおさけがおいしくなるといわれている

en: the colder it is **the more**  delicious sake can become, it is said

Credits: dc, Kuval Anand

### Example 666
