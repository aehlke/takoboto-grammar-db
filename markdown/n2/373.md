## Meaning

due to

due to construction, the train is late.

Meaning credits: dc

## Formation

- Noun + につき

## See also

- [なので](../n4/1248.md)

## Examples

### Example 6409

この道は工事中**につき**、通行止めになっています。

このみちはこうじちゅうにつき、つうこうどめになっています。

en: **Due to** construction, this street is closed to traffic.

Credits: rubyhatchet

### Example 6410

en: **Due to** the air conditioning, please close the door.

Credits: rubyhatchet

### Example 6411
