## Meaning

like, as if (metaphor)

he looked as if he knew nothing about it

Meaning credits: dc

## Examples

### Example 3793

彼はまるで父親に会ったことがない**かのようだ**。

かれはまるでちちおやにあったことがないかのようだ。

en: **It seems like** he has never met his father before.

Credits: dc
