## Meaning

covered with; littered with; infested with,full of

This website is littered with mistakes.

Meaning credits: dc

## Formation

- Noun + だらけ

### Example 6963

en: It was raining since yesterday, and the sports ground was still wet. When the contest finished, the faces of the three soccer team players were **covered with** mud.

en: As I have friends here and there in the world, I receive the picture postcards they send to me. Once, I began to pin such postcards in a wall. Without even noticing it, they increased in number to a great extent. Now, the wall we are speaking about is definitely **covered with** postcards.

## Discussion

### Comment 1 — rubyhatchet

だらけ refers to bad things or objects, such as "mistakes," "garbage," etc. It is only used after nouns, but can be used in one of two ways.<br/><br/>N + だらけ<br/>1. 「Aは(が)Bだらけだ」:　部屋はゴミだらけだ。<br/>2. 「BだらけのA」: ゴミだらけの部屋<br/>*Note that the 2nd use is not a complete sentence.
