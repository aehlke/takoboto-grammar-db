## Meaning

Regardless of

Regardless of the cost - I must buy it.

Meaning credits: Amatuka

### Comment 2 — halx

also ~に(は)かかわりなく

### Comment 3 — dc

is the "mo" in this optional, or is there a logic to when it is/not used?<br/><br/>それに「も」かかわらず彼女は魅力的だ。<br/>She is charming for all that.

### Comment 4 — dc

the も would seem to be emphasis. <br/><br/>来るにもかかわらず<br/>"even if they come"<br/><br/>来るにかかわらず<br/>regardless of whether they come <br/>来るか来ないかにかかわらず<br/>whether they come or not

### Comment 5 — Miki

Mostly, に or にも + かかわらず I think the "mo" is optional.<br/>Exception I realized are<br/>それにもかかわらず<br/>-た／だ + にもかかわらず ex#4468, ex#4469, ex#4478
