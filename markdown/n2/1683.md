## Meaning

regarding, about, concerning

I don't know much concerning the laws.

Meaning credits: va\_tei

## Formation

- Noun + にかけては

## Examples

### Example 7087

卓球**にかけては**弟におよばない

たっきゅうにかけてはおとうとにおよばない

en: **Concerning** table tennis, my skill is not as good as my little brother's.

Credits: va\_tei

### Example 7088

法律**にかけては**私はよくわからない

ほうりつにかけてはわたしはよくわからない

en: I don't know much **about** laws.

Credits: va\_tei

### Example 7801

わが社の製品は知名度は低いものの、性狽ﾌ良さ**にかけては**世界の最高水準だ。

わがしゃのせいひんはちめいどはひくいものの、せいよさにかけてはせかいのさいこうすいじゅんだ。

en: Although our company's products may not get a lot of publicity, the quality of their performance meets the world's highest standards.

Credits: inakaluke

### Comment 3 — missshapoooo

About nuances between these 3 patterns. Firstly,に関して and について are absoulutely the same (=about, concerning) except for the former is used in formal speeches, reports, TV news, on paper etc. So when we speak, search, know...ABOUT smth we use them.<br/>Speaking about にかけては it is more like についていえば. So yes, these three patterns can have the same meaning in case when we speak about N. But also the nuanse in first to patterns and にかけては is that in case of the latter one, after pattern there goes some statement concerning N, but in case of the former ones these patterns are just a conjucture for direct object (N).
