## Meaning

It seems that; I heard that

It seems that he's really wealthy. I envy him.

Meaning credits: rubyhatchet

## Examples

### Example 6260

お嬢さんが結婚なさった**とか**。おめでとうございます。

おじょうさんがけっこんなさったとか。おめでとうございます。

en: I heard you got married. Congratulations.

Credits: rubyhatchet

### Example 6261

Ｇ銀行は経営が危なくなっている**とか**。預けた金は大丈夫だろうか。

じーぎんこうはけいえいがあぶなくなっているとか。あづけたきんはだいじょうぶだろうか。

en: It seems that the management at Bank G is in trouble. I wonder if the money deposited there is safe.

Credits: rubyhatchet

### Example 6262

彼は大金持ちだ**とか**。うらやましいなあ。

かれはおおがねもちだとか。うらやましいなあ。

en: It seems that he's a wealthy man. I envy him.

Credits: rubyhatchet

### Example 7762

田中さんの犬がおじさんを噛んだ**とか**。

たなかさんのいぬがおじさんをかんだとか。

en: **I heard** Mr. Tanaka's dog bit an old man.

Credits: Boomeranger

## Discussion

### Comment 1 — rubyhatchet

＊Use this grammar for bits of news that you heard or read.<br/><br/>FORMATION:<br/>Ｖ(plain form) + とか<br/>いＡ + とか<br/>なＡ・Ｎ + とか

### Comment 2 — Mahou86

For the case of a noun with toka, you actually need だ、it`s shown in the 3rd example below<br/><br/>N＋だ〜とか<br/>金持ちだとか （I heard he was rich)

### Comment 3 — khyron

The usage given in the formation section for なＡ・Ｎ does not match that of the example of ex #6262, which suggests that instead of:<br/>なＡ・Ｎ + とか<br/>it should instead be<br/>なＡ・Ｎ + だ＋とか

### Comment 4 — khyron

I checked and confirmed with a native speaker that when using this grammar after なA・N that the formation is indeed なA・N＋だとか.<br/><br/>Additionally, this is found mainly in novels, whereas a conversational equivalent would be と聞いた・と聞きました.
