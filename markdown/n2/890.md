## Meaning

not only, but also. starting with.

not only french, but also german, spanish etc...

### Example 1055

歌手は最新のヒット曲**をはじめ**２５の新曲を歌った。

かしゅはさいしんのヒットきょくをはじめ２５のしんきょくをうたった。

en: The singer **started with** a recent hit, and then sang 25 other new songs.

## Discussion

### Comment 1 — dc

&lt;b&gt;を始め&lt;/b&gt; is used to preface a list of items, and also to emphasize the first one.<br/><br/>A をはじめ、B, C ...<br/><br/>English equivalents might be &lt;b&gt;not only, but also&lt;/b&gt; <br/>Not only A, but also B and C<br/>Especially A, but also B and C<br/><br/>similar to [karashite] which is mostly used for negative things.<br/><br/>かれの下品の言い方&lt;b&gt;からして&lt;/b&gt;、すごく嫌な人です<br/>I really dislike him, &lt;b&gt;especially&lt;/b&gt; because of his foul mouth.

### Comment 7 — bamboo4

を始め does not necessairly mean "to start with" but the means of enumeration. 彼は英語を始め、フランス語とロシア語にも堪能だ only serves as the means of enumeration. "He is conversant not only in English but also in French and Russian."
