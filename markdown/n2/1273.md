### Comment 10 — Kaens

べき seemingly implies somewhat more of 'should'ness, while 〜ことだ is literally like 'this is about 〜(gerund)', and really is the emphasis on something being required. In a way, ことだ is even more imperative.

### Comment 11 — 誠

It is not necessarily a stern usage(e.g.scolding), it can be an advice or recommendation with the meaning "should". <br/>〜ことだ ： 〜ことを勧める 、〜ことが必要だ

### Comment 12 — 誠

For 〜ものだ, it is used when the thing is of common sense, and usually is used to advice or remind people of morality,principle of life,etc.<br/> <br/>〜ものだ： 〜が一般的な常識だ （人に対して注意する時によく使われる）
