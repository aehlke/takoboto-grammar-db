# に渡る, に渡って

Source: [Takoboto #1759](https://takoboto.jp/bunpo/1759/)  
Label: ni wataru, ni watatte  
JLPT: N2  
Credits: Bartek

Other forms: にわたる, にわたって

## Meaning

for, over, extending, stretching, ranging, covering

The Olympic games were held for sixteen days

Meaning credits: Bartek

## Formation

- Noun + に + わたって
- Noun + に + わたる + (Noun)

## Examples

### Example 8744

約40年**にわたる**冷戦がついに終わった。

やくねんにわたるれいせんがついにおわった。

en: The Cold War, **which lasted for about** 40 years, finally came to an end.

Credits: Lscotty94

### Example 8746

オリンピックが16日間**にわたって**開かれた.

オリンピックがにちかんにわたってひらかれた

en: The Olympic games were held **for** sixteen days.

Credits: Lscotty94

## Discussion

### Comment 1 — Lscotty94

(ni wataru) is a compound particle that indicates something takes (took place) over a particular span of time or physical space. It is often used in written form and in formal speech, such as the news.
