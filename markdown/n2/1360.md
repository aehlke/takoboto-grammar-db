## Meaning

as; in proportion to; with

As I ate, my headache improved.

ja: とともに; に伴って; に従って; と一緒に

Meaning credits: dc

### Comment 3 — rubyhatchet

＊につれ(て) is a phrase used to indicate that a change occurs in accordance with another simultaneous change. Because of this, the verbs in the につれ(て) clause and the main clause have to be verbs of change or of a process, such as 'become proficient' and 'increase.'<br/><br/>＊When と共に(とともに) means 'as,' it can be rephrased as につれて, に伴って(にともなって), or に従って(にしたがって).<br/>EXAMPLE: 景気の回復[と共に/につれて/に伴って/に従って]失業率が下がってきた。(As business recovers, the unemployment rate is coming down.)<br/><br/>＊The difference between Ｖ(inf・nonpast)にしたがって and Ｖ(inf・nonpast)につれて is that the former is more of a written style and the latter is more of a spoken style.<br/><br/>FORMATION:<br/>Ｖ(inf・nonpast) + につれて<br/>Ｎ + につれて
