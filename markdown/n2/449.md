## Meaning

to make.

(The teacher) makes the class interesting.

Meaning credits: Godzilla

## See also

- [にする](../n3/649.md)

## Examples

### Example 647

AutoCADを ３日でもの**にする**！！

を３にちでものにする！！

en: **Make** AutoCAD **yours** in three days\!\!

Credits: Amatuka

### Example 680

日本語を自家薬籠中のもの**にする**

にほんごをじかやくかごちゅうのものにする

en: **Make** Japanese the language you can handle with complete dexterity.

Credits: bamboo4

### Example 447
