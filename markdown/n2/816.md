## Meaning

just at the time when

just as the doors opened

Meaning credits: d9

## Examples

### Example 1179

電車のドアが開いた**かどうかのうちに**

でんしゃのドアがひらいたかどうかのうちに

en: **Before you know** if the train doors opened or not.

Credits: dc, bamboo4

### Example 5243

授業ベルが**鳴らすかどうか**教室から学生が出ました。

じゅぎょうベルがならすかどうかきょうしつからがくせいがでました。

en: **Just as the school bell rang,** the students dashed out of the classroom.

Credits: eastmael

## Discussion

### Comment 1 — dc

this is almost as if the user is not sure the new state has occurred yet.. did the doors open ("ka dou ka") or not yet..

### Comment 2 — bamboo4

Literally, it means "while it is not certain whether the door opened or not," so you cannot tell if the door opened or not. It depends upon the text that follows, but normally, it would mean that the door did open.

### Comment 3 — Miki

#5243 授業（終了の）ベルが鳴るかどうかのうちに　is better. <br/>I would say from the English...<br/>授業終了のベルが鳴るか鳴らないかのうちに、学生たちは教室から飛び出しました。 This does not mean students flew out, by the way.

### Comment 4 — sagittarius
