# てたまらない

Source: [Takoboto #832](https://takoboto.jp/bunpo/832/)  
Label: tetamaranai  
JLPT: N2  
Credits: dc  
Retrieved: 2026-10-03T05:41:02+00:00

## Meaning

be dying to do something, to feel X strongly, can't help it...

I am dying to go home

ja: 非常に

Meaning credits: dc

## See also

- [てならない](../n2/319.md)
