# 気味

Source: [Takoboto #670](https://takoboto.jp/bunpo/670/)  
Label: gimi  
JLPT: N2  
Credits: dc  
Retrieved: 2026-10-03T05:41:46+00:00

Other forms: ぎみ

## Meaning

Vm、N slight tendency toward, seems like, feels so..

He is on the heavy side.

Meaning credits: dc

## Formation

- Verb ます-stem + ぎみ
- Noun + ぎみ

### Comment 7 — bluntcrayon

The main difference between 「がち」and ぎみ is that 「がち」usually describes a general tendency in someone or something while 「ぎみ」describes a visible indication of a tendency. <br/>e.g. 1 田中は最近太りがちだ<br/>Tanaka has a general tendency to gain weigh but is not necessarily overweight at the moment of speech。Compare with;<br/>２）田中は最近太り気味だ<br/>Tanaka is showing signs of being overweight。<br/><br/>Also 「がち」Cannot express the idea of "a touch of" or " a little".<br/>(taken from the Intermediate grammar dictionary)
