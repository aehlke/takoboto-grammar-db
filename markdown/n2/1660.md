# 余り

Source: [Takoboto #1660](https://takoboto.jp/bunpo/1660/)  
Label: amari  
JLPT: N2  
Credits: 赤毛  
Retrieved: 2026-10-03T05:36:55+00:00

Other forms: あまり

## Meaning

not much, too much

あまり面白くない =\> not very interesting / あまり遅い =\> too late

Meaning credits: 赤毛

- [の余り](../n2/1369.md)
