# の余り

Source: [Takoboto #1369](https://takoboto.jp/bunpo/1369/)  
Label: noamari  
JLPT: N2  
Credits: dc  
Retrieved: 2026-10-03T05:36:36+00:00

Other forms: のあまり

## Meaning

because of too much

Meaning credits: dc

## Formation

- 名詞 + のあまり
- Noun + のあまり

- [余り](../n2/1660.md)
