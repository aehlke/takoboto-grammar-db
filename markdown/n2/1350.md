## Meaning

to do it in advance OR to leave it in the current state expecting a later change

I left the window open

Meaning credits: dc

## Formation

- Verb て-Form + おきます
- Verb て-Form + おく

## Examples

## Discussion

### Comment 1 — cup

"oku" and "-te oku" always has something expected in the future.<br/><br/>This is a AuxVerb and is used in by the patterm てーStem + おく<br/>1) To keep the action’s result.<br/>2) To continue the action.<br/>3) Admitting the situation is/will be a certain way and leaving it the way it is.<br/>4) To do something in advance, and expecting a certain result or happening.<br/>5) To doing something temporally to get by for the moment.

### Comment 3 — bamboo4

おく has the primary meaning of "leave it where it is at" or "leave it at its present status." The use of おく as indicating "in advance" is very limited such as 耳に入れておく. Most of the examples shown here are "leave it where it is at" type, and only 3303, 4327 and 4328 would qualify as statements in "in advance" category.For other examples, it is incorrect to label them as in "to do something in advance" category.
