## Meaning

used to

I used to play by the river a lot when I was a kid.

ja: 子供のとき、よく川で遊んだものだ。

Meaning credits: leslie

## Examples

### Example 5779

子供のとき、よく川で遊ん**だものだ。**

こどものとき、よくかわであそんだものだ。

en: I **used to** play by the river a lot when I was a kid.

Credits: leslie
