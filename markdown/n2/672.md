# かねない

## Meaning

Vm, capable of (a bad thing)

he is capable of anything

Meaning credits: dc

## Formation

- Verb ます stem + かねない

## See also

- [兼ねる](../n2/1266.md)

## Examples

### Example 886

en: He looks serious, but there’s a chance he might lie.
