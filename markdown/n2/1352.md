# に先立って; に先立つ

Source: [Takoboto #1352](https://takoboto.jp/bunpo/1352/)  
Label: nisakidatte; nisakidatsu  
JLPT: N2  
Credits: dc  
Retrieved: 2026-10-03T05:37:45+00:00  
License: [CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/)

Other forms: にさきだって; にさきだつ

## Meaning

before; preceeding; prior to

Preceeding the showing, the performer will come onto the stage and greet everyone.

ja: のまえに

Meaning credits: dc

## Examples

### Example 1065

フィルタリング導入**に先立って**、親子の信頼関係を損ねないために考慮すべきこと。

フィルタリングどうにゅうにさきだって、おやこのしんらいかんけいをそこねないためにこうりょすべきこと。

en: Things that should be considered **before** setting up \[Internet\] filtering in order not to damage the relationship of trust between parent and child.

Credits: Amatuka

### Example 1083

お金**に先立つ**のは家族だ。

おかねにさきだつのはかぞくだ。

en: family **comes before** money

en: **Preceeding** the general opening of the film, the leading actress's signing session was opened.

### Comment 1 — dc

this is quite formal, so "preceeding" might be the best translation. Otherwise 前に (maeni) is more casual.

### Comment 2 — dc

先立つ（さきだつ）is the dictionary form, as in one of the examples below.
