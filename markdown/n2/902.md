# どころではない、どころではなく

Source: [Takoboto #902](https://takoboto.jp/bunpo/902/)  
Label: dokorodehanai, dokorodehanaku  
JLPT: N2  
Credits: dc

## Meaning

not at all; far from; anything but; out of the question

Cold is not the word. It's FREEZING\!

Meaning credits: dc

## Discussion

### Comment 1 — dc

dokoro de ha nai / dokoro de ha naku<br/><br/>Dokoro dewa nai goes at the end of a sentence. Dokoro dewa naku goes in the middle.

### Comment 2 — rubyhatchet

＊Use this grammar to strongly deny something.<br/><br/>FORMATION:<br/>Ｖ(plain form) + どころではない<br/>いＡ + どころではない<br/>なＡ・Ｎ + どころではない<br/><br/>As for どころではなく, this grammar fits into the middle of a sentence, as in example #6271. どころではない is meant to end a sentence.
