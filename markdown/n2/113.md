## Meaning

despite; though; although; in spite of

despite the rain lots of people came

Meaning credits: dc

## Formation

- Verb plain form + にもかかわらず
- い-adjective + にもかかわらず
- な-adjective (without な) + (である) + にもかかわらず
- Noun + にもかかわらず
