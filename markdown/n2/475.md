## Meaning

Don't do ~

Don't let this get out.

Meaning credits: Amatuka

## Examples

### Example 494

問題が起これば、学校だけで何とかしよう、外にはもらす**まい**。

もんだいがおこれば、がっこうだけでなんとかしよう、そとにはもらすまい。

en: If some trouble crops up, sort it out somehow or other in school - **don't let it** leak outside.

Credits: Amatuka
