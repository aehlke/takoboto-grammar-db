## Meaning

Cannot help -ing; Unbearably; Irresistibly

I cannot help feeling anxious.

Meaning credits: dc

## Formation

- V（て形） + ならない
- な ADJ + で + ならない
- い ADJ + くて + ならない
- ＊Must omit い when using with い ADJ (Before くて）

### Comment 3 — rubyhatchet

＊This expression is meant to describe insurmountable psychological or physiological feelings.<br/>・If the preceding adjective does not describe psychological or physiological feelings, ならない cannot be used. Also, there are a couple of adjectives describing psychological or physiological feelings that cannot be used; they are merely exceptions. For example: 痛い、嫌い、好き.<br/>・When a verb is connected with ならない, it is usually an idiomatic phrase of psychological feelings, and NOT of physiological feeling. The following sentence would be UNGRAMMATICAL unless ならない is replaced with たまらない:<br/>朝飯を食べて来なかったので、腹が減ってならない。<br/>(I came here without eating my breakfast, so I am awfully hungry.)<br/>This is the correct sentence:<br/>朝飯を食べて来なかったので、腹が減ってたまらない。<br/><br/>FORMATION:<br/>Ｖ・いＡ(て forms) + ならない<br/>なＡ + で + ならない
