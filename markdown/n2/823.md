## Meaning

Can't avoid doing x

I can't not go \[always double-neg\] = I have to / I must go

ja: どうしても〜しなければならない

Meaning credits: dc

## Formation

- V（ない形） + ざるをえない
- ＊する→せざる

### Comment 1 — 誠

〜ざるを得ない: 〜したくないが．．．しなければならない

### Comment 2 — rubyhatchet

This is an auxiliiary indicating that there is no other choice but to do something. Possible translations include: cannot help 〜ing; cannot help but; have no choice but to have to.<br/><br/>Ｖneg + ざるを得ない<br/>Examples: 食べざるを得ない // 行かざるを得ない<br/>Exception: する -&gt; せざるを得ない

### Comment 3 — rubyhatchet

＊Be very careful as to when you replace one of these phrases with another.<br/><br/>Ｖneg + ざるを得ない : used in a situation where one has no choice but to do something.<br/><br/>Ｖ + しかない : used in a situation where one has no choice but to do something.<br/><br/>Ｖneg + なければならない : used in a situation where one has to do something because s/he has an obligation to do it OR where one has no choice but to do something.<br/><br/>Ｖneg + わけに(は)いかない : used in a situation where one has to do something because s/he has an obligation to do it OR where one has no choice but to do something. It also expresses the unwillingness of the speaker to do Ｖ.

### Comment 10 — bamboo4

ざる is an archaic particle indicating negation. It's root form is ず and it inflects as ず/ざら、ず/ざり、ざる/ぬ、ざれ/ね but its imperative form is ざれ. In tbis manner, it has very unique inflections.<br/>行かざる means not going, which is denied by えない so that it becomes "not going is not capable," the double negative thus resulting in "I must go."

### Comment 11 — bamboo4

#4293 is not acceptable. 同情の涙を禁じ得ない would be the correct diction. ざるを得ない is not appropriate so that this example does not belong to this head.

### Comment 12 — 誠

#4293 grammatically is not wrong, but the meaning is very strange. Because 〜ざるを得ない means you have to do 〜even you don't want to, and 禁じる means 抑える or suppress,<br/>So, 同情の涙を禁じざるを得ない means:<br/>You have to suppress tears of sympathy, even if you didn't want to (suppress).<br/><br/>Whereas, 同情の涙を禁じ得ない means(which is more natural in this case):<br/>You couldn't suppress/stop tears of sympathy (of dropping down).同情の涙を抑えることができない
