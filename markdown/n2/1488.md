## Meaning

Despite that

We had heavy rains this morning due to the typhoon. Despite that, it suddenly started getting sunny this afternoon and now it's perfectly lovely outside.

ja: それなのに

Meaning credits: Pan

## Examples

### Example 5041

朝は台風の影響で大雨でした。**それが、**昼前から急に晴れてきて、今はすっかり快晴だ。

あさはたいふうのえいきょうでおおあめでした。それが、ひるまえからきゅうにはれてきて、いまはすっかりかいせいだ。

en: We had heavy rains this morning due to the typhoon. **Despite that,** it suddenly started getting sunny this afternoon and now it's perfectly lovely outside.
