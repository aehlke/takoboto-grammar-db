# Attribution

This scraper and preservation project is authored by
**[Alex Ehlke](https://github.com/aehlke)**. Takoboto's owner granted Alex
permission to run the scraper against the site's grammar section.
Other operators must contact Takoboto before running the scraper against it;
see [PERMISSION.md](PERMISSION.md). This project is independently maintained.

Grammar content originates from **JGram — The Japanese Grammar database**
([jgram.org](http://www.jgram.org/)) and its contributors, and is provided by
**Takoboto** ([grammar collection](https://takoboto.jp/bunpo/)).

Takoboto's [published notice](https://takoboto.jp/) states that the grammar
pages' content is from jgram.org and is
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/).
The [2007 archived JGram page](https://web.archive.org/web/20070411155229/http://www.jgram.org:80/pages/viewOne.php?tagE=ga-2)
also links that license. Supplemental records credit **JGram and its
contributors**, with preserved original URLs and replay links supplied by
**Internet Archive's Wayback Machine**. Archive timestamps identify captures,
not original contribution dates.

Displayed contributor credits are preserved with entries, content blocks,
examples, and comments in the data and reader exports. Those labels identify
the credits shown by the source; they do not establish separate creator/editor
roles or verified account identities. Please retain these credits when
redistributing or adapting the material.

Changes made by this project: extracted grammar content from public HTML,
separated content from navigation and contribution controls, normalized it
into JSON/SQLite, and generated Markdown. Japanese example spacing, readings,
translations, grammar highlighting, credit strings, discussion order, and
links are retained where exposed. Display HTML is sanitized. The source text
has not been editorially corrected. The current collection was retrieved on
October 2–3, 2026 America/Toronto / October 3 UTC. Historical records were
retrieved October 3, 2026 UTC; capture dates vary by entry. Original explanatory
notes, grammar lessons, annotated relationships, and example verification
classes are retained separately. Red-font source emphasis becomes HTML strong
emphasis in display fragments. Historical and current records are not merged
into a synthetic version of an entry.
Original grammar RSS excerpts and their displayed contributor credits are
also retained. RSS publication dates remain feed observations, without
assigning them to entry or contribution creation/update fields.

Pages carrying missing or conflicting license notices are kept as local
response evidence for review and excluded from distributable extracted records.
For example, inspected Tae Kim lesson pages also declare CC BY-NC-SA 2.0.
Their content has not been relabeled or included in this CC BY-SA dataset.

Linked external sites, dictionary data outside grammar entries, app assets,
and JGram illustrations are not part of this extracted dataset.
